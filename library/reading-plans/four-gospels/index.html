The four Gospels, one chapter a day

All four Gospels in about three months, in the order of the Bible: Matthew (days 1 to 28), Mark (29 to 44), Luke (45 to 68) and John (69 to 89). The same life is told four times, each time with new light. Tick each day as you go. Your phone remembers your place.

Each day, ask

  1. Who is Jesus in this chapter?
  2. Who responds to him, and how?
  3. What is he asking of me today?
