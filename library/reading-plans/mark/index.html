The Gospel of Mark, one chapter a day

The shortest Gospel, read in a few minutes a day. Tick each day as you go. Your phone remembers your place.

Each day, ask

  1. Who is Jesus in this chapter?
  2. Who responds to him, and how?
  3. What is he asking of me today?
