Origins

Armenia is honoured as the first Christian nation, through St Gregory the Illuminator around 301.

History

Bonds with Rome grew in the Kingdom of Cilicia. Abraham Ardzivian became the first Catholic patriarch in 1742. The Mekhitarist monks, founded in 1701, kept Armenian learning alive at Venice. In the genocide of 1915, Bishop Ignatius Maloyan died a martyr, and he was beatified in 2001.

Worship

The Armenian liturgy, in Classical Armenian. St Gregory of Narek was named a Doctor of the Church in 2015.

Today

Spread across Lebanon, Syria, Armenia, Europe and the Americas.

History

Armenia became a Christian nation around 301. St Mesrop Mashtots created its alphabet about 405, so the Bible and liturgy could be in Armenian. The Kingdom of Cilicia drew close to Rome in the 12th to 14th centuries. A Catholic patriarchate began in 1742. The genocide of 1915 killed and scattered much of the people.
