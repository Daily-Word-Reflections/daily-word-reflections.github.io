SOS: Priests in Distress

For priests passing through difficulty, loneliness, illness or crisis, and for anyone who wants to help a priest in need. This service is run by Catholic priests.

You are not alone

Every priest carries the weight of others. Sometimes the weight becomes too much: tiredness, loneliness, doubt, conflict, illness, a fall, a crisis in the vocation. Here you can speak freely, without judgement.

If life is in danger

If you, or the priest you are writing about, might come to harm, or there is immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline in your country straight away, and then write to us.

Who can write

A priest himself, or a family member, friend, parishioner or religious who is worried about a priest.

In confidence

What you write is read with discretion and kept confidential. You do not need to give your name, your diocese or your congregation unless you wish. You will be listened to and accompanied in prayer, and helped to find the right person or help near you.

Signs that a priest may be struggling

Constant tiredness, sleeplessness or loss of joy in ministry; withdrawal from brother priests, family and friends; irritability or sudden anger; neglect of prayer and of health; heavy drinking or other compulsive habits; talk of hopelessness or of leaving everything. These signs do not mean that a priest is a bad priest. They often mean that he is carrying too much alone.

How you can help a priest

Speak to him privately and kindly; ask how he really is, and listen without judging. Pray for him every day. Encourage him to talk with a trusted priest, a spiritual director, his bishop or superior, a doctor or a counsellor. Do not spread what you have noticed. If you are worried and do not know what to do, write to us: we can listen and help you think about the next step.

A prayer for priests

Lord Jesus, eternal High Priest, keep your priests close to your heart. Strengthen those who are tired, comfort those who are lonely, heal those who are wounded, and bring back those who have wandered. Give them faithful friends and the joy of serving you. Mary, Mother of priests, pray for them. Amen.

Questions

Who is this service for?

For Catholic priests anywhere in the world who are going through any kind of difficulty, and for anyone who is worried about a priest: family members, friends, parishioners or religious.

Is it really confidential?

Yes. What you write is read with care and kept in confidence. You do not have to give your name, diocese or congregation.

Who will answer?

The service is run by Catholic priests, who listen, pray with you and, if you wish, help you find suitable help near you.

Is this a substitute for medical or emergency help?

No. If anyone is in danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline first. We can accompany you alongside professional help.
