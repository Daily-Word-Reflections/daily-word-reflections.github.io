Privacy Policy

Daily Word · Last updated 3 November 2026

Daily Word (daily-word-reflections.github.io) offers Catholic daily readings, reflections and homilies. You can read it without an account.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read, from which kinds of device and which countries. Your settings, such as language, rite and text size, are stored only on your own device. If you write to us by email or WhatsApp, we receive what you send and your contact details, and we use them only to reply.

Social media

Our app "Daily Word Poster" publishes our own posts to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It does not collect information about people who see or follow those posts, and it does not use anyone else's account.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Analytics data is processed by Google under its own policies.

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete a message or any information we hold about you, write to contactdailywordreflections@gmail.com.
