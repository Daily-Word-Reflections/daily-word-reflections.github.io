Friday after the First Sunday after Denho · Commemoration of Mary, Mother of God, for the Seeds

15 January 2027 · Syro-Malankara Rite

Readings

Word for the day

“He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed for sowing and increase the harvest of your righteousness.” 2 Corinthians 9:10

Reflection

The Syriac Churches keep in January a commemoration of the Mother of God for the seeds: at the season of sowing, farmers brought their seed to church to be blessed, entrusting the year's harvest to the prayers of Mary. Paul's words fit perfectly: “the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully.” And: “He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed.” Generosity is sowing; God gives the growth.

In the Gospel Mary receives the shepherds' story and “treasured all these words and pondered them in her heart.” Mary is the good soil where the Word is sown and bears fruit. Today we pray for farmers and for all who work the land, for a good harvest and fair prices, and for the Eparchy of Muvattupuzha on its anniversary. Let our hearts, like Mary's, receive the seed of God's word, and let our hands sow generously.

Full homily

Dear friends, today our tradition remembers the Mother of God for the seeds. At sowing time farmers brought their seed to be blessed and entrusted the harvest to Mary's prayers.

Paul says: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully. God loves a cheerful giver (2 Corinthians 9:6–7). Generosity is like sowing.

And God is the true giver: he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed (2 Corinthians 9:10). All growth is grace.

In the Gospel Mary treasured all these words and pondered them in her heart (Luke 2:19). She is the good soil where the Word was sown. Let us pray for our farmers and for Muvattupuzha eparchy, and let our hearts receive the Word as Mary did. Amen.

For children

Children, a tiny seed can become a big plant! Mary kept God's words in her heart like seeds. Let us keep Jesus' words in our hearts too.

Prayer

God who gives seed to the sower and bread for food, through the prayers of the Mother of God bless the seed of our farmers and the work of our hands. Make our hearts good soil for your word, as Mary's was, and make us cheerful givers. Amen.
