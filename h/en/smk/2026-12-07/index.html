Word for the day

“I have said this to you, so that in me you may have peace. In the world you face persecution. But take courage; I have conquered the world!” John 16:33

Reflection

From prison Paul tells Timothy not to be ashamed of the testimony about the Lord, and gives his own reason: “I know the one in whom I have put my trust.” Christ has abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel. Timothy's task is to “guard the good treasure entrusted to you, with the help of the Holy Spirit living in us.” St Ambrose did just that in Milan: he guarded the faith against error and handed it on, among others to a restless young teacher named Augustine.

On the night before he died Jesus speaks plainly to his disciples: the Father himself loves you. They will soon be scattered and leave him alone, “yet I am not alone because the Father is with me.” Then a word for every anxious heart: “In the world you face persecution. But take courage; I have conquered the world!” The peace of Christ is not the absence of trouble; it is his victory present in the middle of it.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul writes to Timothy from prison: do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord, or of me his prisoner, but join with me in suffering for the gospel, relying on the power of God, who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace (2 Timothy 1:8–9).

Paul is chained, yet not ashamed: I know the one in whom I have put my trust, and I am sure that he is able to guard until that day what I have entrusted to him (2 Timothy 1:12). Faith is trusting a Person, not just believing ideas.

In the Gospel, at the Last Supper, Jesus says: the hour is coming, indeed it has come, when you will be scattered, each one to his home, and you will leave me alone. Yet I am not alone because the Father is with me. In the world you face persecution. But take courage; I have conquered the world! (John 16:32–33).

Jesus does not promise his disciples an easy life. He promises his presence and his victory. Many Christians today face hardship for their faith, in parts of India and the world. Many face quieter pressures: mockery at college, unfair treatment at work, loneliness abroad.

Take courage. These are among the last words Jesus spoke before his Passion. The Child we await at Yaldo is the One who has conquered the world.

During this fast, let us not be ashamed of the Gospel. Let us make the sign of the cross before meals in public, speak of our faith when asked, and pray for persecuted Christians. And let us say with Paul: I know the one in whom I have put my trust. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: take courage, I am stronger than everything bad. Do not be afraid to say you love Jesus!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you have conquered the world. Give us your peace in every trouble, courage to witness without shame, and, through the prayers of St Ambrose, the grace to guard and hand on the faith. Amen.
