Friday after Denho · Mar Stephanos, the First Martyr

8 January 2027 · Syro-Malankara Rite

  • Epistle 1 Corinthians 16:15–18 · Devoted to the service of the saints

Word for the day

“While they were stoning Stephen, he prayed, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.” Then he knelt down and cried out in a loud voice, “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” When he had said this, he died.” Acts 7:59–60

Reflection

In the days of Denho the Syriac Churches honour Mar Stephanos, the first martyr. After his great speech before the council, “filled with the Holy Spirit, he gazed into heaven and saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at the right hand of God.” What the Baptist saw at the Jordan, the open heavens, Stephen sees at his death. They drag him out and stone him; he prays like his Master: “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit… Lord, do not hold this sin against them.” A young man named Saul stands by, guarding their coats.

Jesus had foretold it: “I send you prophets, sages, and scribes, some of whom you will kill and crucify.” Martyrdom is not failure; it is the Gospel lived to the end. Paul, once Saul, later praises the household of Stephanas, whose name means crown, for devoting themselves “to the service of the saints”. Today is the name day of Bishop Philipos Mar Stephanos; we pray for him and for all who serve. Stephen began as a deacon serving tables and ended with the crown of martyrdom: service and witness are one road.

Full homily

Dear friends, in the days after Denho the Syriac Churches honour Mar Stephanos, the first martyr. At the Jordan the Baptist saw the heavens open. At his death Stephen sees the same: I see the heavens opened and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God (Acts 7:56).

Jesus is standing, as if rising to welcome his witness. The council covers their ears and rushes at him. They stone him. A young man named Saul guards their coats.

And Stephen prays as Jesus prayed on the Cross: Lord Jesus, receive my spirit. Then he kneels and cries out: Lord, do not hold this sin against them (Acts 7:59–60). He dies forgiving. Later, that young Saul becomes Paul the Apostle. The martyr's prayer bore fruit.

Jesus had foretold it: I send you prophets, sages, and scribes, some of whom you will kill (Matthew 23:34). Stephen began as a deacon serving tables and ended with the crown of martyrdom. Service and witness are one road.

Today is the name day of Bishop Philipos Mar Stephanos; we pray for him. Let us ask for Stephen's faith to see heaven, his love to forgive, and his humility to serve. Amen.

For children

Children, Stephen loved Jesus very much. Even when people hurt him, he prayed for them. Let us pray for anyone who has hurt us.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you stood at the right hand of God to receive your martyr Stephen. Give us his faith to see heaven open, his love to forgive our enemies, and his humility to serve. Bless Bishop Philipos Mar Stephanos on his name day. Amen.
