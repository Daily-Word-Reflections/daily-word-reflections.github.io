Word for the day

“Fight the good fight of the faith; take hold of the eternal life, to which you were called and for which you made the good confession in the presence of many witnesses.” 1 Timothy 6:12

Reflection

Today our Church remembers Mar Timotheos, the young disciple whom Paul called “my loyal child in the faith”. Paul's charge to him is a programme for every Christian: “pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, gentleness. Fight the good fight of the faith; take hold of the eternal life, to which you were called.” Timothy was timid, often unwell and young for his office, yet he became a shepherd of the church in Ephesus. God's strength is shown in such weakness.

In the Gospel Jesus says: “Do not think that I have come to abolish the law or the prophets; I have come not to abolish but to fulfill.” The Christian faith does not throw away the Old Testament; it reads it in the light of Christ, as our liturgy does every day. Timothy learned the Scriptures from childhood at the knees of his mother and grandmother, and Paul taught him to see Christ in them. Our families too can be the first school of the word.

Homily outline

Theme: “Fight the good fight of the faith.”

Mar Timotheos: the good fight, Christ's fulfilment, and faith handed on at home.

1. The good fight

  • Six virtues to pursue.
  • Take hold of eternal life.
  • Strength in weakness.
“Be strong and bold; have no fear or dread of them, because it is the LORD your God who goes with you; he will not fail you or forsake you.”” Deuteronomy 31:6
“Fight the good fight of the faith; take hold of the eternal life, to which you were called and for which you made the good confession in the presence of many witnesses.” 1 Timothy 6:12

Apply: Practise one of Paul's six virtues each day.

2. Not to abolish but to fulfil

  • The law and the prophets.
  • Christ the fulfilment.
  • Reading the Old Testament in his light.
“But this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, says the LORD: I will put my law within them, and I will write it on their hearts; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.” Jeremiah 31:33
““Do not think that I have come to abolish the law or the prophets; I have come not to abolish but to fulfill.” Matthew 5:17

Apply: Read one Old Testament reading this week and ask how it points to Christ.

3. Faith at home

  • Lois and Eunice.
  • Scripture from childhood.
  • Families as first schools.
“Keep these words that I am commanding you today in your heart. Recite them to your children and talk about them when you are at home and when you are away, when you lie down and when you rise.” Deuteronomy 6:6–7
“I am reminded of your sincere faith, a faith that lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and now, I am sure, lives in you.” 2 Timothy 1:5

Apply: Pray with your children or grandchildren tonight.

Timothy fought the good fight in weakness.

Christ fulfils the Scriptures; families hand them on.

Take-home resolution: Begin a daily family Bible reading.

Full homily

Dear friends, today we remember Mar Timotheos, the young disciple of St Paul. Paul charges him: pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance, gentleness. Fight the good fight of the faith; take hold of the eternal life, to which you were called (1 Timothy 6:11–12).

Timothy was young, timid and often sick, yet he became a shepherd of the church in Ephesus. God's strength works through our weakness.

In the Gospel Jesus says: I have come not to abolish but to fulfill (Matthew 5:17). The Old Testament is not thrown away; Christ fulfils it. Timothy learned the Scriptures from his mother and grandmother.

Let our homes too be schools of the word. Let us fight the good fight and hand on the faith. Amen.

For children

Children, Timothy learned about God from his mother and grandmother. Who teaches you about God? Thank them today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you came not to abolish but to fulfil. Through the prayers of Mar Timotheos make us fight the good fight of the faith, take hold of eternal life, and hand on the faith in our homes as Lois and Eunice did. Amen.
