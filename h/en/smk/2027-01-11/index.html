Word for the day

“Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and the God of all consolation, who consoles us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to console those who are in any affliction with the consolation with which we ourselves are consoled by God.” 2 Corinthians 1:3–4

Reflection

Paul opens his second letter to the Corinthians not with complaints about his sufferings, though they were many, but with a blessing: God is “the Father of mercies and the God of all consolation”. In Asia Paul had felt “the sentence of death”, and he learned to “rely not on ourselves but on God who raises the dead.” The comfort he received becomes comfort he can give. Our wounds, once consoled by God, become a ministry to others.

In the Gospel John the Baptist says of Jesus: “He whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for he gives the Spirit without measure.” Today we remember Mar Gregorios of Nyssa, younger brother of Mar Baselios, a bishop and mystic who taught that the soul never stops growing toward God: each discovery opens a greater longing. The Spirit given without measure keeps drawing us further into the endless love of God.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul had suffered greatly in Asia. He even felt he had received the sentence of death. Yet he begins his letter with a blessing: blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and the God of all consolation (2 Corinthians 1:3).

God consoles us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to console others with the consolation we ourselves have received. Our healed wounds become a ministry.

Paul says his trials taught him to rely not on ourselves but on God who raises the dead (2 Corinthians 1:9). Suffering strips away false supports and leaves us leaning on God alone.

Today we remember Mar Gregorios of Nyssa, brother of Mar Baselios, who taught that the soul is always growing toward God. Christ gives the Spirit without measure (John 3:34). Let us receive God's comfort, pass it on, and keep growing in his love. Amen.

For children

Children, when you are sad, God comforts you. Then you can comfort a friend who is sad. Who can you cheer up today?

Prayer

Father of mercies and God of all consolation, you comfort us in every affliction. Teach us to rely on you who raise the dead, fill us with the Spirit without measure, and through the prayers of Mar Gregorios let us grow without end in your love. Amen.
