Saturday after New Year · Mar Baselios and Mar Gregorios

2 January 2027 · Syro-Malankara Rite

Readings

Word for the day

“Jesus answered, “Very truly, I tell you, no one can enter the kingdom of God without being born of water and Spirit.” John 3:5

Reflection

Today the Church remembers Mar Baselios (St Basil the Great) and Mar Gregorios (St Gregory Nazianzen), friends and bishops of Cappadocia who defended faith in the Holy Trinity, and whose names are borne by our Catholicos and many of our bishops. It is the name day of Moran Mor Baselios Cardinal Cleemis Catholicos; we pray for him and for the Eparchy of Mavelikara, erected on this day in 2007.

In the Gospel Nicodemus comes to Jesus by night. Jesus tells him: “no one can enter the kingdom of God without being born of water and Spirit.” A few days before Denho, the feast of Jesus' baptism, we are reminded of our own: a new birth from above. Jesus also says that the Son of Man must be lifted up, as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness. Paul tells Timothy that the Lord's servant “must not be quarrelsome but kindly to everyone, an apt teacher, patient.” Basil and Gregory were exactly this: firm in the truth, gentle with people.

Full homily

Dear friends, today we honour Mar Baselios and Mar Gregorios, two great bishops of Cappadocia in the fourth century. They were close friends who defended faith in the Holy Trinity. Our Catholicos bears the name Baselios, and today is his name day. We pray for him and for the Eparchy of Mavelikara.

In the Gospel Nicodemus, a respected teacher, comes to Jesus by night. Jesus tells him: no one can enter the kingdom of God without being born of water and Spirit (John 3:5). Nicodemus is puzzled. How can an old man be born again? Jesus speaks of a birth from above, a gift of God.

In a few days we celebrate Denho, the Baptism of the Lord. Jesus' baptism opens the way for ours. In Baptism we were born of water and Spirit and became children of God.

Jesus also says: just as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up (John 3:14). The new life flows from the Cross.

Paul gives Timothy a portrait of a good shepherd: not quarrelsome but kindly to everyone, an apt teacher, patient, correcting opponents with gentleness (2 Timothy 2:24–25). That was Basil and Gregory. May it be our Catholicos, our bishops, our priests, and every parent and teacher. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said we must be born again from water and the Holy Spirit. That happened when you were baptised! Today let us pray for our Catholicos, Baselios Bava.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you call us to be born from above of water and the Spirit. Through the prayers of Mar Baselios and Mar Gregorios, keep our Catholicos and bishops faithful and gentle shepherds, and renew in us the grace of our Baptism. Amen.
