Word for the day

“Keep awake therefore, for you do not know on what day your Lord is coming.” Matthew 24:42

Reflection

On the second day, Jonah finally obeys. He walks a day's journey into the great city and cries his short message. “And the people of Nineveh believed God; they proclaimed a fast, and everyone, great and small, put on sackcloth.” The king's decree is remarkable: “All shall turn from their evil ways and from the violence that is in their hands. Who knows? God may relent.” Fasting without turning from violence and evil would mean nothing. Nineveh fasted with its hands as well as its stomach.

Jesus warns that his coming will be like the days of Noah: people “were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage” and did not notice until the flood came. “Keep awake therefore.” Paul tells us how the watchful live: “Let love be genuine; hate what is evil, hold fast to what is good… Rejoice in hope, be patient in suffering, persevere in prayer.” Today we remember Mar Macarios the Great, the monk of the Egyptian desert, whose watchful prayer and gentleness made him a father to many.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the second day of the fast Jonah obeys. He enters Nineveh and cries out. And the people of Nineveh believed God; they proclaimed a fast, and everyone, great and small, put on sackcloth (Jonah 3:5).

The king commands: all shall turn from their evil ways and from the violence that is in their hands (Jonah 3:8). Nineveh fasted with its hands as well as its stomach. Our fast too must change how we treat others.

Jesus warns that his coming will be like the days of Noah, when people did not notice until the flood came. Keep awake therefore, for you do not know on what day your Lord is coming (Matthew 24:42).

Paul tells us how the watchful live: let love be genuine; rejoice in hope, be patient in suffering, persevere in prayer (Romans 12:9, 12). With Mar Macarios, let us keep the faith and keep awake. Amen.

For children

Children, the people of Nineveh stopped doing bad things and said sorry to God. Let us be kind at home today, and say sorry if we hurt someone.

Prayer

Lord God, the people of Nineveh believed you and turned from violence and evil. Keep us awake for the coming of your Son, make our love genuine, and through the prayers of Mar Macarios keep us steadfast in the true faith. Amen.
