Word for the day

“Jesus said to him, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.” John 14:6

Reflection

Paul calls himself “the very least of all the saints”, yet to him was given the grace “to bring to the Gentiles the news of the boundless riches of Christ”, the mystery hidden for ages in God. Through the Church the manifold wisdom of God is now made known. The ancient cross venerated at St Thomas Mount in Mylapore is a witness that these riches reached our land early: Christians in India carved the sign of the cross long before the Portuguese arrived.

On the eve of his passion Jesus tells his troubled disciples: “Do not let your hearts be troubled. Believe in God, believe also in me. In my Father's house there are many dwelling places.” When Thomas, the apostle of India, asks, “How can we know the way?”, Jesus answers with one of the greatest sayings of the Gospel: “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.” The cross is the way he walked to prepare a place for us.

Full homily

Dear friends, today we remember the Miraculous Cross of Mylapore, the ancient stone cross connected with the memory of the apostle Thomas. It reminds us how old and deep the faith is in our land, and that it is a faith in the Cross.

St Paul calls himself the least of all the saints, yet he received the grace to proclaim the boundless riches of Christ (Ephesians 3:8). Through the Church the wisdom of God is made known.

In the Gospel Jesus tells His disciples: do not let your hearts be troubled; in my Father's house there are many dwelling places. Thomas asks: how can we know the way? Jesus answers: I am the way, and the truth, and the life (John 14:1–6).

Let us venerate the Cross today and thank God for the faith Thomas brought. Let us walk on the Way, which is Christ, especially in these last days before Yaldo. Amen.

For children

Children, the cross is the sign of Jesus' love. Make the sign of the cross slowly and lovingly today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, way, truth and life, calm our troubled hearts. Through the prayers of St Thomas the Apostle, who asked you the way, lead us by your cross to the Father's house. Amen.
