Word for the day

“Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant in search of fine pearls; on finding one pearl of great value, he went and sold all that he had and bought it.” Matthew 13:45–46

Reflection

Paul speaks of a wisdom “not of this age”, hidden and now revealed: if the rulers of this age had understood it, “they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.” What no eye has seen God has prepared for those who love him, and “these things God has revealed to us through the Spirit; for the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.” The gospel is not a clever theory but a gift received in the Spirit.

Jesus speaks of a man who finds treasure in a field and “in his joy” sells everything to buy it, and of a merchant who sells all for one pearl. The kingdom is worth everything. St John Chrysostom, the great preacher of Antioch and Constantinople, gave his golden words and finally his freedom for that pearl; he died in exile, and his Liturgy is still prayed across the East.

Homily outline

Theme: “One pearl of great value.”

The kingdom worth everything.

1. Seek it like treasure

  • Wisdom is to be sought like silver.
  • The finder sells all in joy.
“if you seek it like silver, and search for it as for hidden treasures” Proverbs 2:4
“The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field, which someone found and hid; then in his joy he goes and sells all that he has and buys that field.” Matthew 13:44

Apply: Give up one thing today that keeps you from God.

2. Revealed by the Spirit

  • No eye has seen any God besides the Lord.
  • The Spirit searches the depths of God.
“From ages past no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides you, who works for those who wait for him.” Isaiah 64:4
“these things God has revealed to us through the Spirit; for the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.” 1 Corinthians 2:10

Apply: Pray to the Holy Spirit before reading Scripture.

3. Treasures new and old

  • God's word is a lamp.
  • The good scribe brings out what is new and what is old.
“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.” Psalm 119:105
“Therefore every scribe who has been trained for the kingdom of heaven is like the master of a household who brings out of his treasure what is new and what is old.” Matthew 13:52

Apply: Read a page of a Church Father.

Christ is the pearl.

Joy makes every sacrifice small.

Take-home resolution: Read one homily of St John Chrysostom this month.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul speaks of a wisdom not of this age, hidden and now revealed: if the rulers of this age had understood it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. What no eye has seen nor ear heard, God has prepared for those who love him, and he has revealed it to us through the Spirit (1 Corinthians 2:8–10).

In the Gospel Jesus compares the Kingdom to treasure hidden in a field and to a pearl of great value; whoever finds it sells all he has with joy to buy it. Every scribe trained for the Kingdom is like the master of a household who brings out of his treasure what is new and what is old (Matthew 13:44–52).

Today the Malankara Church remembers St John Chrysostom, the golden-mouthed, a great Father whom all the Eastern Churches still treasure. He brought out of Scripture what is new and what is old. Let us seek this treasure every day too: ten minutes with the Bible today. Amen.

For children

Children, a man found treasure in a field and gave everything with joy to have it. Jesus is our greatest treasure!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you are the treasure hidden in the field and the pearl of great value. Give us joy to choose you above all, and through the prayers of St John Chrysostom, wisdom that comes from your Spirit. Amen.
