Word for the day

“The greatest among you will be your servant.” Matthew 23:11

Reflection

Paul turns to the Christian home. Children are to obey their parents “in the Lord” and honour father and mother, the first commandment with a promise. And fathers are told something revolutionary for their time: “do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.” Authority in a Christian family is a service of love, not a tool of control.

Jesus criticises leaders who “do not practice what they teach”, who tie heavy burdens on others and love the best seats and titles. Among his disciples it must be different: one teacher, one Father, one instructor, the Messiah. “The greatest among you will be your servant.” Whether at home or in the Church, the one who serves most is greatest in God's sight.

Homily outline

Theme: “The greatest among you will be your servant.”

The Christian home and Christian leadership.

1. Honour your parents

  • The commandment carries a promise.
  • Children obey their parents in the Lord.
“Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.” Exodus 20:12
“Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.” Ephesians 6:1

Apply: Call or visit your parents today.

2. Teach them diligently

  • Moses told parents to speak of God's words at home.
  • Paul tells fathers to raise children in the Lord.
“Keep these words that I am commanding you today in your heart. Recite them to your children and talk about them when you are at home and when you are away, when you lie down and when you rise.” Deuteronomy 6:6–7
“And, fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.” Ephesians 6:4

Apply: Pray with your children tonight.

3. Walk humbly

  • God asks justice, kindness and humility.
  • The humble will be exalted.
“He has told you, O mortal, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?” Micah 6:8
“The greatest among you will be your servant. All who exalt themselves will be humbled, and all who humble themselves will be exalted.” Matthew 23:11–12

Apply: Do one household task without being asked.

Authority is service.

The home is the first school of the Gospel.

Take-home resolution: Pray together as a family every evening this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul turns to the Christian home: children, obey your parents in the Lord; honour your father and mother, the first commandment with a promise. And he tells fathers something revolutionary for the time: do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord (Ephesians 6:1–4).

In the Gospel Jesus warns against leaders who say but do not do. The greatest among you will be your servant; all who exalt themselves will be humbled, and all who humble themselves will be exalted (Matthew 23:11–12).

Faith is first learnt at home, more by example than by words. Parents, let your children see you pray and forgive. Children, thank your father and mother today. A family that walks humbly is a small picture of God's Kingdom. Amen.

For children

Children, honouring your father and mother is God's commandment. Today give them a hug and say thank you!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you came to serve. Bless our homes: make children obedient and loving, parents patient and gentle, and all of us servants of one another in your name. Amen.
