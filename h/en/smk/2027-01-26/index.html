Word for the day

“They answered, “The emperor's.” Then he said to them, “Give therefore to the emperor the things that are the emperor's, and to God the things that are God's.”” Matthew 22:21

Reflection

Some in Corinth wanted letters of recommendation from Paul. He answers: “You yourselves are our letter, written on our hearts, to be known and read by all; and you show that you are a letter of Christ… written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts.” The best proof of the Gospel is a changed community. And Paul is humble: “Not that we are competent of ourselves… our competence is from God.”

On Republic Day the Gospel could not be more fitting. The Pharisees and Herodians try to trap Jesus: is it lawful to pay taxes to the emperor? He asks for a coin: “Whose head is this, and whose title?” “The emperor's.” “Give therefore to the emperor the things that are the emperor's, and to God the things that are God's.” The coin bears Caesar's image; we bear God's image. Christians are loyal citizens: we pay taxes, obey just laws, vote, serve the common good and pray for those in authority. But the State is not God. Our deepest belonging is to God, and that gives us the courage to stand for justice, for the poor, and for the freedom of conscience that India's Constitution guarantees to all.

Homily outline

Theme: “Give… to God the things that are God's.”

A letter of Christ, competence from God, and faithful citizenship.

1. A letter of Christ

  • No need of recommendations.
  • Written by the Spirit.
  • On tablets of the heart.
“I will give them one heart, and put a new spirit within them; I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,” Ezekiel 11:19
“and you show that you are a letter of Christ, prepared by us, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on tablets of stone but on tablets of human hearts.” 2 Corinthians 3:3

Apply: Let your conduct today be a letter of Christ.

2. Competence from God

  • Not of ourselves.
  • God's Spirit.
  • Humble service.
“He said to me, “This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, says the LORD of hosts.” Zechariah 4:6
“Not that we are competent of ourselves to claim anything as coming from us; our competence is from God,” 2 Corinthians 3:5

Apply: Begin your work with a prayer for God's help.

3. Caesar and God

  • The trap of the coin.
  • Caesar's image.
  • God's image in us.
“So God created humankind in his image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.” Genesis 1:27
“They answered, “The emperor's.” Then he said to them, “Give therefore to the emperor the things that are the emperor's, and to God the things that are God's.”” Matthew 22:21

Apply: Pray for India and its leaders today.

We are God's letter and God's image.

Faithful citizens, wholly belonging to God.

Take-home resolution: Serve the common good in one concrete way this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul tells the Corinthians: you show that you are a letter of Christ, written not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, on tablets of human hearts (2 Corinthians 3:3). The best proof of the Gospel is a changed community.

And he adds: our competence is from God (2 Corinthians 3:5).

In the Gospel, on this Republic Day, Jesus says: give to the emperor the things that are the emperor's, and to God the things that are God's (Matthew 22:21). The coin bears Caesar's image; we bear God's.

So we are loyal citizens: we pay taxes, obey just laws, serve the common good and pray for our leaders. But the State is not God. Belonging to God gives us courage to stand for justice and for the freedom of conscience our Constitution guarantees to all. Amen.

For children

Children, the coin had the king's picture, but you have God's picture in you! Let us pray for India today and give our hearts to God.

Prayer

Lord God, you made us in your image and wrote your Spirit on our hearts. On this Republic Day bless India and its people of every faith; grant wisdom and justice to those who govern; and make us loyal citizens who give to God the things that are God's. Amen.
