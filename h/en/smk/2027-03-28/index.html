Word for the day

“He is not here; for he has been raised, as he said.” Matthew 28:6

Reflection

Christ is risen! Very early on the first day of the week, the women go to the tomb, and an angel says: “Do not be afraid; I know that you are looking for Jesus who was crucified. He is not here; for he has been raised, as he said.” Then Jesus himself meets them: “Greetings!” They take hold of his feet and worship him. The One who was dead is alive for ever.

In John's Gospel Mary Magdalene runs to Peter and the beloved disciple; they run to the tomb and find the linen cloths lying there. The other disciple “saw and believed”. The empty tomb is not proof by itself, but it is the first sign; faith is born when we see with the heart that God has kept his word.

The Old Testament readings have prepared us. Daniel is brought up alive from the lions' den, because God shut the lions' mouths (Daniel 6:22). Moses sets up the tabernacle, and God's glory fills it. Isaiah announces: “Peace, peace, to the far and the near.” In the risen Christ the glory of God dwells among us for ever, and his first word to the disciples will be: “Peace be with you.”

St Peter preaches on the day of Pentecost: “This Jesus God raised up, and of that all of us are witnesses.” St Paul hands on the heart of the Gospel: “Christ died for our sins in accordance with the scriptures… he was buried… he was raised on the third day.” Easter is not a beautiful story; it is the event on which everything stands. Because Christ is risen, sin is forgiven, death is defeated, and our own bodies will rise. Let us live as Easter people.

Homily outline

Theme: “He is not here; he has been raised.”

Christ is risen! This is the feast of feasts. Everything in our faith depends on it: if Christ has not been raised, our faith is futile; but Christ has been raised. Today we celebrate the victory over death, the witness of the empty tomb, and our mission to proclaim it.

1. Death is swallowed up

  • Isaiah promised: God will swallow up death forever and wipe away every tear.
  • Peter proclaims: it was impossible for death to hold him.
  • The Resurrection is not a return to ordinary life but the beginning of new creation.
“He will swallow up death forever. Then the Lord GOD will wipe away the tears from all faces.” Isaiah 25:8
“But God raised him up, having freed him from death, because it was impossible for him to be held in its power.” Acts 2:24

Apply: Bring your grief to the Risen Lord and let him wipe away your tears.

2. The empty tomb: he saw and believed

  • Daniel came out of the lions' den unharmed because he trusted in God.
  • Mary Magdalene finds the stone rolled away; the beloved disciple enters, sees the linen and believes.
  • Faith in the Resurrection rests on the witness of those who saw the empty tomb and the risen Lord.
“Daniel was taken up out of the den, and no kind of harm was found on him, because he had trusted in his God.” Daniel 6:23
“Then the other disciple, who reached the tomb first, also went in, and he saw and believed.” John 20:8

Apply: Profess the Creed today with special faith: “On the third day he rose again.”

3. Go and tell

  • The angel says: “Do not be afraid”; Jesus says the same.
  • The women are sent to the apostles: the first announcers of Easter.
  • “Arise, shine”: every Christian is sent to bring Easter joy to the world.
“Arise, shine; for your light has come, and the glory of the LORD has risen upon you.” Isaiah 60:1
“Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid; go and tell my brothers to go to Galilee; there they will see me.”” Matthew 28:10

Apply: Greet your family and neighbours today with “Christ is risen!” and share the joy with someone who is sad or alone.

The tomb is empty, death is defeated, and the Lord is alive.

Let us live as Easter people: without fear, full of hope, witnesses of the Risen One.

Take-home resolution: During the fifty days of Easter, greet others with “Christ is risen” and pray the Glory Be with special joy.

Full homily

Christ is risen! He is risen indeed! Dear brothers and sisters, very early this morning the women went to the tomb with spices, expecting to anoint a dead body. Instead they found an angel who said: “Do not be afraid. He is not here; for he has been raised, as he said.”

As he said. On the road to Jerusalem Jesus had told his disciples three times that he would suffer, die and rise on the third day. They did not understand. Today God has kept his word. And Jesus himself meets the women: “Greetings!” They hold his feet and worship him. Peter and John run to the tomb; the beloved disciple “saw and believed”.

St Paul gives us the heart of the Gospel: “Christ died for our sins, he was buried, and he was raised on the third day.” Because Christ is risen, our sins can be forgiven; because Christ is risen, death is not the end; because Christ is risen, the graves of our loved ones are not the last word. Daniel came out of the lions' den at dawn; Christ came out of the tomb, and he will bring us out too.

St Peter said: “God raised this Jesus, and we are all witnesses.” Now we are the witnesses. Let us live as Easter people: joyful, forgiving, hopeful. Bring this joy to someone who is sad this week. Christ is risen! He is truly risen! Amen.

For children

Children, Christ is risen! Say it with me: He is risen indeed!

On Sunday morning the women went to Jesus' tomb and found it empty. An angel said: “He is not here, he is risen!” Then Jesus himself came to meet them. They were so happy!

Jesus is alive for ever, and he is with us. Today tell everyone at home: “Jesus is alive!” Let's shout: “Alleluia!”

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, you have trampled down death by death and given life to those in the tombs. Fill us with Easter joy and peace, make us witnesses of your Resurrection, and raise us with you on the last day. Christ is risen! He is truly risen! Amen.
