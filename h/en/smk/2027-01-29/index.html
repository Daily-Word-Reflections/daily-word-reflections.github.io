Word for the day

“Very truly, I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains just a single grain; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.” John 12:24

Reflection

Some Greeks come to Philip with a simple request: “Sir, we wish to see Jesus.” Jesus answers by speaking of his death: “unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains just a single grain; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.” To see Jesus truly is to see the cross. And the disciple follows the same path: “Whoever serves me must follow me, and where I am, there will my servant be also.” Paul applies this to daily life: “present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God… Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your minds.”

Our Church today honours Mar Ignatius of Antioch, called Noorono, the fiery one, the third bishop of Antioch, after St Peter and Evodius. Condemned to the beasts in Rome around the year 107, he wrote seven letters on the journey, urging unity with the bishop and love of the Eucharist, and saying that he wished to be God's wheat, ground by the teeth of beasts, so as to become the pure bread of Christ. He lived the Gospel of the grain of wheat. We also pray for Joshua Mar Ignathios on his name day, and for all who serve the Body of Christ with their gifts, as Paul says: “we, who are many, are one body in Christ.”

Homily outline

Theme: “If it dies, it bears much fruit.”

The grain of wheat, the living sacrifice, and Mar Ignatius, God's wheat.

1. A living sacrifice

  • By the mercies of God.
  • Bodies offered.
  • Minds renewed.
“The sacrifice acceptable to God is a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise.” Psalm 51:17
“I appeal to you therefore, brothers and sisters, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship.” Romans 12:1

Apply: Offer your day's work to God this morning.

2. One body, many gifts

  • Not thinking too highly.
  • Members of one another.
  • Gifts that differ.
“But Moses said to him, “Are you jealous for my sake? Would that all the LORD's people were prophets, and that the LORD would put his spirit on them!”” Numbers 11:29
“so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually we are members one of another.” Romans 12:5

Apply: Use one of your gifts for the parish this week.

3. The grain of wheat

  • We wish to see Jesus.
  • Falling and dying.
  • Much fruit.
“May those who sow in tears reap with shouts of joy. Those who go out weeping, bearing the seed for sowing, shall come home with shouts of joy, carrying their sheaves.” Psalm 126:5–6
“Very truly, I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains just a single grain; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.” John 12:24

Apply: Let go of one selfish attachment.

To see Jesus is to see the grain that dies and bears fruit.

Like Mar Ignatius, let us become God's wheat.

Take-home resolution: Make one hidden sacrifice each day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, some Greeks say to Philip: sir, we wish to see Jesus (John 12:21). Jesus answers by speaking of his death: unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains just a single grain; but if it dies, it bears much fruit (John 12:24).

To see Jesus is to see the cross, and to follow him is to share it. Paul says: present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God (Romans 12:1).

Today we honour Mar Ignatius Noorono, bishop of Antioch, who on the way to martyrdom in Rome wrote that he wished to be God's wheat, ground by the teeth of beasts, to become the pure bread of Christ.

We pray too for Joshua Mar Ignathios on his name day. Let us offer ourselves as a living sacrifice, and use our gifts as members of one body in Christ. Amen.

For children

Children, a seed goes into the ground and grows into a big plant! Jesus gave his life for us, and we can give little gifts of love to him every day.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, grain of wheat who fell into the earth and rose to bear much fruit, make us a living sacrifice. Through the prayers of Mar Ignatius Noorono, kindle in us his fiery love for you and for your Church; bless Joshua Mar Ignathios on his name day. Amen.
