Word for the day

“Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her,” Ephesians 5:25

Reflection

Paul begins with a principle for every Christian household: “Be subject to one another out of reverence for Christ.” Mutual self-giving, not domination, is the law of Christian marriage. He then speaks to wives and husbands in the language of his time, but turns everything toward Christ: “Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her.” The measure of a husband's love is the cross. Quoting Genesis, “the two will become one flesh”, Paul adds: “This is a great mystery, and I am applying it to Christ and the church.”

In the Gospel Jesus restores marriage to God's original plan. Moses had allowed a certificate of divorce, but Jesus teaches that the bond is not to be broken. The Church calls marriage a sacrament, a living icon of Christ's faithful love for his Bride; in our tradition the Crowning shows husband and wife as king and queen of a small kingdom, the domestic church. Today let us pray for married couples, for families under strain, and for those whose marriages have been wounded, that they may find in the Church the mercy and truth of Christ.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul begins: be subject to one another out of reverence for Christ (Ephesians 5:21). Christian marriage is mutual self-giving, not domination.

Then he says: husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her (Ephesians 5:25). The measure of love is the cross.

Quoting Genesis, the two will become one flesh, he adds: this is a great mystery, and I am applying it to Christ and the church (Ephesians 5:32). In the Gospel Jesus teaches that this bond is not to be broken.

In our tradition the Crowning shows husband and wife as king and queen of a domestic church. Let us pray for all couples and for wounded families, that they may find Christ's mercy and truth. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus loves his Church like a good husband loves his wife. Let us pray for our mummy and daddy, and help them at home today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you love the Church as a faithful Bridegroom and gave yourself up for her. Bless all married couples with mutual reverence, faithful love and the grace to forgive. Heal wounded families, and make every home a domestic church. Amen.
