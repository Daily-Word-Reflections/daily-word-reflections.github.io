Word for the day

“He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answered, “The Messiah of God.”” Luke 9:20

Reflection

The covenant with Abram is sealed in the dark: “When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passed between these pieces. On that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram.” Only God passes between the halves of the sacrifice; Abram does nothing but watch. In the ancient custom, those who passed between the pieces bound themselves to keep the covenant. Here God binds himself alone. The promise rests on his faithfulness, not ours. Paul, who knew that faithfulness, still says: “Not that I have already obtained this or have already reached the goal; but I press on to make it my own, because Christ Jesus has made me his own… forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal for the prize of the heavenly call of God in Christ Jesus.” The Fast is that pressing on.

In the Gospel Jesus is praying alone and asks his disciples: “Who do the crowds say that I am?” Then the question that matters: “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answers: “The Messiah of God.” Today our Church gives thanks for its own place in that confession. On 10 February 2005 Pope John Paul II raised the Syro-Malankara Catholic Church to the rank of a Major Archiepiscopal Church, with Moran Mor Cyril Baselios as its first Major Archbishop; it is now led by Moran Mor Baselios Cardinal Cleemis, Major Archbishop-Catholicos. A Church that came home to communion with the See of Peter in 1930 was recognised as a Church with its own head and synod within that communion. The torch of God's faithfulness has passed through our history; we press on, confessing with Peter that Jesus is the Messiah of God.

Full homily

Dear friends, the covenant with Abram was sealed at night: a smoking fire pot and a flaming torch passed between the pieces (Genesis 15:17). God alone passed through. His promise rests on his faithfulness, not ours.

Paul says: not that I have already reached the goal; but forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on (Philippians 3:12–14). The Fast is our pressing on.

Jesus asks his disciples: but who do you say that I am? Peter answers: the Messiah of God (Luke 9:20). Every Christian must give that answer personally.

Today our Church thanks God for 10 February 2005, when it was raised to a Major Archiepiscopal Church in communion with the See of Peter. Let us pray for our Major Archbishop-Catholicos and Synod, and press on together, confessing Christ. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus asked: who do you say I am? Peter said: you are the Christ! Today our Church says thank you to God for making us a big family together with the Pope. Can you say to Jesus today: you are my Lord?

Prayer

Lord God, faithful to your covenant, you passed through the darkness to bind yourself to Abram and his children. We thank you for our Syro-Malankara Catholic Church and for the gift of its Major Archiepiscopal dignity in communion with the Successor of Peter. Bless our Major Archbishop-Catholicos and our Holy Synod. Help us to press on toward the goal, confessing that Jesus is the Messiah of God. Amen.
