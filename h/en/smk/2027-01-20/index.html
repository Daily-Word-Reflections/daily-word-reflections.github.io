Word for the day

“saying, “I called to the LORD out of my distress, and he answered me; out of the belly of Sheol I cried, and you heard my voice.” Jonah 2:2

Reflection

On the third day we hear Jonah praying from the depths. Swallowed by the great fish, at the bottom of the sea, with weeds wrapped around his head, he does not despair: “I called to the LORD out of my distress, and he answered me.” “As my life was ebbing away, I remembered the LORD; and my prayer came to you.” And on the third day the fish spews him onto dry land. The Fathers saw here Christ's descent into death and his rising, and also our Baptism: going down into the water and coming up to new life.

In the Gospel people tell Jesus of a disaster in Jerusalem, and he answers: do not think the victims were worse sinners than you; “unless you repent, you will all perish as they did.” Repentance is not for others; it is for me, now. Paul says: “you are all children of light and children of the day”; let us “keep awake and be sober.” Today the Church honours St Sebastian, the soldier who served Christ more than Caesar and twice faced death for him. Jonah prayed in the depths; Sebastian stood firm under the arrows. Both teach us that no depth is beyond God's hearing.

Homily outline

Theme: “I called to the LORD out of my distress, and he answered me.”

The prayer from the depths, the call to repent, and the courage of St Sebastian.

1. Prayer from the depths

  • In the belly of the fish.
  • Weeds around the head.
  • God hears.
“Out of the depths I cry to you, O LORD. Lord, hear my voice! Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications!” Psalm 130:1–2
“Therefore we have been buried with him by baptism into death, so that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, so we too might walk in newness of life.” Romans 6:4

Apply: Bring your deepest trouble to God in prayer tonight.

2. Unless you repent

  • Disasters and blame.
  • Not worse sinners.
  • Repentance for me, now.
“Cast away from you all the transgressions that you have committed against me, and get yourselves a new heart and a new spirit! Why will you die, O house of Israel?” Ezekiel 18:31
“No, I tell you; but unless you repent, you will all perish as they did.” Luke 13:3

Apply: Make your confession before the fast ends.

3. Children of light (St Sebastian)

  • Awake and sober.
  • Armour of faith and love.
  • The soldier of Christ.
“The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?” Psalm 27:1
“Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell.” Matthew 10:28

Apply: Stand firm in your faith at work or school.

No depth is beyond God's hearing.

Repent now, and live as children of the light.

Take-home resolution: Make a good confession before the fast ends.

Full homily

Dear friends, today we hear Jonah praying from the belly of the fish. At the bottom of the sea, with weeds around his head, he prays: I called to the LORD out of my distress, and he answered me; out of the belly of Sheol I cried, and you heard my voice (Jonah 2:2).

There is no depth where God cannot hear us. On the third day the fish brought Jonah to dry land. The Fathers saw here the death and Resurrection of Christ, and our own Baptism.

In the Gospel Jesus says: unless you repent, you will all perish (Luke 13:3). We often think repentance is for others. Jesus says it is for me, now. Paul adds: you are all children of light and children of the day (1 Thessalonians 5:5).

Today we honour St Sebastian, who served Christ more than Caesar and did not fear death. On this last day of the fast, let us pray from our depths, repent honestly and stand firm. Amen.

For children

Children, Jonah prayed to God from inside a big fish, and God heard him! God hears us wherever we are.

Prayer

Lord our God, you heard Jonah from the belly of the fish and brought him up on the third day. Hear us in our distress, give us true repentance, and through the prayers of St Sebastian make us brave children of the light. Amen.
