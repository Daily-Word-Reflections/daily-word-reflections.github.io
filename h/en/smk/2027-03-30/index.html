Word for the day

“For our paschal lamb, Christ, has been sacrificed.” 1 Corinthians 5:7

Reflection

Three days ago the Church kept Pesaha; today St Paul tells us what Pesaha now means. “For our paschal lamb, Christ, has been sacrificed. Therefore, let us celebrate the festival, not with the old yeast, the yeast of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.” Paul is writing to Corinth about a scandal the community had tolerated, and he uses the image every Jewish family knew: before Passover the house is searched and every bit of leaven is cleared out, because “a little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough.” The Passover lamb was sacrificed so that Israel might pass from slavery to freedom; Christ, the true Lamb, was sacrificed on the Cross so that we might pass from death to life. St John Chrysostom, commenting on these verses, said that for the Christian the whole of life is a festival, because the Lamb has been offered for us (in substance). We keep that festival above all at the altar, where the Lamb who was sacrificed and is now risen gives himself to us, truly present, Body and Blood, soul and divinity. Many St Thomas Christian homes ate unleavened Pesaha bread last Thursday; Paul asks us to become unleavened bread ourselves, people of sincerity and truth.

The Gospel continues the Easter light. “Is a lamp brought in to be put under the bushel basket, or under the bed, and not on the lampstand? For there is nothing hidden, except to be disclosed; nor is anything secret, except to come to light.” On Good Friday the light of the world was hidden in the tomb; on Qyomtho it was set on the lampstand for all to see. No stone could keep it under the bed. Now the risen Lord puts his light in us, and he does not want it hidden by fear or by shame. Then Jesus adds a warning that is also a promise: “Pay attention to what you hear; the measure you give will be the measure you get, and still more will be given you.” Easter faith grows when it is given away. The one who shares the joy of the Resurrection receives more of it; the one who keeps it under the bed may find even what he has slipping away.

Hevoro week is a good time to clear out old yeast. Paul names it: malice and evil. In a family it may be an old resentment, a habit of sarcasm, a lie that has never been put right. Easter is the moment to throw it out, as a household clears the leaven before the feast. Practically: go to the Holy Qurbono today if you can, and receive the Lamb with a clean heart; this evening, let each person at home say one thing he or she is sorry for and one thing he or she is thankful for; and then set your Easter light on the lampstand by one visible act of goodness tomorrow, at work, in school or in the neighbourhood. Keep greeting one another with the Easter greeting all this week.

Theme: “For our paschal lamb, Christ, has been sacrificed.”

Full homily

Dear friends, Christ is risen! Today St Paul says: for our paschal lamb, Christ, has been sacrificed. Therefore, let us celebrate the festival (1 Corinthians 5:7–8). Easter is the true Pesaha. The Lamb was sacrificed on the Cross, and he is alive.

Before Passover, Jewish families clear every bit of leaven from the house. Paul says: clean out the old yeast, the yeast of malice and evil. A little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough (1 Corinthians 5:6). One unforgiven hurt can sour a whole family.

Jesus asks: is a lamp brought in to be put under the bushel basket, or under the bed? (Mark 4:21). The tomb could not hide the Light. Now he has lit his light in us. We are not to hide it out of fear or shame.

And the measure you give will be the measure you get (Mark 4:24). Easter joy grows when it is shared. Let us receive the Lamb at the Holy Qurbono with clean hearts, give up one resentment, and let our light shine. Amen.

For children

Children, Christ is risen! Jesus said: nobody lights a lamp and puts it under the bed. A lamp is for shining! Jesus has put his light in your heart. You can let it shine today by being kind to someone, by telling the truth, and by saying with a big smile: Christ is risen!

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, our paschal Lamb, you were sacrificed for us and you are risen. Clear out of our hearts the old yeast of malice and evil, and make us unleavened bread of sincerity and truth. You give yourself to us at your altar; make our whole life a festival of thanksgiving. Let the light you have kindled in us shine on the lampstand, so that others may see it and come to you. Amen.
