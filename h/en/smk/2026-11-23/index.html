Word for the day

“The good person out of the good treasure of the heart produces good, and the evil person out of evil treasure produces evil; for it is out of the abundance of the heart that the mouth speaks.” Luke 6:45

Reflection

The short letter to Philemon begins with warmth. Paul, a prisoner, thanks God for Philemon's love and faith, and tells him: “the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, my brother.” Some Christians are like a cool drink on a hot day; their love refreshes everyone around them. Their goodness flows from a heart rooted in Christ.

Jesus says the same in a picture: a good tree does not bear bad fruit; each tree is known by its own fruit. Figs do not grow on thorns. And the fruit of the human tree is above all our words: “it is out of the abundance of the heart that the mouth speaks.” To change our speech we must change our heart; to change our heart we must stay close to the one who is good. St Clement, who wrote to the quarrelling Corinthians, knew that peace in the Church begins in the heart.

Homily outline

Theme: “Out of the abundance of the heart.”

Good trees, good words, refreshing love.

1. Known by its fruit

  • The just are like trees by streams of water.
  • Each tree is known by its own fruit.
“They are like trees planted by streams of water, which yield their fruit in its season, and their leaves do not wither. In all that they do, they prosper.” Psalm 1:3
“for each tree is known by its own fruit.” Luke 6:44

Apply: Ask: what fruit did my day bear?

2. Guard your heart

  • The springs of life flow from the heart.
  • The mouth speaks what fills the heart.
“Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life.” Proverbs 4:23
“The good person out of the good treasure of the heart produces good, and the evil person out of evil treasure produces evil; for it is out of the abundance of the heart that the mouth speaks.” Luke 6:45

Apply: Speak no word of complaint today.

3. A friend who refreshes

  • A true friend loves at all times.
  • Philemon refreshed the hearts of the saints.
“A friend loves at all times, and kinsfolk are born to share adversity.” Proverbs 17:17
“I have indeed received much joy and encouragement from your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, my brother.” Philemon 7

Apply: Encourage a tired person today.

A good heart bears good fruit.

Let our love refresh others.

Take-home resolution: Say one word of encouragement every day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, the short letter to Philemon begins with warmth. Paul, a prisoner, thanks God for Philemon's love and faith: the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, my brother (Philemon 7). The very presence of some people is a comfort to others.

In the Gospel Jesus says: no good tree bears bad fruit; each tree is known by its own fruit. The good person out of the good treasure of the heart produces good, for it is out of the abundance of the heart that the mouth speaks (Luke 6:43–45).

Today the Church remembers Pope St Clement and St Felicity, trees that bore fruit. Our words reveal our heart. Today speak one word that refreshes someone's heart: a thank you, an encouragement, a blessing. Amen.

For children

Children, a good tree gives good fruit; a good heart speaks good words. Say three kind words to others today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, make my heart a good treasure, so that my words may bring life. Through the prayers of St Clement and St Felicity, let my love refresh the hearts of your saints. Amen.
