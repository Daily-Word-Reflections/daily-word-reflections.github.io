Word for the day

“The police answered, “Never has anyone spoken like this!”” John 7:46

Reflection

Paul tells Timothy to have nothing to do with silly myths and to “train yourself in godliness”. Physical training has some value, but godliness is valuable in every way, “holding promise for both the present life and the life to come.” That is what a fast is: spiritual training. Mar Saba, whom the East remembers today, trained himself for decades in the desert and founded a monastery that still stands near Jerusalem.

The temple police were sent to arrest Jesus and came back empty-handed. Asked why, they could only say: “Never has anyone spoken like this!” The crowds argue whether he is the prophet or the Messiah; the leaders sneer that no prophet comes from Galilee. Only Nicodemus asks for a fair hearing. In this fast, let us give Jesus that fair hearing, listening to his word until, like the police, we are disarmed by it.

Full homily

Dear friends, today the Church remembers Mar Saba, the great monk of the Judean desert, and St Clement of Alexandria, an early teacher of the faith. Both trained themselves in godliness.

St Paul tells Timothy: train yourself in godliness, for while physical training is of some value, godliness is valuable in every way, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come (1 Timothy 4:7–8). We train our bodies for health; how much more should we train our souls?

The fast is spiritual training. Like an athlete, we practise small disciplines: getting up for prayer, controlling our tongue, saying no to some food, saying yes to charity. These small exercises make the soul strong.

In the Gospel the people are divided about Jesus. Some say he is the prophet, others the Messiah. The temple police, sent to arrest him, return empty-handed and say: never has anyone spoken like this! (John 7:40–46). His words disarmed them.

Nicodemus, who had come to Jesus by night, now speaks up a little for him: our law does not judge people without first giving them a hearing (John 7:51). It is a small step of courage, but it is a beginning. Faith often grows by small steps.

This week, let us take one small step of courage for Christ, and one step of training in godliness. The Lord who spoke like no one else still speaks to us in the Gospel. Let us listen. Amen.

For children

Children, athletes train their bodies; Christians train their hearts with prayer and kindness. Train your heart today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, no one has ever spoken as you speak. Train us in godliness during this fast, set our hope on the living God, and through the prayers of Mar Saba, let your word disarm our hearts. Amen.
