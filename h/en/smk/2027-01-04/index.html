Word for the day

“Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.” Matthew 5:8

Reflection

Hebrews begins its great chapter on faith with three ancient figures. Abel offered a more acceptable sacrifice; Enoch “pleased God” and was taken up; Noah, warned of things not yet seen, built an ark and saved his household. The principle is simple: “without faith it is impossible to please God, for whoever would approach him must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.” Faith is not a feeling but a life that trusts God's word and acts on it.

The Gospel gives the Beatitudes, the portrait of a life of faith: poor in spirit, mourning, meek, hungry for righteousness, merciful, pure in heart, peacemakers, persecuted. Today we remember the prophet Malachi, last of the prophets, who promised that “the sun of righteousness shall rise, with healing in its wings”, and St Kuriakose Elias Chavara, a son of Kerala who lived the Beatitudes as priest, founder and teacher. Two days before Denho, they point us to the Sun that rises at the Jordan.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews 11 is the great hall of faith. Today we hear about the first three heroes. Abel offered God a more acceptable sacrifice. Enoch pleased God and was taken up. Noah, warned about things not yet seen, built an ark and saved his household (Hebrews 11:4–7).

The key sentence is this: without faith it is impossible to please God, for whoever would approach him must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him (Hebrews 11:6). Faith is trusting God's word enough to build an ark before the rain.

In the Gospel Jesus goes up the mountain and gives the Beatitudes. They are the portrait of a person of faith: poor in spirit, merciful, pure in heart, a peacemaker. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God (Matthew 5:8).

Today we remember the prophet Malachi, who promised that the sun of righteousness would rise with healing in its wings (Malachi 4:2), and St Kuriakose Elias Chavara, who lived the Beatitudes among the poor of Kerala, building schools and homes of prayer.

On Wednesday we celebrate Denho, when that Sun rises at the Jordan. Let us choose one Beatitude to live this week, and prepare our hearts to see God. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said: happy are the kind, the gentle and the peacemakers! Choose one this week. Will you be a peacemaker at home?

Prayer

Lord Jesus, Sun of righteousness, you taught us the Beatitudes and lived them perfectly. Through the prayers of the prophet Malachi and St Kuriakose Elias Chavara, give us a pure heart and a living faith, so that we may see God. Amen.
