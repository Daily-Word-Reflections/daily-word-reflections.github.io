Thursday after Denho · The Praise of St John the Baptist

7 January 2027 · Syro-Malankara Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Ephesians 3:1–7 · A servant of the gospel by the gift of God's grace
  • Gospel Luke 7:24–29 · Among those born of women no one is greater than John

Word for the day

“I tell you, among those born of women no one is greater than John; yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.” Luke 7:28

Reflection

On the day after Denho the Syriac tradition praises the one who baptised the Lord. Jesus himself gives John's praise: “What did you go out into the wilderness to look at? A reed shaken by the wind?” No, John was firm. “Someone dressed in soft robes?” No, he lived in poverty. “A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.” He is the messenger foretold to prepare the way. “Among those born of women no one is greater than John.”

And then a surprise: “yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.” John stood at the threshold; we, by Baptism, have entered. The grace of the Kingdom is greater than any human greatness. Paul calls himself “the very least of all the saints”, yet a servant of the Gospel “according to the gift of God's grace”. The mystery hidden for ages is now revealed: the Gentiles are fellow heirs in Christ. John prepared the way; we are sent to walk it.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the day after Denho the Syriac Churches praise John the Baptist, who baptised the Lord in the Jordan. Jesus himself speaks his praise.

He asks the crowds: what did you go out into the wilderness to look at? A reed shaken by the wind? (Luke 7:24). No. John was not shaken by public opinion or by Herod's power. Someone dressed in soft robes? No. John lived in poverty.

A prophet? Yes, says Jesus, and more than a prophet. He is the messenger sent ahead to prepare the way. Among those born of women no one is greater than John (Luke 7:28).

Then a surprise: yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he. John stood at the door; by Baptism we have entered. This is not about our merit but about grace. Paul calls himself a servant of the gospel according to the gift of God's grace (Ephesians 3:7).

Let us honour John by being firm in faith, simple in life, and grateful for the Kingdom we have received. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said John the Baptist was very great and strong, like a tree in a storm. Let us be strong for Jesus too!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you praised John the Baptist, your messenger and the friend who baptised you. Make us firm as he was, simple as he was, and grateful for the grace of the Kingdom we have received in Baptism. Amen.
