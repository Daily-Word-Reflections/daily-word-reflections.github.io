Readings

  • Epistle Romans 10:5–13 · The word is near you
  • Gospel Matthew 13:1–9 · The parable of the sower

Word for the day

“For, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.”” Romans 10:13

Reflection

Salvation is not far away, Paul says, as if we had to climb to heaven or descend to the abyss to fetch Christ. “The word is near you, on your lips and in your heart.” If you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. There is no distinction between Jew and Greek: the same Lord is generous to all who call on him.

Jesus tells of a sower who throws seed everywhere: on the path, on rocky ground, among thorns, and on good soil. The sower is not stingy; the seed is the same; the difference is the soil. Some hearts are hard as a path, some shallow, some choked with worries. But the good soil yields thirty, sixty, a hundredfold. Each day we choose what kind of soil we will be.

Homily outline

Theme: “The word is near you.”

Confess, call, and be good soil.

1. The word is near

  • Moses said the word is in your mouth and heart.
  • Paul applies it to faith in the risen Lord.
“No, the word is very near to you; it is in your mouth and in your heart for you to observe.” Deuteronomy 30:14
“because if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.” Romans 10:9

Apply: Say “Jesus is Lord” aloud in your morning prayer.

2. Call on the name

  • Joel promised salvation to all who call.
  • Paul opens it to Jew and Greek alike.
“Then everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved;” Joel 2:32
“For, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.”” Romans 10:13

Apply: Pray the Jesus Prayer during the day.

3. Good soil

  • Jeremiah: break up the fallow ground.
  • Jesus: the good soil bears much fruit.
“Break up your fallow ground, and do not sow among thorns.” Jeremiah 4:3
“Other seeds fell on good soil and brought forth grain, some a hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty. Let anyone with ears listen!” Matthew 13:8–9

Apply: Remove one distraction that chokes your prayer.

God sows generously.

Let our hearts be good soil.

Take-home resolution: Read the Gospel of the day every morning this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul says salvation is not far away, as if we had to climb to heaven or descend to the abyss to fetch Christ. The word is near you, on your lips and in your heart. If you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved (Romans 10:8–9). Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.

In the parable of the sower the same seed falls on the path, on rocky ground, among thorns and on good soil; what fell on good soil brought forth grain, a hundredfold, sixtyfold, thirtyfold. Let anyone with ears listen (Matthew 13:3–9).

The word is near; the question is how open our heart is. This week let us call on the name of Jesus in our heart throughout the day: Lord Jesus, have mercy on me. That short prayer turns the heart into good soil. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus saves everyone who calls on his name. Today say many times in your heart: Jesus, I love you.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, your word is near us. Make our hearts good soil, clear away the thorns of worry, and let us call on your name and be saved. Amen.
