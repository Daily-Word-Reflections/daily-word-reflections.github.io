Word for the day

“But to all who received him, who believed in his name, he gave power to become children of God,” John 1:12

Reflection

The prologue now speaks of rejection and welcome. “He was in the world, and the world came into being through him; yet the world did not know him. He came to what was his own, and his own people did not accept him.” There was no room at the inn. But to all who did receive him, “who believed in his name, he gave power to become children of God”, born not of blood or of human will, but of God. Christmas is God's offer of adoption.

Paul struggles in prayer for the Colossians and for many he has never met, so that their hearts may be encouraged and united in love, and that they may have “the knowledge of God's mystery, that is, Christ himself, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.” The child in the manger looks poor, but in him are all the treasures of God.

Full homily

Dear friends, the Prologue speaks of rejection and welcome: He was in the world, yet the world did not know Him; He came to what was His own, and His own people did not accept Him (John 1:10–11). There was no room at the inn.

But to all who received Him, who believed in His name, He gave power to become children of God (John 1:12). Christmas is about this decision: to receive Him or not.

St Paul writes that in Christ are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge (Colossians 2:3). We do not need to look elsewhere for meaning.

Two days before Yaldo, let us make room for Him: in our homes, by preparing the crib together; in our hearts, by confession and prayer; in our families, by forgiveness. Then we will become children of God. Amen.

For children

Children, there was no room for Jesus at the inn. Make room for Jesus in your heart by saying a prayer every night!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you came to your own and they did not receive you. We welcome you now: make room in our hearts and homes, make us children of God, and show us all the treasures hidden in you. Amen.
