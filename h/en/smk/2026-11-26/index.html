Thursday after the Sunday of the Annunciation

26 November 2026 · Syro-Malankara Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle 1 Corinthians 10:14–22 · The cup of blessing and the bread we break
  • Gospel Luke 4:31–37 · He commands with authority

Word for the day

“They were all amazed and kept saying to one another, “What kind of utterance is this? For with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and out they come!”” Luke 4:36

Reflection

Paul warns the Corinthians to flee from idolatry, and gives a reason that is pure Eucharistic theology: “The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ?” Those who share the Lord's table cannot also share the table of demons. The Holy Qurbono binds us to Christ and to one another; it excludes every other lord.

In the synagogue at Capernaum the people are astounded at Jesus' teaching, “because he spoke with authority.” A man with an unclean spirit cries out: “I know who you are, the Holy One of God.” Jesus rebukes the spirit and it leaves him. His word is not just information; it is power that frees. The same word is spoken over the bread and cup at our altar.

Homily outline

Theme: “He commands with authority.”

The word that frees and the table that unites.

1. He spoke and it came to be

  • God's word created the world.
  • Jesus' word astonishes the synagogue.
“For he spoke, and it came to be; he commanded, and it stood firm.” Psalm 33:9
“They were astounded at his teaching, because he spoke with authority.” Luke 4:32

Apply: Let one word of the Gospel command your day.

2. The Holy One

  • The seraphim cry “Holy, holy, holy”.
  • The demon confesses the Holy One of God.
“Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory.” Isaiah 6:3
“I know who you are, the Holy One of God.” Luke 4:34

Apply: Sing the Qadishat Aloho with attention.

3. One cup, one body

  • Moses sealed the covenant with blood.
  • Christ's cup makes us one body.
“Moses took the blood and dashed it on the people, and said, “See the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you in accordance with all these words.”” Exodus 24:8
“The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ?” 1 Corinthians 10:16

Apply: Prepare carefully for your next Holy Communion.

His word frees; his table unites.

Let us have no other lord.

Take-home resolution: Make a visit to the Blessed Sacrament this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul warns the Corinthians to flee from idolatry, and gives a reason that is pure Eucharistic theology: the cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ? Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body (1 Corinthians 10:16–17).

In the Gospel Jesus teaches with authority in the synagogue at Capernaum; a man with an unclean spirit cries out: I know who you are, the Holy One of God. Jesus drives it out with a word, and all are amazed: what kind of utterance is this (Luke 4:33–36)!

It is the Holy One of God whom we receive at the Holy Qurbono. Those who share the one cup cannot serve other masters: money, power, superstition. This Sunday let us come to church a moment early and prepare our hearts in silence. Amen.

For children

Children, at the Holy Qurbono Jesus gives himself to us. On Sunday sit quietly in church and talk to Jesus!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, Holy One of God, your word frees us from every evil spirit. Make us one through the bread we break and the cup we bless, and let no other lord rule our hearts. Amen.
