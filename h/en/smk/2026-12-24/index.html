Word for the day

“And the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory, the glory as of a father's only son, full of grace and truth.” John 1:14

Reflection

On the eve of Yaldo the prologue reaches its summit: “And the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory.” The Greek says he “pitched his tent” among us, like the tabernacle in the desert filled with God's glory. From his fullness we have all received “grace upon grace”. The law came through Moses; grace and truth through Jesus Christ. “No one has ever seen God. It is God the only Son, who is close to the Father's heart, who has made him known.”

Paul's words to the Romans are sobering: God's eternal power and divine nature have been visible in creation from the beginning, yet human beings exchanged the glory of the immortal God for images of mortal things. Christmas is God's answer to our idolatry: no longer an image made by human hands, but the true Image of God, born of a woman, lying in a manger. Tonight the whole Church kneels before him.

Homily outline

Theme: “The Word became flesh.”

The vigil of Yaldo.

1. Glory in the tent

  • God's glory filled the tabernacle.
  • The Word pitched his tent among us.
“Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.” Exodus 40:34
“And the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory, the glory as of a father's only son, full of grace and truth.” John 1:14

Apply: Come to the Yaldo Qurbono with a prepared heart.

2. Grace upon grace

  • The Lord is merciful and gracious.
  • From his fullness we receive grace upon grace.
“The LORD, the LORD, a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness,” Exodus 34:6
“From his fullness we have all received, grace upon grace. The law indeed was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.” John 1:16–17

Apply: Thank God for three graces received this year.

3. The heavens declare his glory

  • Creation proclaims God's handiwork.
  • God's power is seen in what he made.
“The heavens are telling the glory of God; and the firmament proclaims his handiwork.” Psalm 19:1
“Ever since the creation of the world his eternal power and divine nature, invisible though they are, have been understood and seen through the things he has made.” Romans 1:20

Apply: Keep Christ, not things, at the centre of Christmas.

God has pitched his tent among us.

Let us kneel before him tonight.

Take-home resolution: Spend time before the crib each day of the Christmas season.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the eve of Yaldo the Prologue reaches its summit: and the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen His glory, the glory as of a father's only son, full of grace and truth (John 1:14). The Greek says He pitched His tent among us.

St Paul says that God's eternal power and divine nature have been seen through the things He has made (Romans 1:20). But tonight God shows Himself in a new way: not only through creation, but in a human face.

From His fullness we have all received, grace upon grace. No one has ever seen God; it is the only Son, close to the Father's heart, who has made Him known (John 1:16–18).

Tonight, at the Yaldo service, let us welcome the Word made flesh with joy and adoration. Let us light our lamps, sing our hymns, and above all open our hearts. A blessed Yaldo to you all. Amen.

For children

Children, tonight Jesus, the Son of God, is born as a little baby! Welcome Him with joy and tell Him you love Him.

Prayer

Word made flesh, you pitched your tent among us and showed us the Father's glory. As we keep the vigil of your birth, fill us with grace upon grace, and make our hearts a fitting dwelling for you. Amen.
