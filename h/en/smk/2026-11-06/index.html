Friday after Qudos Eetho Sunday

Readings

  • Epistle 1 Timothy 6:17–19 · Rich in good works
  • Gospel Luke 7:36–50 · The woman who loved much

Word for the day

“Therefore, I tell you, her sins, which were many, have been forgiven; hence she has shown great love.” Luke 7:47

Reflection

Paul tells Timothy what to say to the rich: not to be haughty, not to hope in uncertain riches but in God, who richly provides everything for our enjoyment; to be rich in good works, generous and ready to share. Money is a good servant and a cruel master. Shared, it becomes “a good foundation for the future”.

At Simon's table a woman known as a sinner bathes Jesus' feet with her tears, dries them with her hair and anoints them. Simon sees only her past; Jesus sees her love. The one who is forgiven much loves much. On Friday, the day of the Cross, we stand with her at his feet: “Your faith has saved you; go in peace.”

Homily outline

Theme: “She has shown great love.”

True riches and the tears of the forgiven.

1. Do not set your heart on riches

  • The psalmist warns against trusting wealth.
  • Paul tells the rich to hope in God.
“if riches increase, do not set your heart on them.” Psalm 62:10
“As for those who in the present age are rich, command them not to be haughty, or to set their hopes on the uncertainty of riches, but rather on God who richly provides us with everything for our enjoyment.” 1 Timothy 6:17

Apply: Give something today to a person in need.

2. A broken heart at his feet

  • God does not despise a contrite heart.
  • The woman weeps at Jesus' feet.
“The sacrifice acceptable to God is a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise.” Psalm 51:17
“She stood behind him at his feet, weeping, and began to bathe his feet with her tears and to dry them with her hair.” Luke 7:38

Apply: Spend five minutes before a crucifix.

3. Forgiven much, loving much

  • Scarlet sins become white as snow.
  • Faith saves and sends us in peace.
“Come now, let us argue it out, says the LORD: though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be like snow;” Isaiah 1:18
“And he said to the woman, “Your faith has saved you; go in peace.”” Luke 7:50

Apply: Make a good confession soon.

God sees love where others see sin.

Let mercy make us generous.

Take-home resolution: Make one act of generosity every Friday.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul tells Timothy what to say to the rich: not to be haughty, not to set their hopes on the uncertainty of riches, but on God who richly provides us with everything for our enjoyment; to do good, to be rich in good works, generous and ready to share (1 Timothy 6:17–18).

In the Gospel, at a Pharisee's house, a sinful woman washes Jesus' feet with her tears, dries them with her hair and anoints them with ointment. Jesus says: her sins, which were many, have been forgiven; hence she has shown great love (Luke 7:47). Her true treasure was her broken heart.

Those who are forgiven much love much. Today is the First Friday; this week experience the joy of forgiveness in a good confession, and let that love flow out in a generous gift. Amen.

For children

Children, the woman cried and loved Jesus, and Jesus forgave her. Tonight tell Jesus: sorry, and I love you.

Prayer

Merciful Lord, you received the tears of the sinful woman and forgave her much. Free me from trust in riches, make me rich in good works, and let my love grow from your forgiveness. Amen.
