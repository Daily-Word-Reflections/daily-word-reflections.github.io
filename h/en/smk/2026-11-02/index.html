Word for the day

“Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.” Matthew 5:8

Reflection

On the day after the Church was sanctified in the liturgy, St Paul tells the Thessalonians plainly what God wants: their holiness. “For God did not call us to impurity but in holiness.” Holiness is not a feeling; it is a way of living in the body, in marriage, in business, in every relationship, so that no brother is wronged.

On the mountain Jesus paints the portrait of the holy person: poor in spirit, mourning, meek, hungry for justice, merciful, pure in heart, a peacemaker, persecuted for his sake. It is first of all his own portrait. To live the Beatitudes is to let his face appear in ours, and the promise is the greatest of all: the pure in heart will see God.

Homily outline

Theme: “Blessed are the pure in heart.”

The Beatitudes as God's call to holiness.

1. Called to be holy

  • Holiness is not for a few; it is God's will for all.
  • It shapes how we live in the body and treat others.
“You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy.” Leviticus 19:2
“For God did not call us to impurity but in holiness.” 1 Thessalonians 4:7

Apply: Avoid one occasion of sin you know well.

2. A pure heart sees God

  • The psalmist asks who may stand in God's holy place.
  • Jesus promises the vision of God to the pure.
“Who shall ascend the hill of the LORD? And who shall stand in his holy place? Those who have clean hands and pure hearts,” Psalm 24:3–4
“Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.” Matthew 5:8

Apply: Make a short examination of conscience tonight.

3. The kingdom of the humble

  • The meek inherit the land.
  • The poor in spirit already have the kingdom.
“But the meek shall inherit the land, and delight themselves in abundant prosperity.” Psalm 37:11
“Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.” Matthew 5:3

Apply: Give way gently in one argument today.

The Beatitudes are the face of Jesus.

Let his face appear in ours.

Take-home resolution: Live one Beatitude each day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, the day after the Church was dedicated to God on Qudos Eetho Sunday, Paul tells us plainly what God wants: this is the will of God, your sanctification; God did not call us to impurity but in holiness (1 Thessalonians 4:3, 7). Holiness is not for a few; it is the calling of all the baptised.

In the Beatitudes Jesus paints the portrait of holiness: the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, those who hunger for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God (Matthew 5:8).

The Beatitudes are the face of the saints. This week choose one of them and try to live it: perhaps meekness, perhaps making peace. We are the living stones of the Church that was dedicated yesterday. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said: blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God. Today, only good thoughts and good words!

Prayer

Holy Father, you have called us not to impurity but to holiness. Give us a pure heart, that we may see you, and make us poor in spirit, meek and merciful like your Son. Amen.
