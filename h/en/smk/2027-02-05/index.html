Friday after Aneede Sunday

Readings

  • Epistle 1 Corinthians 10:1–13 · Written down to instruct us; God is faithful

Word for the day

“If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me before it hated you.” John 15:18

Reflection

Paul looks back at Israel in the wilderness. They had every gift: “our ancestors were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea… and all ate the same spiritual food, and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.” Yet “God was not pleased with most of them.” They desired evil, worshipped idols, tested the Lord and grumbled. “These things happened to them to serve as an example, and they were written down to instruct us.” Baptism and the Eucharist are not a charm that saves us without our cooperation. “So if you think you are standing, watch out that you do not fall.” And at once Paul adds the comfort: “God is faithful, and he will not let you be tested beyond your strength, but with the testing he will also provide the way out so that you may be able to endure it.”

In the Gospel Jesus prepares his friends for another kind of testing, the hostility of the world: “If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me before it hated you… Servants are not greater than their master. If they persecuted me, they will persecute you.” The disciples are hated not for their faults but because they belong to him: “I have chosen you out of the world.” On this Friday, the day of the Cross, we remember Christians in India and across the world who suffer for the name of Jesus. As the Great Fast approaches, Paul's warning and Jesus' promise belong together: watch, because you can fall; take heart, because God is faithful and Christ has gone ahead of you. St John Chrysostom says that Paul names the way out together with the trial, so that no one may despair (in substance).

Homily outline

Theme: “If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me before it hated you.”

Paul's warning from the wilderness and Jesus' word to the persecuted.

1. Written down to instruct us

  • All received the gifts.
  • Most of them fell.
  • Grace calls for watchfulness.
“Do not harden your hearts, as at Meribah, as on the day at Massah in the wilderness,” Psalm 95:8
“So if you think you are standing, watch out that you do not fall.” 1 Corinthians 10:12

Apply: Name one temptation you will guard against during the Fast.

2. God is faithful

  • Testing is common to all.
  • Never beyond our strength.
  • Always a way out.
“When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and through the rivers, they shall not overwhelm you; when you walk through fire you shall not be burned, and the flame shall not consume you.” Isaiah 43:2
“God is faithful, and he will not let you be tested beyond your strength,” 1 Corinthians 10:13

Apply: In your next temptation, stop and ask God to show you the way out.

3. Hated without a cause

  • The world hated him first.
  • Chosen out of the world.
  • Servants share the master's road.
“More in number than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause;” Psalm 69:4
“If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me before it hated you.” John 15:18

Apply: Pray for persecuted Christians each Friday of the Great Fast.

Grace received must be guarded with watchfulness.

God is faithful to those who suffer for his name.

Take-home resolution: Choose one Friday act of prayer for the persecuted Church and keep it through the Fast.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul reminds us that Israel passed through the sea, ate the manna and drank from the rock, and the rock was Christ. Yet most of them fell in the wilderness (1 Corinthians 10:4–5).

These things were written down to instruct us. So if you think you are standing, watch out that you do not fall (1 Corinthians 10:11–12). Grace is not a charm; it calls for watchfulness.

But Paul adds at once: God is faithful, and he will not let you be tested beyond your strength, but with the testing he will also provide the way out (1 Corinthians 10:13).

Jesus tells us not to be surprised if the world hates us: it hated him first (John 15:18). On this Friday, let us pray for persecuted Christians, and enter the coming Fast watchful and full of trust. Amen.

For children

Children, sometimes it is hard to do what is right. But God is faithful! He never gives us a test that is too big, and he always shows us a way out. Ask him for help when you are tempted.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you were hated without a cause and you went before us on the way of the Cross. Keep us watchful so that we do not fall, and faithful when we are tested. Strengthen all who suffer for your name today, and give them the way out that you have promised. Amen.
