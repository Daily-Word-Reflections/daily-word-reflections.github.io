The Entry of Mary, Mother of God, into the Temple

21 November 2026 · Syro-Malankara Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Philippians 4:4–9 · Rejoice in the Lord always
  • Gospel Matthew 12:46–50 · Whoever does the will of my Father

Word for the day

“For whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother.” Matthew 12:50

Reflection

The Church celebrates today the tradition that Mary, as a little child, was brought by her parents Joachim and Anne to the Temple and given to God. The details come from Tradition, not from the Gospels, but the meaning is biblical: like Samuel, whom Hannah “lent to the LORD”, Mary belonged wholly to God from the beginning. The little girl in the Temple would one day become the Temple, carrying God's Son in her womb.

In the Gospel Jesus' mother and brothers stand outside wanting to speak with him. Pointing to his disciples he says: “Whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother.” This is not a rejection of Mary but her highest praise: no one did the Father's will more completely than she who said, “let it be with me according to your word.” Paul's words fit her perfectly: rejoice, do not worry, pray with thanksgiving, think on whatever is true, honourable, just, pure and lovely.

Full homily

Dear friends, today the Church celebrates the tradition that Mary, Mother of God, as a little child was brought by her parents Joachim and Anne to the Temple and given to God. The details come from Tradition, not from the Gospels, but their meaning is clear: from the very beginning Mary's whole life was given to God, like Samuel whom Hannah lent to the Lord.

In the Gospel, when Jesus' mother and brothers stand outside, he points to his disciples and says: here are my mother and my brothers! Whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother (Matthew 12:49–50). Mary is Jesus' mother first not in the body but in a heart that received God's will.

Paul says: rejoice in the Lord always; whatever is true, honourable, just and pure, think about these things (Philippians 4:4, 8). This was Mary's heart. Today let us give our day to God with Mary; pray three Hail Marys and place this day in her hands. Amen.

For children

Children, little Mary was given to God. Today you too can say: God, this day is yours!

Prayer

Holy Mary, Mother of God, given to the Lord from childhood, teach us to belong wholly to God, to do the Father's will, and to rejoice always in your Son. Amen.
