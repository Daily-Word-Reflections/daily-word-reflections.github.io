  • Epistle Hebrews 13:7–16 · Remember your leaders; Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever

Word for the day

“Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.” Hebrews 13:8

Reflection

Hebrews asks us to “Remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith.” Today we remember two such leaders: St Peter, whose chains in prison (Acts 12) the Church honours, and Bishop Geevarghese Mar Divannasios, a shepherd of our own Church who died in 2018. Leaders pass; the one they served remains: “Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.”

Jesus asks: “Who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom his master has put in charge of his household, to give the other slaves their allowance of food at the proper time? Blessed is that slave whom his master will find at work when he arrives.” Every Christian is a steward of something: family, work, talents, a parish ministry. Hebrews adds the worship that pleases God: a “sacrifice of praise… the fruit of lips that confess his name”, and doing good and sharing what we have.

Homily outline

Theme: “Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.”

Remembering our leaders, trusting the unchanging Lord, serving faithfully.

1. Remember your leaders

  • Those who spoke God's word.
  • Consider their life.
  • Imitate their faith.
“Remember the days of old, consider the years long past; ask your father, and he will inform you; your elders, and they will tell you.” Deuteronomy 32:7
“Remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith.” Hebrews 13:7

Apply: Pray for a departed priest or teacher who formed you.

2. The faithful steward

  • Put in charge of the household.
  • Food at the proper time.
  • Found at work.
“So Joseph found favor in his sight and attended him; he made him overseer of his house and put him in charge of all that he had.” Genesis 39:4
““Who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom his master has put in charge of his household, to give the other slaves their allowance of food at the proper time? Blessed is that slave whom his master will find at work when he arrives.” Matthew 24:45–46

Apply: Do your daily duties well, as for the Lord.

3. The sacrifice of praise

  • We have an altar.
  • Lips that confess his name.
  • Doing good and sharing.
“Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and pay your vows to the Most High.” Psalm 50:14
“Through him, then, let us continually offer a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips that confess his name. Do not neglect to do good and to share what you have, for such sacrifices are pleasing to God.” Hebrews 13:15–16

Apply: Share something with someone in need this week.

Leaders pass; Christ remains.

Let him find us faithful at our work.

Take-home resolution: Pray this week for all our bishops and priests, living and departed.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews says: remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith (Hebrews 13:7). Today we remember St Peter in chains, and Bishop Geevarghese Mar Divannasios, who served our Church and went home to God in 2018.

Leaders come and go. But Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever (Hebrews 13:8). The faith they handed on is anchored in him.

Jesus asks: who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom his master has put in charge of his household? Blessed is that slave whom his master will find at work when he arrives (Matthew 24:45–46).

Each of us has been put in charge of something: a family, a job, a ministry. Let us be faithful in it, offer God the sacrifice of praise, and share what we have. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus told about a servant who did his job well even when nobody was watching. Do your homework and chores well, for Jesus!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, the same yesterday, today and forever, we thank you for the leaders who spoke your word to us. Give rest to Bishop Geevarghese Mar Divannasios, strengthen our shepherds as you freed Peter from his chains, and make us faithful stewards found at work when you come. Amen.
