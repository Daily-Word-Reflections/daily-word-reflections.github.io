Word for the day

“Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.” Hebrews 13:8

Reflection

Today our Church keeps the feast of the See of St Peter at Antioch. Ancient tradition holds that Peter led the Church at Antioch before he went to Rome, and it was in Antioch “that the disciples were first called ‘Christians.’” Our Syro-Malankara Church, which prays in the Antiochene tradition, honours this throne of the Apostle with special love. It is not a feast of furniture but of a ministry: the Lord said to Peter, “I have prayed for you that your own faith may not fail; and you, when once you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.” St Leo the Great taught that the firmness of Peter's faith, which Christ praised, lives on in the Church through his successors (in substance). So the Letter to the Hebrews says: “Remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith. Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.” Leaders change; the Lord they serve does not.

Genesis shows what a true leader does for his people: he stands before God for them. “Abraham remained standing before the Lord. Then Abraham came near and said, ‘Will you indeed sweep away the righteous with the wicked?’” Six times he asks, from fifty righteous down to ten, humbly, “I who am but dust and ashes,” yet boldly: “Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?” And six times God agrees. Intercession is the heart of every shepherd's work. In the Gospel Jesus asks: “Who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom his master has put in charge of his household, to give the other slaves their allowance of food at the proper time?” Peter and his successors, our bishops and priests, are servants who feed the household with the word and the sacraments. Today we also remember Thomas Mar Dioscoros, who died in 1943, and we give thanks for the consecration of St Mary's Cathedral in Thiruvananthapuram in 1965.

Homily outline

Theme: “Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.”

On the feast of Peter's See at Antioch: the intercessor, the leaders who spoke God's word, and the faithful servant.

1. Standing before the Lord

  • Will you sweep away the righteous?
  • Dust and ashes, yet bold.
  • From fifty down to ten.
“Far be it from you to do such a thing, to slay the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous fare as the wicked! Far be that from you! Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?” Genesis 18:25
“First of all, then, I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for everyone,” 1 Timothy 2:1

Apply: Pray by name each day this week for one town or neighbourhood.

2. Remember your leaders

  • Peter at Antioch.
  • Strengthen your brothers.
  • The same Christ forever.
“Listen to me, you that pursue righteousness, you that seek the Lord. Look to the rock from which you were hewn, and to the quarry from which you were dug.” Isaiah 51:1
“And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.” Matthew 16:18

Apply: Pray daily during the Fast for the Pope, our Catholicos and your bishop.

3. The faithful servant

  • Food at the proper time.
  • Found at work when he comes.
  • The master is not delayed.
“I will look with favor on the faithful in the land, so that they may live with me; whoever walks in the way that is blameless shall minister to me.” Psalm 101:6
“Moreover, it is required of stewards that they be found trustworthy.” 1 Corinthians 4:2

Apply: Do one duty entrusted to you this week with special faithfulness, as for the Lord.

Shepherds pass; the Christ they serve is the same forever.

Every Christian is called to intercede and to serve faithfully in God's household.

Take-home resolution: Pray each day of the Fast for the shepherds of the Church and for one place that needs God's mercy.

Full homily

Dear friends, today we honour the See of St Peter at Antioch, where the disciples were first called Christians (Acts 11:26). Jesus prayed for Peter that his faith might not fail, and told him to strengthen his brothers (Luke 22:32).

Hebrews says: remember your leaders, those who spoke the word of God to you; consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith. Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever (Hebrews 13:7–8). Shepherds come and go; Christ remains.

A true shepherd stands before God for his people. Abraham remained standing before the Lord and asked: will you indeed sweep away the righteous with the wicked? (Genesis 18:22–23). From fifty to ten he pleaded, and God listened.

Jesus asks: who then is the faithful and wise slave, whom his master has put in charge of his household? (Matthew 24:45). Let us pray for the Pope and our bishops, and let each of us be faithful in the place God has given us. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus chose St Peter to be the leader of his apostles and said: strengthen your brothers. Today we pray for the Pope and our bishops, who take care of the Church like shepherds. Abraham prayed for a whole city. You can pray for your town tonight!

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, you are the same yesterday and today and forever. You prayed for Peter that his faith might not fail and made him the rock of your Church, whose throne at Antioch we honour today. Strengthen the Pope, our Major Archbishop-Catholicos and all our bishops in the faith of Peter. Make us intercessors like Abraham and faithful servants in your household. Grant eternal rest to Thomas Mar Dioscoros, and bless all who pray in our cathedral church. Amen.
