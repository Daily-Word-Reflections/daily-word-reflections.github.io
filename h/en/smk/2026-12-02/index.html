Word for the day

“And how are they to proclaim him unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!”” Romans 10:15

Reflection

Paul builds a chain: people cannot call on one they have not believed in, cannot believe in one they have not heard of, cannot hear without a preacher, and there are no preachers unless they are sent. So the Church sends, and the feet of those who bring good news are beautiful. In this season before Christmas, every Christian is sent to announce that the Saviour comes.

Jesus asks for a simple, truthful tongue. Do not swear by heaven or earth or Jerusalem or your own head. “Let your word be ‘Yes, Yes’ or ‘No, No’; anything more than this comes from the evil one.” The messenger of the Word made flesh must be a person whose word can be trusted. Our fasting purifies the tongue as well as the stomach.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul asks a chain of questions: how are they to call on one in whom they have not believed? How are they to believe in one of whom they have never heard? How are they to hear without someone to proclaim him? And he answers with Isaiah: how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news (Romans 10:14–15).

Faith comes through proclamation. Someone told us about Jesus: a mother, a catechist, a priest, a friend. Now it is our turn. The Lord wants beautiful feet that carry good news to others.

In the Gospel Jesus speaks about honesty: do not swear at all. Let your word be yes, yes or no, no; anything more than this comes from the evil one (Matthew 5:34, 37). A disciple does not need oaths, because his ordinary word can be trusted.

These two readings go together. The best proclamation of the Gospel is a truthful life. If our words at work, at home and on social media are not trustworthy, our words about Jesus will not be believed either.

In Kerala we sometimes swear casually: on God, on our mother, on the Bible. Jesus asks for something simpler and harder: be honest always, so that your yes is enough.

During this fast, let us examine our speech. Do I exaggerate, deceive, make promises I do not keep? Let us fast from lies and half-truths, and let our feet carry good news to someone who has not heard it. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus wants us to tell the truth always. When you say yes, mean it; when you promise, keep it!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you send us to bring good news. Purify our tongues, make our yes and no truthful, and make our feet beautiful on the mountains of this world. Amen.
