Mar Mathai Shleeha (St Matthew the Apostle)

Apostle and Evangelist · Feast: 16 November

Open in the Daily Word app

Word for the day

“As Jesus was walking along, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax booth; and he said to him, “Follow me.” And he got up and followed him.” Matthew 9:9

Life

Matthew, also called Levi, was a tax collector at Capernaum, working for the authorities and despised by his own people. Jesus saw him at the tax booth and said: follow me; and he got up and followed. That same day Jesus ate at his house with tax collectors and sinners, saying that he came to call not the righteous but sinners (Matthew 9:9–13).

The Church has from early times attributed to Matthew the first Gospel, which presents Jesus as the fulfilment of the Law and the prophets. Traditions differ on where he preached and died, naming Ethiopia or Persia. The Latin Church keeps his feast on 21 September; the Eastern Churches remember him on 16 November.

Homily

1. Jesus sees

He saw a man everyone else had written off.

2. Follow me

Two words changed a life; Matthew got up at once.

3. Mercy, not sacrifice

Jesus came to call sinners (Matthew 9:13).

Dear friends, today we remember Mar Mathai Shleeha. Matthew sat at the tax booth, a man his neighbours despised. Jesus passed by, looked at him and said two words: follow me. And he got up and followed him (Matthew 9:9).

That evening Matthew gave a feast for his friends, tax collectors and sinners, and Jesus sat with them. When the Pharisees complained, Jesus answered: I desire mercy, not sacrifice; I have come to call not the righteous but sinners (Matthew 9:13).

No one is too far from God to be called. Jesus still looks at each of us with love and says: follow me. Let us answer at once, and let our table, like Matthew's, be open to those others reject. Amen.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you called Matthew from the tax booth to be your apostle and evangelist. Through his prayers, help us to answer your call at once and to show mercy to all. Amen.
