St Joseph, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary

Guardian of the Redeemer, Patron of the Universal Church

Word for the day

“When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him; he took her as his wife,” Matthew 1:24

Life

Joseph, a descendant of King David, lived in Nazareth as a carpenter (Matthew 13:55). He was betrothed to Mary. When he learned that she was with child, being “a righteous man and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace”, he planned to dismiss her quietly; but an angel told him in a dream that the child was from the Holy Spirit. Joseph obeyed and took Mary into his home.

He gave Jesus his name and, as his legal father, placed him in the line of David. He protected the Child and his mother on the journey to Bethlehem, in the flight to Egypt and on the return to Nazareth. He taught Jesus to work, to pray and to keep the Law. The Gospels record not a single word of his; they record his actions.

He probably died before the public ministry of Jesus, and Christian tradition honours him as patron of a happy death, dying in the arms of Jesus and Mary. Blessed Pius IX declared him Patron of the Universal Church in 1870. Pope St John Paul II wrote Redemptoris Custos (1989) and Pope Francis Patris Corde (2020) about him.

Lessons

Silent and faithful

Joseph speaks through obedience. Holiness does not need many words.

Just and merciful

He would not expose Mary to disgrace: justice with mercy.

The dignity of work

The Son of God learnt a trade from Joseph; ordinary work can be holy.

A father who protects

Joseph rose at night and acted at once to protect his family. Fathers today are called to the same courage.

Stories

The flight into Egypt (history)

An angel warned Joseph in a dream that Herod wanted to kill the child. That very night he took Mary and Jesus and fled to Egypt (Matthew 2:13–15).

Go to Joseph (history)

St Teresa of Ávila had great devotion to St Joseph and spread it among her Carmelite sisters; she wrote that she could not remember asking him for anything that he had failed to obtain.

Words of the saint

“I do not remember even now that I have ever asked anything of him which he has failed to grant.”
St Teresa of Ávila, The Book of Her Life, chapter 6

Homily · The saint's life and message

1. A just man

Faithful to God's law, merciful to Mary (Matthew 1:19).

2. He did as the angel commanded

Prompt obedience (Matthew 1:24).

3. Protector of the Child and His mother

Bethlehem, Egypt, Nazareth.

4. The dignity of work

The carpenter of Nazareth (Matthew 13:55).

Dear brothers and sisters, St Joseph never speaks a word in the Gospels, but his actions speak loudly. He is called a righteous man (Matthew 1:19): faithful to God's law, yet merciful. When he discovered Mary was with child, he did not want to expose her to shame.

When the angel told him in a dream that the child was from the Holy Spirit, Joseph woke and did as the angel commanded (Matthew 1:24). He took Mary into his home, gave Jesus His name, and as legal father placed Him in the line of David. His obedience was prompt and complete.

Joseph protected Jesus and Mary through every danger: the journey to Bethlehem, the flight to Egypt, the return to Nazareth. He worked as a carpenter to support them and taught Jesus to work, to pray and to keep the Law. Pope Pius IX declared him Patron of the Universal Church: he who guarded the Holy Family now guards the Church.

Fathers, workers, and all who protect others, look to St Joseph. Go to Joseph with your needs, as St Teresa of Ávila advised. And let us ask him to guard our families and our Church.

Homily option 2 · In the light of the Word: the dreams of Joseph

1. Joseph of Egypt and Joseph of Nazareth

Two dreamers who saved their families (Genesis 37; 41).

2. God speaks in the night

Four dreams in Matthew 1–2.

3. Faith in the dark

Joseph obeyed without full understanding.

4. Waking up to God's will

He rose and did it.

Dear friends, the Bible has two famous Josephs, and both were dreamers. Joseph, son of Jacob, dreamed dreams, was sold into Egypt and became the saviour of his family in a time of famine; he told Pharaoh: go to Joseph (Genesis 41:55). Joseph of Nazareth also received God's guidance in dreams and also went down to Egypt to save his family.

Four times in the first chapters of Matthew an angel speaks to Joseph in a dream: take Mary as your wife; flee to Egypt; return to Israel; go to Galilee. Each time the message comes in the night, when Joseph is helpless. God often speaks to us in the dark moments of life, when our own plans fail.

Joseph did not understand everything. He was not told how the story would end. He had to trust. Faith is not having all the answers; it is obeying God step by step.

And each time, Joseph rose and did what he was told. He did not argue or delay. Many of us know God's will but postpone it. Let us learn from Joseph to wake up to God's will and do it today.

Homily option 3 · For young people and families: a father's love

1. Fathers as protectors

Guarding the family's body and soul.

2. Tenderness and strength

Joseph's justice was merciful.

3. Work and family

Earning a living without losing the family.

4. Young men: preparing to be fathers

Purity, responsibility, faith.

Dear families, St Joseph is the model for fathers. He protected Mary and Jesus from danger, worked hard to support them and taught Jesus the faith. A father's first task is to protect his family, not only from physical danger but also from what harms the soul.

Joseph's strength was tender. He did not want to shame Mary. Fathers, be strong without being harsh. Correct your children without humiliating them. Show affection. Let your children see you pray.

Many fathers today work far from home, in the Gulf or abroad. Joseph also lived in exile for his family's sake. Even at a distance, stay present: call, listen, pray for your family daily, and do not let money become more important than love.

Young men, you are preparing to be husbands and fathers. Learn from Joseph: purity, responsibility, hard work and faith. A real man is not one who dominates, but one who protects and serves. Go to Joseph and ask him to make you such a man.

Prayer

Almighty God, you entrusted the beginnings of our salvation to the faithful care of St Joseph. Through his intercession may your Church protect the mystery of salvation, and may our fathers and families imitate his faith, obedience and love. Amen.
