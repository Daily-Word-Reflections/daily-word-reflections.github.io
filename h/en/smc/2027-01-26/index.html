Fourth Tuesday of Denha · Republic Day of India

Readings

Word for the day

“But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumphal procession, and through us spreads in every place the fragrance that comes from knowing him.” 2 Corinthians 2:14

Reflection

Paul uses a vivid picture: a Roman victory procession, with incense burning along the streets. Christ is the victor, and we who follow him spread “the fragrance that comes from knowing him” everywhere. “For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing.” A Christian life should have a scent: of kindness, honesty, joy, prayer. People should sense Christ before we speak a word. And Paul adds: “we are not peddlers of God's word like so many; but in Christ we speak as persons of sincerity.”

In the Gospel Jesus begins his ministry in Galilee of the nations, fulfilling Isaiah: “the people who sat in darkness have seen a great light.” “From that time Jesus began to proclaim, ‘Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near.’” Today India celebrates Republic Day, the day its Constitution came into force in 1950, guaranteeing to every citizen justice, liberty, equality and fraternity, and the freedom to profess, practise and propagate religion. As Christians we give thanks for our country, pray for its leaders, and seek to be the fragrance of Christ in our public life. We also remember Timothy and Titus, Paul's companions, who spread that fragrance to Ephesus and Crete.

Homily outline

Theme: “We are the aroma of Christ to God.”

The fragrance of Christ, the light in Galilee, and prayer for our nation.

1. The fragrance of Christ

  • The victory procession.
  • Incense in the streets.
  • A life that smells of Christ.
“Let my prayer be counted as incense before you, and the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice.” Psalm 141:2
“For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing;” 2 Corinthians 2:15

Apply: Do one hidden act of kindness today.

2. Light in Galilee

  • Galilee of the nations.
  • Darkness and shadow.
  • The Kingdom near.
“The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light; those who lived in a land of deep darkness— on them light has shined.” Isaiah 9:2
“the people who sat in darkness have seen a great light, and for those who sat in the region and shadow of death light has dawned.”” Matthew 4:16

Apply: Bring light to one dark situation around you.

3. Prayer for the nation

  • Thanks for the Constitution.
  • Justice, liberty, equality, fraternity.
  • Christians in public life.
“Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people whom he has chosen as his heritage.” Psalm 33:12
“First of all, then, I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for everyone, for kings and all who are in high positions, so that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and dignity.” 1 Timothy 2:1–2

Apply: Pray for India and its leaders today.

Our lives should carry the fragrance of Christ.

We pray and work for the good of our nation.

Take-home resolution: Be honest and courteous in every public dealing this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul writes: thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumphal procession, and through us spreads in every place the fragrance that comes from knowing him (2 Corinthians 2:14). We are the aroma of Christ.

A Christian life should have a scent: kindness, honesty, joy, prayer. People should sense Christ before we speak. Paul adds that we are not peddlers of God's word, but speak as persons of sincerity (2 Corinthians 2:17).

In the Gospel Jesus begins his preaching in Galilee: the people who sat in darkness have seen a great light (Matthew 4:16). Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near.

Today is Republic Day. We thank God for our Constitution, which guarantees justice, liberty, equality and fraternity to all. With Timothy and Titus, let us spread the fragrance of Christ in our nation. Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul says we can be like a sweet smell of Jesus. When we are kind and honest, others notice Jesus! Today, let us pray for India.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, through us spread everywhere the fragrance of knowing you. Make us sincere witnesses of your light. On this Republic Day bless India with justice, liberty, equality and fraternity, and through the prayers of Timothy and Titus make us faithful servants of the Gospel. Amen.
