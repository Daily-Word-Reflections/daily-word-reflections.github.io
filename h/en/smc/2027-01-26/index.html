Word for the day

“And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, for in spite of persecution you received the word with joy inspired by the Holy Spirit,” 1 Thessalonians 1:6

Reflection

Paul's earliest letter begins with thanksgiving for a young Church in Thessalonica: “remembering before our God and Father your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.” Faith that works, love that labours, hope that endures: this is the whole Christian life in one line. The Gospel came to them “not in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and with full conviction”, and they received it with joy even under persecution. Timothy, whom the Church honours today with Titus, was sent back to this very community to strengthen it.

In the Gospel Jesus argues with those who claim Abraham as their father but reject the truth he brings: “Whoever is from God hears the words of God.” Belonging to God is shown by listening to God. On Republic Day, when India remembers the Constitution that came into force in 1950, guaranteeing justice, liberty, equality and fraternity to all and the freedom to profess, practise and propagate religion, Christians are asked to be citizens of that same kind: people whose faith works, whose love labours for the common good, whose hope does not give up on the nation. We give thanks for our country, pray for those who govern, and commit ourselves to truth in public life.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul thanks God for the Christians of Thessalonica, remembering your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ (1 Thessalonians 1:3). Faith that works, love that labours, hope that endures.

The Gospel came to them not in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit, and they received it with joy even in persecution (1 Thessalonians 1:5–6). Timothy, whom we honour today with Titus, was sent to strengthen them.

In the Gospel Jesus says: whoever is from God hears the words of God (John 8:47). Belonging to God is shown by listening to him.

Today India celebrates Republic Day. Let us thank God for our Constitution, which guarantees justice, liberty, equality and fraternity to all, pray for those who govern, and be citizens whose faith works for the common good. Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul was happy because the Christians worked hard, loved others and never gave up. Today let us pray for India and help someone in our town!

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, your word came to Thessalonica in power and in the Holy Spirit. Make our faith active, our love tireless and our hope steadfast. On this Republic Day bless India with justice, liberty, equality and fraternity, and through the prayers of Sts Timothy and Titus make us faithful servants of your Gospel. Amen.
