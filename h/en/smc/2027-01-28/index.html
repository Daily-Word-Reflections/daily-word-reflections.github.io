Word for the day

““You search the scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that testify on my behalf.” John 5:39

Reflection

In Solomon's Portico Peter tells the crowd: “Repent therefore, and turn to God so that your sins may be wiped out, so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord.” He shows that the whole of Scripture points to Jesus: Moses promised a prophet like himself; Samuel and all the prophets “predicted these days”; God told Abraham that in his descendants “all the families of the earth shall be blessed.” Jesus is the one in whom every promise meets.

In the Gospel Jesus says to those who opposed him: “You search the scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that testify on my behalf. Yet you refuse to come to me to have life.” It is possible to study the Bible and miss its centre. “If you believed Moses, you would believe me, for he wrote about me.” Today the Church honours St Thomas Aquinas, who searched the Scriptures and all human wisdom, and found Christ at the heart of both. His learning ended in adoration of Christ in the Eucharist. In this season of Denha, let our reading of Scripture lead us not only to knowledge, but to Christ himself.

Full homily

Dear friends, Peter tells the crowd: repent therefore, and turn to God so that your sins may be wiped out, so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord (Acts 3:19–20).

He shows that all Scripture points to Jesus: Moses, Samuel, all the prophets, and the promise to Abraham that in his descendants all the families of the earth shall be blessed.

In the Gospel Jesus says: you search the scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that testify on my behalf. Yet you refuse to come to me (John 5:39–40). We can study the Bible and miss its centre.

St Thomas Aquinas searched the Scriptures and all wisdom, and found Christ at the heart of both. His study ended in adoration. Let our reading lead us to Christ. Amen.

For children

Children, the whole Bible tells us about Jesus! St Thomas Aquinas studied very hard, but most of all he loved Jesus in the Eucharist.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, all the Scriptures testify to you. Give us humble hearts to repent, open minds to search, and through the prayers of St Thomas Aquinas lead our study to adoration of you, present in the Holy Qurbana. Amen.
