Word for the day

“The child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom; and the favor of God was upon him.” Luke 2:40

Reflection

Luke ends the story of the Presentation with an old woman: Anna, a prophet, widowed after seven years of marriage and now eighty-four. “She never left the temple but worshiped there with fasting and prayer night and day.” At the very moment the Holy Family is in the Temple, she comes, praises God, “and to speak about the child to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.” A lifetime of hidden prayer ends in joyful witness.

Then the family returns to Nazareth, where “the child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom.” Paul thanks God that the Corinthians “have been enriched in him, in speech and knowledge of every kind”, so that they are “not lacking in any spiritual gift” as they wait for the Lord. God is faithful; he gives his people what they need to grow. Anna in old age and the Child in his youth both show that every stage of life can be full of God.

Full homily

Dear friends, at the end of the Presentation story Luke tells us about Anna. She was a prophet, widowed after only seven years of marriage, and now eighty-four years old. She never left the Temple but worshipped there with fasting and prayer night and day (Luke 2:36–37).

Imagine those decades of prayer. No visions, no headlines, just faithfulness. And then one day, at that very moment, she comes and sees the Child. She begins to praise God and to speak about him to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem (Luke 2:38).

Anna teaches us that no prayer is wasted. Many grandmothers and grandfathers in our parishes are Annas: their hidden prayer carries families, vocations and the whole Church.

St Paul thanks God that the Corinthians are not lacking in any spiritual gift as they wait for the Lord (1 Corinthians 1:7). God is faithful. In Nazareth the child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom (Luke 2:40). Let us ask for Anna's faithfulness and the Child's wisdom in this new year. Amen.

For children

Children, Anna was a very old lady who prayed every day. When she saw baby Jesus, she told everyone! Let us thank our grandparents who pray for us.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you grew in wisdom at Nazareth, and Anna proclaimed you to all who waited. Make me faithful in prayer like her, and help me to grow in your grace all through this year. Amen.
