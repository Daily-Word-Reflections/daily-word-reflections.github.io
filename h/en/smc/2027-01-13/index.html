Word for the day

“Jesus did this, the first of his signs, in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him.” John 2:11

Reflection

The ancient Church celebrated three manifestations together: the Magi, the Baptism in the Jordan and the wedding at Cana. Today, in the season of Denha, we hear of Cana. At a village wedding the wine runs out, a disaster for the family's honour. Mary notices and says simply: “They have no wine.” Then she tells the servants: “Do whatever he tells you.” Six stone jars for purification are filled with water, and the water becomes the best wine. John says: “Jesus did this, the first of his signs… and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him.”

Revelation shows where Cana points: “Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” The wedding at Cana is a sign of the great wedding between Christ and his Church, and of the Eucharist where his blood becomes our wine of joy. Mary's words are the best advice ever given: “Do whatever he tells you.” When our lives run out of wine, of joy, love or strength, let us tell Jesus through Mary, and do what he says.

Full homily

Dear friends, the early Church celebrated three epiphanies: the Magi, the Jordan, and Cana. Today, in Denha, we go to the wedding at Cana. The wine runs out. For a village family that is a public shame.

Mary notices first. She says to Jesus: they have no wine (John 2:3). Then she tells the servants: do whatever he tells you (John 2:5). These are her last words in John's Gospel, and the best advice anyone has ever given.

Six stone jars are filled with water. The water becomes the best wine. Jesus did this, the first of his signs, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him (John 2:11).

Revelation shows where it leads: blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb (Revelation 19:9). Every Holy Qurbana is a foretaste of that feast.

When the wine of joy or love or strength runs out in our homes, let us tell Jesus through Mary, and then do whatever he tells us. Amen.

For children

Children, at a wedding there was no more wine. Mary told Jesus, and Jesus turned water into wine! Mary helps us too. Let us ask her today.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, at Cana you revealed your glory and turned water into wine. Through the prayers of your Mother, fill what is empty in our homes, and help us do whatever you tell us, until we sit at the marriage supper of the Lamb. Amen.
