Word for the day

“And he has put all things under his feet and has made him the head over all things for the church, which is his body, the fullness of him who fills all in all.” Ephesians 1:22–23

Reflection

Paul prays for the Ephesians “so that, with the eyes of your heart enlightened, you may know what is the hope to which he has called you.” Faith has eyes: it sees what the world cannot see. And what does it see? The immeasurable power of God at work in raising Christ from the dead and seating him at his right hand, “far above all rule and authority”. God has made Christ “the head over all things for the church, which is his body”. The Church is not a human club; she is the Body of the risen Lord.

John the Baptist says the same in the Gospel: “The one who comes from above is above all… The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands. Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life.” In Denha we contemplate Christ revealed: not one teacher among many, but the Lord above all. The practical question is simple: is Christ really above all in my life, above my plans, my fears, my money, my time?

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul prays that the Ephesians may have the eyes of their heart enlightened, to know the hope to which God has called them (Ephesians 1:18). Faith is a way of seeing.

What does faith see? The power of God that raised Christ from the dead and seated him at his right hand, far above every power. And God has made him the head over all things for the church, which is his body (Ephesians 1:22–23). The Church is the Body of the risen Lord.

In the Gospel John the Baptist says: the one who comes from above is above all. The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands. Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life (John 3:31, 35–36).

In Denha we contemplate Christ revealed. The question is practical: is he really above all in my life? Above my plans, my money, my phone, my fears? Let us give him the first moment of each day this week. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus is above everyone and everything. Let us say good morning to Jesus first thing every day!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, risen and seated at the Father's right hand, you are head over all things. Enlighten the eyes of my heart, let me see the hope of my calling, and be truly first in my life. Amen.
