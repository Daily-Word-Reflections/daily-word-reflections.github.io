Word for the day

“Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, which you have from God, and that you are not your own? For you were bought with a price; therefore glorify God in your body.” 1 Corinthians 6:19–20

Reflection

After Cana, John shows Jesus in the Temple of Jerusalem at Passover. He makes a whip of cords, drives out the sellers and overturns the tables: “Stop making my Father's house a marketplace!” The disciples remember the psalm: “Zeal for your house will consume me.” Asked for a sign, Jesus says: “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.” John explains: “he was speaking of the temple of his body.” The true meeting place of God and humanity is now the body of Jesus, crucified and risen.

Paul draws the conclusion for us: “your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you… you are not your own. For you were bought with a price; therefore glorify God in your body.” Christian chastity, sobriety and care for health are not rules imposed from outside; they are reverence for a temple. Today the Church in India honours St Devasahayam, who after his Baptism refused to treat anyone as untouchable and gave his body in martyrdom. In him the temple of the body became a living offering.

Full homily

Dear friends, after Cana Jesus goes up to Jerusalem for the Passover. In the Temple he finds people selling cattle, sheep and doves, and money changers at their tables. He makes a whip of cords, drives them out and says: stop making my Father's house a marketplace! (John 2:16).

The disciples remember the psalm: zeal for your house will consume me (John 2:17). Jesus burns with love for his Father's house.

When they ask for a sign, he says: destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up. John explains: he was speaking of the temple of his body (John 2:19, 21). God now meets us in Jesus, crucified and risen.

St Paul applies this to us: your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit; you are not your own; you were bought with a price; therefore glorify God in your body (1 Corinthians 6:19–20). Purity, sobriety and care for health are reverence for a temple.

Today we honour St Devasahayam, who after Baptism gave his body in martyrdom for Christ. Let us honour God's house, and the temple of our bodies. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus loved his Father's house very much. Your body is God's house too! Take good care of it, and be quiet and prayerful in church.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, zeal for your Father's house consumed you, and your body became the true temple, destroyed and raised for us. Cleanse the temple of my body and heart, and through the prayers of St Devasahayam make my life a living offering. Amen.
