Fourth Wednesday of the Dedication of the Church

25 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Ephesians 1:3–12 · The hymn of God's plan of salvation
  • Gospel Luke 1:46–55 · Mary's Magnificat

Word for the day

“He has brought down the powerful from their thrones, and lifted up the lowly; he has filled the hungry with good things.” Luke 1:52–53

Reflection

Paul's great blessing opens the mystery of the whole year: “Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing… he chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless before him in love.” God's plan is “to gather up all things in him.”

Mary's Magnificat is the Church's song of that plan seen from below: God looks on the lowliness of his servant, scatters the proud, lifts up the lowly, fills the hungry. As the year ends and Annunciation approaches, the Church sings with Mary, the first of the redeemed and the image of the Church herself.

Homily outline

Theme: “He has done great things.”

Paul's hymn of God's plan and Mary's Magnificat close the year in praise.

1. Chosen in Christ

  • God chose us before the foundation of the world.
  • His plan is to gather all things in Christ.
“And in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.” Genesis 12:3
“His mercy is for those who fear him from generation to generation.” Luke 1:50

Apply: Thank God for three blessings of this year.

2. He lifts the lowly

  • Hannah sang it before Mary.
  • God reverses the world's order.
“The LORD makes poor and makes rich; he brings low, he also exalts. He raises up the poor from the dust; he lifts the needy from the ash heap.” 1 Samuel 2:7–8
“He has brought down the powerful from their thrones, and lifted up the lowly; he has filled the hungry with good things.” Luke 1:52–53

Apply: Do one act of respect for someone overlooked.

3. The Church sings with Mary

  • Mary is the first of the redeemed.
  • The Church is called to her humility and praise.
“Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people whom he has chosen as his heritage.” Psalm 33:12
“For he has looked with favor on the lowliness of his servant… for the Mighty One has done great things for me.” Luke 1:48–49

Apply: Pray the Magnificat tonight.

The year ends in praise.

With Mary we sing: holy is his name.

Take-home resolution: Pray the Magnificat each evening until Annunciation Sunday.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul's great blessing opens the mystery of the whole year: blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places, just as he chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless before him in love (Ephesians 1:3–4).

Mary sings the same joy in her Magnificat: my soul magnifies the Lord, for he has looked with favour on the lowliness of his servant. He has brought down the powerful from their thrones and lifted up the lowly (Luke 1:46–52). Mary's song is the Church's song.

As the season of the Dedication of the Church comes to an end, let us thank God with Mary for the blessings of a whole year. This evening, sit together as a family, name five blessings of this year, and pray a rosary of thanksgiving. Amen.

For children

Children, Mary sang a song of praise to God. Today thank God for five good things he gave you this year!

Prayer

Blessed be God, who chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world. With Mary I praise you: you have done great things for me, and holy is your name. Amen.
