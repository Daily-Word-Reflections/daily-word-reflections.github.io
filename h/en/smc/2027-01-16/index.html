Word for the day

“Come to me, all you that are weary and are carrying heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me; for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.” Matthew 11:28–29

Reflection

Jesus prays aloud: “I thank you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because you have hidden these things from the wise and the intelligent and have revealed them to infants.” The revelation celebrated in Denha is received not by the proud but by the little. Then he turns to us with the most tender invitation in the Gospels: “Come to me, all you that are weary and are carrying heavy burdens, and I will give you rest… learn from me; for I am gentle and humble in heart.”

Paul shows how deep this humility goes. “Let the same mind be in you that was in Christ Jesus, who, though he was in the form of God, did not regard equality with God as something to be exploited, but emptied himself, taking the form of a slave.” He humbled himself to death on a cross, and therefore God exalted him. At the end of this week of Denha, the revealed Lord shows us his heart: gentle and humble. His yoke is not a heavy law but a shared harness with him, and in it we find rest.

Full homily

Dear friends, Jesus prays: I thank you, Father, because you have hidden these things from the wise and the intelligent and have revealed them to infants (Matthew 11:25). The revelation of Denha is received by the humble, not by the proud.

Then he invites us: come to me, all you that are weary and are carrying heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me; for I am gentle and humble in heart (Matthew 11:28–29).

Paul shows how deep his humility goes: though he was in the form of God, he emptied himself, taking the form of a slave, and humbled himself to death on a cross (Philippians 2:6–8).

A yoke joins two animals so they pull together. Jesus does not lay a burden on us and leave; he walks beside us under the same yoke. Bring him your burden this week and learn from his gentle, humble heart. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: come to me when you are tired, and I will help you. Jesus is gentle and kind. Tell him what is hard for you.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, gentle and humble of heart, you emptied yourself for us. Reveal the Father to me as to a little child, take my burden, and let me learn from you and find rest for my soul. Amen.
