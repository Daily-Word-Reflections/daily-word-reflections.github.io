First Monday of Annunciation

30 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Galatians 3:9–14 · The righteous will live by faith
  • Gospel Luke 9:37–43a · Faithless and perverse generation

Word for the day

“Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and gave him back to his father. And all were astounded at the greatness of God.” Luke 9:42–43

Reflection

Paul reminds the Galatians that “those who believe are blessed with Abraham who believed.” Christ became a curse for us “in order that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.” The Annunciation season is about this faith: Abraham's, Zechariah's, Mary's.

Coming down from the mountain, Jesus finds his disciples unable to heal a boy. “You faithless and perverse generation,” he says, and heals the child, giving him back to his father. Our failures are not the end; Christ himself acts, and all are astounded at the greatness of God.

Homily outline

Theme: “The righteous live by faith.”

Abraham's faith brings blessing; Jesus heals where the disciples failed.

1. Abraham believed

  • Faith was reckoned to Abraham as righteousness.
  • Believers are Abraham's children.
“And he believed the LORD; and the LORD reckoned it to him as righteousness.” Genesis 15:6
“For this reason, those who believe are blessed with Abraham who believed.” Galatians 3:9

Apply: Make an act of faith each morning this week.

2. Living by faith

  • The prophet Habakkuk: the righteous live by faith.
  • Christ bore the curse so that the blessing might reach us.
“Look at the proud! Their spirit is not right in them, but the righteous live by their faith.” Habakkuk 2:4
“In order that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.” Galatians 3:14

Apply: Thank Christ for bearing the curse for you.

3. The greatness of God

  • The disciples could not heal.
  • Jesus restores the boy to his father.
“Is anything too wonderful for the LORD?” Genesis 18:14
“Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, healed the boy, and gave him back to his father. And all were astounded at the greatness of God.” Luke 9:42–43

Apply: Bring one impossible situation to Jesus.

Faith receives what God promises.

Let us trust the One who heals.

Take-home resolution: Pray “Lord, increase my faith” daily during this season.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the first weekday of the season of Annunciation the Church looks to the faith of Abraham. Paul reminds the Galatians that those who believe are blessed with Abraham who believed; Christ became a curse for us, so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, and we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith (Galatians 3:9, 13–14).

In the Gospel a father brings to Jesus his only son, whom the disciples could not heal. Jesus rebukes the unclean spirit, heals the boy and gives him back to his father; and all were astounded at the greatness of God (Luke 9:42–43).

Today is also the feast of St Andrew the Apostle, who, like our father St Thomas, carried Christ to distant lands. In these weeks before Christmas let us believe like Abraham and bring our children to Jesus like that father. The Twenty-Five Days Lent begins tomorrow; make your resolution today. Amen.

For children

Children, the season of Annunciation has begun! Do one good thing every day these weeks to prepare your heart for baby Jesus.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you healed the boy and gave him back to his father. Strengthen my weak faith, and let all who see your works be astounded at the greatness of God. Amen.
