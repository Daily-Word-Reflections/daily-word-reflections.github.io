Word for the day

“But the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.” 2 Thessalonians 3:3

Reflection

Paul ends his letter to the Thessalonians by asking for their prayers: “pray for us, so that the word of the Lord may spread rapidly and be glorified everywhere, just as it is among you, and that we may be rescued from wicked and evil people; for not all have faith.” He is honest about the opposition he meets. But he does not rest his hope on how many believe. He rests it on someone else: “But the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.” Our faith rises and falls; the Lord's faithfulness does not. So Paul can end with a blessing that is also a direction for the whole of life: “May the Lord direct your hearts to the love of God and to the steadfastness of Christ.”

In the Gospel two daughters meet the faithful Lord. A leader of the synagogue kneels before Jesus: “My daughter has just died; but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live.” On the way a woman who has suffered from hemorrhages for twelve years comes up behind him. Under the law her illness made her unclean and kept her apart from worship and from people. She does not dare to ask; she only reaches out: “If I only touch his cloak, I will be made well.” Jesus turns and looks at her. He does not scold her for touching him; he calls her by a family name: “Take heart, daughter; your faith has made you well.” Then at the leader's house, among the flute players and the noisy mourners, he says, “Go away; for the girl is not dead but sleeping.” They laugh at him. He goes in, takes her by the hand, and the girl gets up. In these last days of Denha Christ is revealed as Lord over sickness and death. A touch of faith is enough, because the one who is touched is faithful.

Homily outline

Theme: “But the Lord is faithful.”

The faithful Lord, the woman who reached out, and the girl raised by the hand.

1. The Lord is faithful

  • Not all have faith.
  • He strengthens and guards.
  • Hearts directed to God's love.
“The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases, his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.” Lamentations 3:22–23
“But the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.” 2 Thessalonians 3:3

Apply: Thank God each morning this week for one sign of his faithfulness.

2. If I only touch his cloak

  • Twelve years of suffering.
  • Reaching out from behind.
  • Take heart, daughter.
“Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all his benefits— who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases,” Psalm 103:2–3
“Jesus turned, and seeing her he said, “Take heart, daughter; your faith has made you well.” And instantly the woman was made well.” Matthew 9:22

Apply: Bring one long-standing trouble to Jesus in prayer today.

3. Not dead but sleeping

  • The mourners laugh.
  • He takes her by the hand.
  • The girl gets up.
“Your dead shall live, their corpses shall rise. O dwellers in the dust, awake and sing for joy!” Isaiah 26:19
“But when the crowd had been put outside, he went in and took her by the hand, and the girl got up.” Matthew 9:25

Apply: Pray for a family that has lost a child or a young person.

Our faith may be small, but the Lord is faithful.

Reach out and touch him; he turns, and he raises up.

Take-home resolution: Each day this week, make one short act of trust in the Lord's faithfulness.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul asks the Thessalonians to pray that the word of the Lord may spread, because not all have faith. Then he adds: but the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one (2 Thessalonians 3:3).

In the Gospel a woman who has been ill for twelve years comes up behind Jesus. She thinks: if I only touch his cloak, I will be made well (Matthew 9:21). Jesus turns, looks at her and says: take heart, daughter; your faith has made you well.

Then he goes to the house of the synagogue leader, whose daughter has died. The mourners laugh when he says she is sleeping. But he takes her by the hand, and the girl gets up.

Our faith may be as small as a touch. It is enough, because the one we touch is faithful. Let us reach out to him today, and let him direct our hearts to the love of God. Amen.

For children

Children, a sick lady touched just the edge of Jesus' clothes, and she was healed! Jesus loves it when we come to him with trust. What will you ask Jesus today?

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, faithful and true, you turned to the woman who touched your cloak and took the dead girl by the hand. Strengthen us and guard us from the evil one, direct our hearts to the love of God and to your own steadfastness, and when our faith is small, let it be enough to reach out and touch you. Amen.
