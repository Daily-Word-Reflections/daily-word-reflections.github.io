Word for the day

“Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” Revelation 19:9

Reflection

Heaven bursts into song: “Hallelujah! For the Lord our God the Almighty reigns… the marriage of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready.” The whole history of salvation is a love story that ends in a wedding: Christ and his Church.

In Jesus' parable the invited guests make excuses, and the king fills the hall with people from the streets, “both good and bad”. But one guest has no wedding garment. The invitation is free, but it must be received: the garment is the new life of grace put on in baptism and kept clean by conversion.

Homily outline

Theme: “The wedding of the Lamb.”

The Bible ends with a wedding; the parable warns us to accept the invitation and wear the garment of grace.

1. God rejoices over his bride

  • The prophets describe God as a Bridegroom.
  • Christ loves the Church as his Bride.
“For as a young man marries a young woman, so shall your builder marry you, and as the bridegroom rejoices over the bride, so shall your God rejoice over you.” Isaiah 62:5
“Let us rejoice and exult and give him the glory, for the marriage of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready.” Revelation 19:7

Apply: Thank God for loving you as a Bridegroom.

2. A feast for all peoples

  • Isaiah sees a banquet for all nations.
  • The king's servants go to the streets.
“On this mountain the LORD of hosts will make for all peoples a feast of rich food, a feast of well-aged wines.” Isaiah 25:6
“Go therefore into the main streets, and invite everyone you find to the wedding banquet.” Matthew 22:9

Apply: Invite someone to come with you to the Qurbana.

3. Blessed are those invited

  • The garment is the grace of baptism.
  • Keep it clean through confession.
“And I will take you for my wife forever; I will take you for my wife in righteousness and in justice, in steadfast love and in mercy.” Hosea 2:19
“And the angel said to me, “Write this: Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.”” Revelation 19:9

Apply: Plan a good confession.

The feast is ready; the invitation is ours.

Let us come, clothed in grace.

Take-home resolution: Attend one weekday Qurbana this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, heaven bursts into song: Hallelujah! For the Lord our God the Almighty reigns; the marriage of the Lamb has come, and his bride has made herself ready (Revelation 19:6–7). The whole history of salvation is a love story that ends in a wedding: Christ and his Church.

In Jesus' parable a king prepares a wedding feast for his son. The invited guests refuse to come, so the servants gather all they find on the roads, both good and bad. But the man who came without a wedding garment is put out (Matthew 22:9–13). The invitation is for all, but those who accept must prepare their hearts.

Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb (Revelation 19:9). Every Holy Qurbana is a foretaste of that feast. Our wedding garment is grace: received in Baptism and washed again in Confession. This Sunday let us come to the feast with a clean heart. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus invites us to a great wedding feast. The Holy Qurbana is a little taste of that feast. Come to church with joy!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, Lamb and Bridegroom, you invite us all to your wedding feast. Clothe us with your grace and make us ready for the marriage supper of the Lamb. Amen.
