Third Saturday of Denha

23 January 2027 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Romans 10:5–13 · Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved
  • Gospel Mark 1:40–45 · Jesus heals the leper

Word for the day

“because if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.” Romans 10:9

Reflection

Paul tells the Romans that salvation is not far away, as if we had to climb to heaven or descend into the abyss to find it: “The word is near you, on your lips and in your heart.” Faith is simple and total: “if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.” And it is for everyone: “there is no distinction between Jew and Greek… Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Mark shows what calling on the Lord looks like. A leper, excluded from every town, comes to Jesus, kneels and begs: “If you choose, you can make me clean.” Jesus is moved with pity, touches him and heals him. The leper did what Paul describes: he called on the Lord with his lips, trusting with his heart. Salvation is near to anyone who calls, however far they feel from God. This is the good news of Denha for all peoples.

Homily outline

Theme: “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

The near word, the saving confession, the leper who called.

1. The word is near

  • Not in heaven.
  • Not in the abyss.
  • On your lips and in your heart.
“No, the word is very near to you; it is in your mouth and in your heart for you to observe.” Deuteronomy 30:14
“But what does it say? “The word is near you, on your lips and in your heart” (that is, the word of faith that we proclaim);” Romans 10:8

Apply: Memorise Romans 10:9 this week.

2. Confess and believe

  • Jesus is Lord.
  • God raised him.
  • Salvation for all.
“Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is no other.” Isaiah 45:22
“because if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.” Romans 10:9

Apply: Say aloud each day: Jesus, you are Lord.

3. If you choose

  • The excluded leper.
  • He kneels and calls.
  • Jesus touches and heals.
“Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.” Psalm 51:7
“A leper came to him begging him, and kneeling he said to him, “If you choose, you can make me clean.”” Mark 1:40

Apply: Bring your deepest need to Jesus in prayer today.

Salvation is near to anyone who calls.

Lips and heart together confess: Jesus is Lord.

Take-home resolution: Pray the name of Jesus whenever you are tempted or afraid this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul says salvation is not far away. We do not have to climb to heaven or go down into the abyss. The word is near you, on your lips and in your heart (Romans 10:8).

The faith that saves is simple: if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved (Romans 10:9). Lips and heart together.

And it is for everyone: everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved (Romans 10:13). No distinction of race or past.

In the Gospel a leper kneels and says: if you choose, you can make me clean (Mark 1:40). He called on the Lord, and Jesus touched and healed him. However far we feel from God, he is near to anyone who calls. Let us say today with lips and heart: Jesus is Lord. Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul says anyone who calls on Jesus will be saved. Let us say it together now: Jesus is Lord!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, your word is near me, on my lips and in my heart. I confess that you are Lord and believe that the Father raised you from the dead. Like the leper I call on you: if you choose, you can make me clean. Amen.
