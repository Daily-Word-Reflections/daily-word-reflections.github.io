Second Tuesday of Nativity · Eve of Denha

5 January 2027 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Hebrews 3:1–6 · Moses faithful as a servant, Christ as a son
  • Gospel John 1:19–28 · The testimony of John

Word for the day

“Christ, however, was faithful over God's house as a son, and we are his house if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope.” Hebrews 3:6

Reflection

On the eve of Denha the Church sets two great figures side by side. Hebrews honours Moses: he “was faithful in all God's house as a servant.” But Jesus is greater, “faithful over God's house as a son”, as the builder is greater than the house. And then a surprise: “we are his house if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope.” The house of God is not only a building; it is the people who keep faith.

In the Gospel the priests and Levites question John at the Jordan. He refuses every great title: he is not the Messiah, nor Elijah, nor the prophet. He is a voice, and he baptises only with water: “Among you stands one whom you do not know.” Tomorrow that unknown one will come down into the Jordan and be revealed by the Father's voice and the Spirit. Our ancestors in Kerala kept this night with lights on plantain stems, crying “El Paiya”, God is light, and with a bath in river or pond recalling the Jordan. Tonight let us prepare our hearts to see him.

Homily outline

Theme: “We are his house if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope.”

Servant and Son; the unknown one among us.

1. Moses the faithful servant

  • Entrusted with God's house.
  • Faithful in all things.
  • A witness to what would come.
“Not so with my servant Moses; he is entrusted with all my house.” Numbers 12:7
“Now Moses was faithful in all God's house as a servant, to testify to the things that would be spoken later.” Hebrews 3:5

Apply: Thank God for a faithful servant who taught you the faith.

2. Christ the Son; we are his house

  • The builder is greater than the house.
  • Christ is the Son and heir.
  • We are the house if we hold firm.
“He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.” 2 Samuel 7:13
“Christ, however, was faithful over God's house as a son, and we are his house if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope.” Hebrews 3:6

Apply: Make one change at home that makes it more a house of God.

3. One whom you do not know

  • John refuses titles.
  • He baptises with water.
  • The hidden one stands among us.
“Every valley shall be lifted up, and every mountain and hill be made low; the uneven ground shall become level, and the rough places a plain.” Isaiah 40:4
“John answered them, “I baptize with water. Among you stands one whom you do not know, the one who is coming after me; I am not worthy to untie the thong of his sandal.”” John 1:26–27

Apply: Light a lamp tonight and pray for those who do not know Christ.

Tomorrow the hidden one is revealed at the Jordan.

Let us be his house, lit and ready.

Take-home resolution: Keep the Denha eve with a lamp and family prayer.

Full homily

Dear friends, tonight is the eve of Denha. The letter to the Hebrews compares Moses and Jesus. Moses was faithful in all God's house as a servant. Christ was faithful over God's house as a son (Hebrews 3:5–6). The servant is honoured; the Son is the heir and builder.

Then Hebrews says something wonderful: we are his house, if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope (Hebrews 3:6). We are not only visitors in God's house. We are the house, the living temple where God dwells.

In the Gospel John the Baptist is questioned. Who are you? He refuses every title. He is only a voice. And he says: among you stands one whom you do not know (John 1:26). Jesus is already there in the crowd, unknown.

Tomorrow that hidden one will step into the Jordan, and the heavens will open. Our ancestors kept this night with lamps on plantain stems, crying El Paiya, God is light, and with a bath that recalled the Jordan. Let us light a lamp in our hearts tonight, and prepare to see him. Amen.

For children

Children, tonight is the eve of Denha! In many homes people light lamps and say: God is light! Light a lamp with your family and say thank you to Jesus.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, faithful Son over God's house, John prepared your way at the Jordan. On this holy night make our home your house, and let us hold firm in hope until we see your glory. Amen.
