Word for the day

“For a child has been born for us, a son given to us; authority rests upon his shoulders; and he is named Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.” Isaiah 9:6

Reflection

Two days before Christmas the whole Church holds her breath. The long season of announcements, to Zechariah, to Mary, to Joseph, is ending. Isaiah's names for the Child gather all our hopes: Wonderful Counselor for our confusion, Mighty God for our weakness, Everlasting Father for our loneliness, Prince of Peace for our quarrels.

These last days are for quiet preparation: finishing the 25 Days' Fast well, making peace, going to confession, and preparing a place, in the home and in the heart, for the Child who comes.

Full homily

Dear friends, two days before Christmas the Church holds her breath. Isaiah gives us four names for the Child: Wonderful Counsellor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace (Isaiah 9:6). Let us take them as four gifts for our needs.

Wonderful Counsellor, for our confusion. Many of us do not know which way to go: in studies, marriage, work, a family conflict. The Child born in Bethlehem is Wisdom itself. Bring your decisions to him in prayer and in his word.

Mighty God, for our weakness. We fail in the same sins again and again. We feel powerless before illness, debts or addictions. The Child is weak in appearance, but he is almighty God. His power works in our weakness.

Everlasting Father, for our loneliness. Some will spend this Christmas alone: widows, migrants, the sick, the elderly whose children are far away. The Child carries the Father's heart and never leaves us.

Prince of Peace, for our quarrels. Families divided over property, neighbours not speaking, communities in conflict. The Prince of Peace comes to make peace. Before Christmas, let us take one step towards someone we are not at peace with.

These last two days are for quiet preparation: finish the Nombu well, go to confession, make peace, and prepare a place in the home and in the heart. Let us pray today: come, Wonderful Counsellor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus is called the Prince of Peace. Make peace with your brother or sister today to get ready for Christmas!

Prayer

Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace: come and be born in our hearts and homes. Amen.
