Word for the day

“And he said to them, “Follow me, and I will make you fish for people.” Immediately they left their nets and followed him.” Matthew 4:19–20

Reflection

On the last day of the liturgical year the weekday readings look at the heavenly city: “I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb.” Jesus tells the Samaritan woman that true worshippers will worship the Father “in spirit and truth”. The whole year of feasts and Sundays has been a school for that worship.

We also keep St Andrew, the first-called. In John's Gospel he is the first to follow Jesus and immediately brings his brother: “We have found the Messiah.” Andrew is the patron of everyone who brings another to Christ. As the new year begins tomorrow with the Annunciation, Andrew reminds us that the Good News is meant to be shared: “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!”

Homily outline

Theme: “We have found the Messiah.”

The last day of the year: the heavenly temple, worship in spirit and truth, and St Andrew who brings others to Jesus.

1. The gate of heaven

  • Jacob finds God in an unexpected place.
  • Every church is a gate of heaven.
“And he was afraid, and said, “How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven.”” Genesis 28:17
“I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb.” Revelation 21:22

Apply: Enter church tomorrow with reverence.

2. Follow me

  • Andrew and Peter leave their nets at once.
  • Discipleship begins with a decision.
“I am now sending for many fishermen, says the LORD, and they shall catch them.” Jeremiah 16:16
“And he said to them, “Follow me, and I will make you fish for people.” Immediately they left their nets and followed him.” Matthew 4:19–20

Apply: Leave one “net” that holds you back.

3. Bring your brother

  • Andrew brings Peter.
  • The nations will say: “Let us go with you, for God is with you.”
“In those days ten men from nations of every language shall take hold of a Jew, grasping his garment and saying, “Let us go with you, for we have heard that God is with you.”” Zechariah 8:23
“He first found his brother Simon and said to him, “We have found the Messiah.”” John 1:41

Apply: Invite someone to the Qurbana tomorrow.

The year ends where it began: with the house of God.

Tomorrow a new year begins; bring someone with you.

Take-home resolution: Begin the new liturgical year with confession and Holy Qurbana.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the last day of the season of the Dedication of the Church we see Jacob dreaming of a ladder set up on the earth, its top reaching to heaven, and the angels of God ascending and descending on it. Jacob wakes and says: this is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven (Genesis 28:12–17). Every church, every Holy Qurbana, is a gate of heaven.

Today we keep in advance the feast of St Andrew. By the Sea of Galilee Jesus calls Peter and Andrew: follow me, and I will make you fish for people. Immediately they left their nets and followed him (Matthew 4:19–20). Paul says that everyone who calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved; but how are they to hear without someone to proclaim him (Romans 10:13–14)?

The first thing Andrew did was to bring his brother to Jesus. Tomorrow the season of Annunciation begins. Decide today to bring one person to Jesus this Christmas season: your brother or sister, or a friend. Amen.

For children

Children, when Andrew met Jesus he ran and brought his brother. Invite a friend to church too!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, as this liturgical year ends, I thank you for all your gifts. Like Andrew, let me follow you at once and bring others to you, that we may worship the Father in spirit and truth. Amen.
