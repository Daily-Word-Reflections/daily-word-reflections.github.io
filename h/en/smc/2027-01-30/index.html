Fourth Saturday of Denha

Readings

  • Epistle Ephesians 4:25–32 · Forgive each other
  • Gospel Matthew 6:5–15 · Forgive your brother

Word for the day

“and be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ has forgiven you.” Ephesians 4:32

Reflection

Paul gives the Ephesians a practical rule of life for a community made one in Christ: speak the truth, “for we are members of one another.” “Be angry but do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger, and do not make room for the devil.” Let no evil talk come out of your mouths, “but only what is useful for building up.” “Put away from you all bitterness and wrath and anger and wrangling and slander.” And the summit: “be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ has forgiven you.”

In the Gospel Jesus teaches how to pray: not to be seen by others, but in secret, to the Father who sees in secret; not heaping up empty phrases, for “your Father knows what you need before you ask him.” Then he gives the Our Father, and adds a single commentary, on one line only: “For if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you; but if you do not forgive others, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.” Every time we pray the Lord's Prayer, we ask to be forgiven in the measure we forgive. Saturday, the day before the Lord's Day, is a good day to make peace.

Homily outline

Theme: “Forgiving one another, as God in Christ has forgiven you.”

Speech that builds, anger that does not last, and the Our Father's condition.

1. Members of one another

  • Speak the truth.
  • Words that build up.
  • Grieve not the Spirit.
“A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.” Proverbs 15:1
“Let no evil talk come out of your mouths, but only what is useful for building up, as there is need, so that your words may give grace to those who hear.” Ephesians 4:29

Apply: Say only words that build up today.

2. Before the sun goes down

  • Anger without sin.
  • No room for the devil.
  • Peace before sleep.
“When you are disturbed, do not sin; ponder it on your beds, and be silent.” Psalm 4:4
“Be angry but do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger,” Ephesians 4:26

Apply: Make peace with someone before bedtime.

3. Forgive as you are forgiven

  • Prayer in secret.
  • The Father knows.
  • The one condition.
“Forgive your neighbor the wrong he has done, and then your sins will be pardoned when you pray.” Sirach 28:2
“For if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you;” Matthew 6:14

Apply: Pray the Our Father slowly, naming someone to forgive.

We are one body; let our words and hearts build it up.

Forgive before the sun sets, and pray as forgiven children.

Take-home resolution: Never go to bed angry this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul tells the Ephesians: we are members of one another. So speak the truth, and let no evil talk come out of your mouths, but only what is useful for building up.

He adds: be angry but do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger (Ephesians 4:26). Anger kept overnight becomes bitterness.

In the Gospel Jesus teaches the Our Father, and then comments on only one line: if you forgive others their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you (Matthew 6:14).

Every Our Father is a promise to forgive. Today, the eve of the Lord's Day, let us be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, as God in Christ has forgiven us (Ephesians 4:32). Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul says: do not stay angry when the sun goes down! Before you sleep tonight, say sorry and forgive.

Prayer

Our Father, who see in secret, teach us to pray and to forgive. Take away all bitterness and anger from our hearts, make us kind and tenderhearted, and forgive us as we forgive those who trespass against us. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
