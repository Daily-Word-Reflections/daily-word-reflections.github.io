First Thursday of Denha

7 January 2027 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Philippians 1:12–18 · Christ is proclaimed in every way
  • Gospel John 3:22–30 · He must increase, but I must decrease

Word for the day

“What does it matter? Just this, that Christ is proclaimed in every way, whether out of false motives or true; and in that I rejoice.” Philippians 1:18

Reflection

The day after Denha the Church listens to two men who were glad when Christ was made known, whatever it cost them. Paul writes from prison, yet his letter is full of joy: “what has happened to me has actually helped to spread the gospel.” His chains have become a sermon to the imperial guard, and other believers have grown bolder. Some preach Christ out of rivalry, hoping to hurt Paul; he does not mind: “Christ is proclaimed in every way… and in that I rejoice.”

John the Baptist shows the same freedom when his disciples complain that everyone is going to Jesus: “He must increase, but I must decrease.” The friend of the bridegroom rejoices at the bridegroom's voice. In the season of Denha, when Christ is manifested to the world, the Church asks us to check our hearts: do we rejoice when Christ is known, even through others, even at our expense?

Full homily

Dear friends, on the day after Denha we hear from Paul in prison. You would expect complaints. Instead he writes: what has happened to me has actually helped to spread the gospel (Philippians 1:12). The whole imperial guard knows why he is in chains, and other believers have become bolder.

Some, he says, preach Christ out of envy, trying to make his imprisonment harder. Paul's answer is astonishing: what does it matter? Christ is proclaimed in every way, and in that I rejoice (Philippians 1:18). He cares more about Christ being known than about his own reputation.

John the Baptist has the same heart. When his disciples complain that everyone is going to Jesus, he says: he must increase, but I must decrease (John 3:30). He is the friend of the bridegroom, and his joy is complete.

In the season of Denha Christ is manifested to the world. Let us check our hearts. Envy and rivalry can enter even church work. May we rejoice whenever Christ is proclaimed, by whoever proclaims him. Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul was in prison, but he was happy because people were hearing about Jesus. Let us be happy when others do good things too!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, manifested at the Jordan, let your name be proclaimed in every way. Free me from envy and rivalry; let me rejoice when you increase, even if I decrease. Amen.
