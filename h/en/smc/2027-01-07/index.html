First Thursday of Denha

7 January 2027 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle 1 Corinthians 2:6–10 · God's wisdom, secret and hidden
  • Gospel Luke 7:11–17 · A great prophet has risen among us

Word for the day

“Fear seized all of them; and they glorified God, saying, “A great prophet has risen among us!” and “God has looked favorably on his people!”” Luke 7:16

Reflection

The day after Denha the Church goes on contemplating what God has revealed. St Paul speaks of a wisdom that is “not a wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age”, but “God's wisdom, secret and hidden, which God decreed before the ages for our glory.” The powerful did not grasp it; “if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.” Now it has been opened to us. “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the human heart conceived, what God has prepared for those who love him”, God has revealed “through the Spirit; for the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God.”

In the Gospel this hidden wisdom shows its face. At the gate of Nain Jesus meets a funeral: a widow is burying “his mother's only son”. No one asks him for anything. Luke says simply that the Lord saw her and “had compassion for her”. He tells her not to weep, touches the bier and commands: “Young man, I say to you, rise!” Then “Jesus gave him to his mother”, the very gesture of Elijah at Zarephath. The crowd cries out: “A great prophet has risen among us!” and “God has looked favorably on his people!” In the season of Denha this is what manifestation means: the wisdom of God revealed as a compassion that stops at our funerals and gives life.

Homily outline

Theme: “A great prophet has risen among us!”

The hidden wisdom of God, revealed in compassion.

1. Wisdom hidden and revealed

  • Not the wisdom of this age.
  • Decreed before the ages.
  • Revealed by the Spirit.
“For the LORD gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding;” Proverbs 2:6
“But we speak God's wisdom, secret and hidden, which God decreed before the ages for our glory.” 1 Corinthians 2:7

Apply: Ask the Holy Spirit for wisdom each time you open the Scriptures.

2. What no eye has seen

  • Beyond our imagining.
  • Prepared for those who love him.
  • Love opens the eyes.
“From ages past no one has heard, no ear has perceived, no eye has seen any God besides you, who works for those who wait for him.” Isaiah 64:4
“But, as it is written, “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the human heart conceived, what God has prepared for those who love him”” 1 Corinthians 2:9

Apply: Make an act of love for God each morning this week.

3. Do not weep

  • The widow's only son.
  • Jesus has compassion.
  • He gives him to his mother.
“Elijah took the child, brought him down from the upper chamber into the house, and gave him to his mother; then Elijah said, “See, your son is alive.”” 1 Kings 17:23
“When the Lord saw her, he had compassion for her and said to her, “Do not weep.”” Luke 7:13

Apply: Visit or call someone who is grieving.

God's wisdom is not cold knowledge but compassion that gives life.

In Denha, God has looked favourably on his people.

Take-home resolution: Stop this week for someone in sorrow.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the day after Denha St Paul speaks of a wisdom that is not of this age. God's wisdom, secret and hidden, was decreed before the ages for our glory (1 Corinthians 2:7). The rulers of this age did not understand it; if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

This wisdom cannot be reached by cleverness. What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the human heart conceived, God has prepared for those who love him, and he has revealed it to us through the Spirit (1 Corinthians 2:9–10).

In the Gospel we see that wisdom with a human face. At the gate of Nain a widow follows the body of her only son. Nobody asks Jesus for help. He sees her, has compassion for her, and says: do not weep. He touches the bier and says: young man, I say to you, rise! And he gives him back to his mother (Luke 7:13–15).

The people cry out: a great prophet has risen among us; God has looked favourably on his people (Luke 7:16). This is the manifestation of Denha: God's wisdom is a compassion that stops at our funerals and gives life. Let us ask for it, and let us stop for those who weep. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus saw a mother crying because her son had died. He felt sorry for her and brought her son back to life! Jesus cares when we are sad. Let us care for sad people too.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, wisdom of God made visible, you stopped at the gate of Nain and gave a widow back her son. Open my eyes by your Spirit to what God has prepared for those who love him, and give me your compassion for those who weep. Amen.
