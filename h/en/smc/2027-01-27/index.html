Word for the day

“there is need of only one thing. Mary has chosen the better part, which will not be taken away from her.”” Luke 10:42

Reflection

Paul writes to Corinth with a sense of urgency: “the appointed time has grown short… the present form of this world is passing away.” Married or unmarried, buying or mourning, Christians should hold the things of this world lightly. He praises those who give themselves to God in celibacy, so that they may be “holy in body and spirit”, and says his purpose is “to promote good order and unhindered devotion to the Lord.” The Church has always honoured consecrated virginity as a sign of the Kingdom, while also honouring marriage as a true vocation and a sacrament.

In the Gospel Martha welcomes Jesus and is busy with much serving; Mary sits at his feet and listens. Martha complains, and Jesus answers gently: “Martha, Martha, you are worried and distracted by many things; there is need of only one thing. Mary has chosen the better part.” Jesus does not despise Martha's service; he corrects her anxiety. Service without listening becomes distraction. The one thing necessary is Christ himself, received in his word. Every vocation, married, consecrated or single, must keep a place at the Lord's feet.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul says: the appointed time has grown short; the present form of this world is passing away (1 Corinthians 7:29, 31). Christians hold this world's things lightly.

He praises those who give themselves wholly to the Lord, and says his aim is unhindered devotion to the Lord (1 Corinthians 7:35). The Church honours consecrated life as a sign of the Kingdom, and marriage as a holy vocation.

In the Gospel Martha is busy and Mary listens. Jesus says: Martha, Martha, you are worried and distracted by many things; there is need of only one thing (Luke 10:41–42). He corrects not her service but her anxiety.

The one thing necessary is Christ. Whatever our vocation, let us keep a place every day at his feet. Amen.

For children

Children, Mary sat and listened to Jesus. Jesus said that was the best thing to do! Let us spend a little quiet time with Jesus today.

Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, Mary chose the better part by sitting at your feet. Free us from anxiety about many things, grant us undivided devotion to you, and bless all who serve you in marriage, in consecrated life and in the single life. Amen.
