Word for the day

“But while he was still far off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion; he ran and put his arms around him and kissed him.” Luke 15:20

Reflection

Paul speaks of our body as a tent that will be folded up, and of “a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.” In this season of the Church we remember that our true home is the Father's house.

The younger son learns this the hard way. Far from home, hungry among pigs, he “came to himself” and set out for his father's house. The father was already watching the road. Every confession is this homecoming: the Church is the house where the Father runs to meet us.

Homily outline

Theme: “The Father's house.”

Paul longs for the eternal dwelling; the prodigal son returns home and is embraced.

1. An everlasting love

  • God's love for us is everlasting and faithful.
  • Even when we wander, he does not stop loving.
“I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have continued my faithfulness to you.” Jeremiah 31:3
“So he set off and went to his father. But while he was still far off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion; he ran and put his arms around him and kissed him.” Luke 15:20

Apply: Thank God today for one sign of his faithful love.

2. He came to himself

  • Hunger made the son remember his father's house.
  • Conversion is coming back to the truth about ourselves and God.
“Have I any pleasure in the death of the wicked, says the Lord GOD, and not rather that they should turn from their ways and live?” Ezekiel 18:23
“For this son of mine was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found!” And they began to celebrate.” Luke 15:24

Apply: Examine your conscience tonight.

3. Our true home

  • Our body is a tent; heaven is our house.
  • The Church is the doorway to the Father's house.
“But will God indeed dwell on the earth? Even heaven and the highest heaven cannot contain you, much less this house that I have built!” 1 Kings 8:27
“Jesus said to him, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”” John 14:6

Apply: Plan a good confession this month.

The Father is watching the road.

Let us go home.

Take-home resolution: Make a good confession before the end of the liturgical year.

Full homily

Dear friends, in the season of the Dedication of the Church we reflect on our true home. Paul says that our body is a tent that will be taken down, but we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens (2 Corinthians 5:1).

The younger son learned this the hard way. He left his father's house, wasted everything, and while feeding pigs he came to himself: I will get up and go to my father. While he was still far off, his father saw him, was filled with compassion, ran and put his arms around him and kissed him (Luke 15:17–20).

The Father whom Jesus shows us is a Father who waits and runs. However far we have wandered, the door of his house is open. This week let us return to the Father's embrace through a good confession, and share with joy in the feast of the Holy Qurbana. Amen.

For children

Children, even when we do wrong, God our Father waits for us and runs to hug us. Say sorry to someone today!

Prayer

Merciful Father, you watch the road for every child who is far away. Bring me home to your house, forgive me, and clothe me again with your love. Amen.
