Word for the day

“So Jesus said, “When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will realize that I am he, and that I do nothing on my own, but I speak these things as the Father instructed me.” John 8:28

Reflection

Denha is the season of manifestation: who is this Jesus revealed at the Jordan? Today's readings answer with the name of God. At the burning bush Moses asks God's name, and God answers: “I AM WHO I AM… Thus you shall say to the Israelites, ‘I AM has sent me to you.’” God is not one being among many; he is the One who simply is, the source of all that exists. Sirach marvels at this God: “He who lives forever created the whole universe”, and yet “the compassion of the Lord is for every living thing.”

In the Gospel Jesus takes up this name. “You are from below, I am from above… you will die in your sins unless you believe that I am he.” In Greek he says simply egō eimi, “I am”. And he adds: “When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will realize that I am he.” The full manifestation of who Jesus is will happen on the Cross. The God who said “I AM” to Moses hangs on the wood for us, and there his glory shines.

The Book of Revelation gives the same name in the language of praise: “I am the Alpha and the Omega,” says the Lord God, “who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty.” Jesus Christ is “the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead”, the one “who loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood”. In the Holy Qurbana we worship this Lord, present among us. To believe that Jesus is “I AM” is to build our lives on the only foundation that does not pass away.

Full homily

Dear brothers and sisters, in the season of Denha the Church keeps asking: who is this Jesus revealed at the Jordan? Today the readings answer with the name of God.

At the burning bush Moses asks God's name. God replies: I AM WHO I AM… Thus you shall say to the Israelites, I AM has sent me to you (Exodus 3:14). God is not one being among many; he is the One who simply is. Everything else comes and goes; he remains.

In the Gospel Jesus speaks in the same way. You are from below, I am from above. You will die in your sins unless you believe that I am he (John 8:23–24). In the Greek, Jesus simply says: I am. His hearers understood that he was speaking the name of God.

Then he says something deeper still: when you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will realize that I am he (John 8:28). We would expect God to show himself in power. Instead he shows himself on the Cross. There the I AM of the burning bush is revealed as Love that gives itself.

The Book of Revelation praises him: I am the Alpha and the Omega, who is and who was and who is to come (Revelation 1:8). And it calls him the one who loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood (Revelation 1:5).

Our world changes quickly: jobs, technology, relationships. Where do we stand? On the One who is. Let us make an act of faith each day this week: Jesus, I believe that you are the Lord, and let us look at the Cross where his name shines most brightly. Amen.

For children

Children, God told Moses his name: I AM. That means God is always there! Jesus said the same: I am. Jesus is God with us.

When you feel alone, say: Jesus, you are here. He always is!

Prayer

O Lord our God, you revealed your holy name to Moses: I AM WHO I AM. In your Son you have shown us your face. Grant that we may believe that Jesus is the Lord, lifted up on the Cross for us, the Alpha and the Omega, and so not die in our sins but live with you. To you, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, be glory, now and always and for ever. Amen.
