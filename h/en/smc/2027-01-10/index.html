Word for the day

“And the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory, the glory as of a father's only son, full of grace and truth.” John 1:14

Reflection

Denha celebrates the manifestation of the Lord, and today's Gospel puts its heart into one sentence: “And the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory.” The Greek verb means that the Word pitched his tent among us. Israel already knew a God who travels with his people. In the Book of Numbers the tribes leave Sinai “with the ark of the covenant of the LORD going before them three days' journey, to seek out a resting place for them”, and the cloud of the Lord is over them by day. Whenever the ark set out, Moses prayed: “Arise, O LORD, let your enemies be scattered.” God did not stay on the mountain; he went ahead of his people on the road.

Isaiah confesses the same mystery from the other side: “Truly, you are a God who hides himself, O God of Israel, the Savior.” The Creator whose hands stretched out the heavens is beyond our sight, and yet he saves: “Israel is saved by the LORD with everlasting salvation.” John answers this longing of every human heart: “No one has ever seen God. It is God the only Son, who is close to the Father's heart, who has made him known.” The hidden God has shown his face in Jesus. That is Denha: the God who hides himself becomes visible in a child of Nazareth, in a man baptised in the Jordan, in the Lord who walks the road with us.

The Letter to the Hebrews compares Moses and Jesus. Moses was faithful in God's house as a servant; “Christ, however, was faithful over God's house as a son, and we are his house if we hold firm the confidence and the pride that belong to hope.” John says the same: “The law indeed was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.” The law was a gift; Christ is the Giver himself. “From his fullness we have all received, grace upon grace.” In the Holy Qurbana that fullness is offered to us again: the Word who pitched his tent among us dwells on our altar and in our hearts, and we become his house, his tent among the nations.

Full homily

Dear brothers and sisters, the season of Denha keeps one question before us: how has God shown himself to us? Today's Gospel answers in a single line: the Word became flesh and lived among us, and we have seen his glory (John 1:14).

Long before Bethlehem, God was already a travelling God. In the Book of Numbers Israel leaves Sinai with the ark of the covenant going before them to seek out a resting place for them, and the cloud of the Lord over them by day (Numbers 10:33–34). Moses even begs his relative Hobab to come along: come with us, and we will treat you well. And whenever the ark set out, Moses prayed: Arise, O Lord! God did not stay on the mountain; he went ahead of his people.

Yet Isaiah also knows the pain of faith: truly, you are a God who hides himself (Isaiah 45:15). Many of us know that feeling. We pray, and God seems silent. John gives the answer: no one has ever seen God; it is God the only Son, close to the Father's heart, who has made him known (John 1:18). If you want to know what God is like, look at Jesus.

The Letter to the Hebrews tells us that Moses was faithful in God's house as a servant, but Christ is faithful over God's house as a son, and we are his house (Hebrews 3:5–6). John says: the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ (John 1:17). The law showed the way; Jesus is the Way himself.

From his fullness we have all received, grace upon grace (John 1:16). Every Holy Qurbana is that fullness given again. The Word who pitched his tent among us comes to dwell in us, and we leave the church as his house, carrying him into our streets and homes.

So this week, before you leave home each morning, pray the prayer of Moses: Arise, O Lord, go before me today. And each night count one grace you received. We are his house; let us let him live in it. Amen.

For children

Children, long ago God's people carried a special golden box called the ark, and it went in front of them on the road to show that God was with them. Then God did something even better: his Son Jesus became a baby and came to live with us!

When you walk to school, say: Jesus, walk in front of me today. He will!

Prayer

O Lord our God, you went before your people in the ark and in the cloud, and in the fullness of time your eternal Word became flesh and pitched his tent among us. Grant that we who have received from his fullness grace upon grace may hold firm our hope, become your living house, and walk with him until we see your glory face to face. To you, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, be glory, now and always and for ever. Amen.
