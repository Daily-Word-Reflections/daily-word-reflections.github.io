Readings

  • Epistle 2 Corinthians 12:1–5 · The apostle's heavenly vision
  • Gospel John 3:31–36 · The one who comes from heaven speaks of heavenly things

Word for the day

“The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands. Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life.” John 3:35–36

Reflection

Paul speaks reluctantly of being “caught up to the third heaven… into Paradise” and hearing things that cannot be told. Yet he refuses to boast except of his weaknesses. The heavenly vision does not make him proud; it makes him humble.

John's Gospel tells us why: only the one who comes from above can speak of heavenly things. “The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands.” We do not climb to heaven by our own visions; heaven came down to us in the Son. To believe in him is already to have eternal life.

Homily outline

Theme: “All things in his hands.”

Paul's vision makes him humble; Jesus, who comes from above, gives eternal life to those who believe.

1. No one sees God and lives

  • Even Moses could not see God's face.
  • The Son who comes from above makes him known.
““You cannot see my face; for no one shall see me and live.”” Exodus 33:20
“The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands.” John 3:35

Apply: Thank Jesus for revealing the Father.

2. Humility after vision

  • Paul boasts only of weakness.
  • God's gifts are not trophies but trusts.
“But I have calmed and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with its mother.” Psalm 131:2
“He must increase, but I must decrease.” John 3:30

Apply: Admit one weakness honestly to God.

3. Believe and live

  • Faith in the Son is eternal life now.
  • Refusing the Son is refusing life.
“The LORD make his face to shine upon you, and be gracious to you.” Numbers 6:25
“Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life; whoever disobeys the Son will not see life, but must endure God's wrath.” John 3:36

Apply: Make an act of faith at midday.

Heaven came down to us in the Son.

Let us place all things in his hands.

Take-home resolution: Pray “Jesus, I trust in you” whenever you are anxious this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul speaks reluctantly of being caught up to the third heaven, into Paradise, and hearing things that cannot be told (2 Corinthians 12:2–4). Yet he refuses to boast except of his weaknesses. The heavenly vision does not make him proud; it makes him humble.

John's Gospel tells us whose testimony to believe: the one who comes from above is above all; he testifies to what he has seen and heard. Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life (John 3:31–36).

We do not need to chase spiritual experiences; it is enough to believe in the Son who came from the Father's side. Let every grace we receive make us humbler. Tonight let us thank God for one grace and offer Jesus one weakness. Amen.

For children

Children, when God gives us good gifts, we should not boast but say thank you. Thank Jesus today for one gift!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you came from above to show us the Father. Into your hands the Father has placed all things; I place my life there too. Give me the faith that is eternal life. Amen.
