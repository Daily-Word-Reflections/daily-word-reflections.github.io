Word for the day

“But as for that in the good soil, these are the ones who, when they hear the word, hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit with patient endurance.” Luke 8:15

Reflection

On the third Friday of Denha the Church honours the four Evangelists: Matthew, Mark, Luke and John, through whom Christ is made known to the whole world. The readings use rich symbols. In Eden “a river flows out… and from there it divides and becomes four branches”, watering the earth: the Fathers saw in the four rivers the four Gospels flowing from Christ to every land. Ezekiel and Revelation describe four living creatures around God's throne, with the faces of a human being, a lion, an ox and an eagle; tradition linked them to Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.

The Gospel is the parable of the sower: “The seed is the word of God.” Some falls on the path, some on rock, some among thorns. “But as for that in the good soil, these are the ones who, when they hear the word, hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit with patient endurance.” The Evangelists wrote so that the seed could reach us. The question for us is the soil: do we let the Gospel take root, or do worries and pleasures choke it?

Full homily

Dear friends, on the third Friday of Denha we honour the four Evangelists. In Eden a river flows out to water the garden, and from there it divides and becomes four branches (Genesis 2:10). The Fathers saw here the four Gospels flowing from Christ to water the whole earth.

Ezekiel and Revelation describe four living creatures around God's throne: a human face, a lion, an ox and an eagle. Tradition gave them to Matthew, Mark, Luke and John: four faces, one Christ.

The Gospel is the parable of the sower. The seed is the word of God. Some falls on the path, some on rock, some among thorns. But as for that in the good soil, these are the ones who hold it fast in an honest and good heart, and bear fruit with patient endurance (Luke 8:15).

The Evangelists have done their part: the seed has reached us. Now the question is our soil. Let us read a Gospel this month, and remove the thorns that choke the word. Amen.

For children

Children, Matthew, Mark, Luke and John wrote down the story of Jesus for us! Let us read a little of the Gospel every day so that God's word grows in our hearts.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, through the four Evangelists your Gospel has flowed like rivers to the ends of the earth. Make my heart good soil, honest and patient, so that your word may bear fruit in me. Amen.
