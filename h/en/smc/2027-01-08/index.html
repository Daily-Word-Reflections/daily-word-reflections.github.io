Word for the day

“Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you, says the LORD.” Jeremiah 1:8

Reflection

On the Fridays of Denha the Church honours the great witnesses who made Christ known. The first is John the Baptist, who saw the heavens open over Jesus at the Jordan. Today's readings form a gallery of prophetic courage. Nathan goes to King David after his adultery and murder and, through a simple story, leads him to condemn himself; then he says: “You are the man!” Jeremiah, young and afraid, is told: “Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you.”

John paid the price of this courage. He told Herod plainly, “It is not lawful for you to have her.” Herod imprisoned him, and at a drunken birthday banquet, to keep a foolish oath, had him beheaded. In Acts, King Herod Agrippa kills James with the sword and arrests Peter; “the church prayed fervently to God for him”, and an angel leads Peter out. Some witnesses are freed, some are killed; all are held by God. In a world where power often silences conscience, the Baptist's head on a platter is a question to every Christian: will I speak the truth with love, whatever it costs?

Homily outline

Theme: “Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you.”

The Baptist and the prophets: courage for the truth, held by God.

1. You are the man

  • Nathan's parable.
  • David condemns himself.
  • Correction leads to repentance.
“Nathan said to David, “You are the man! Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel: I anointed you king over Israel, and I rescued you from the hand of Saul;” 2 Samuel 12:7
“For Herod had arrested John, bound him, and put him in prison on account of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife, because John had been telling him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”” Matthew 14:3–4

Apply: Accept one correction this week without defending yourself.

2. Called before birth

  • Jeremiah consecrated in the womb.
  • John filled with the Spirit before birth.
  • God's call is older than our fears.
“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you were born I consecrated you; I appointed you a prophet to the nations.” Jeremiah 1:5
“for he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink; even before his birth he will be filled with the Holy Spirit.” Luke 1:15

Apply: Pray for unborn children and for the vocations God has prepared.

3. The Church prays; God delivers

  • James killed, Peter imprisoned.
  • The Church prays fervently.
  • God holds all his witnesses.
“They will fight against you; but they shall not prevail against you, for I am with you, says the LORD, to deliver you.” Jeremiah 1:19
“While Peter was kept in prison, the church prayed fervently to God for him.” Acts 12:5

Apply: Pray daily this week for the persecuted Church.

The Baptist's courage still speaks.

Truth with love, held by God's promise: I am with you.

Take-home resolution: Speak one hard truth this week, gently and with love.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the Fridays of Denha the Church remembers the witnesses of Christ. Today we honour John the Baptist, who saw the heavens open at the Jordan. The readings show us what a prophet looks like.

Nathan goes to King David after his sin with Bathsheba and the murder of Uriah. He tells a story of a rich man who stole a poor man's only lamb. David is furious. Then Nathan says: you are the man! (2 Samuel 12:7). Courage and wisdom bring the king to repentance.

Jeremiah is young and afraid, but God says: do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you (Jeremiah 1:8). John the Baptist was consecrated even before birth, leaping in his mother's womb before Jesus.

John told Herod: it is not lawful for you to have her (Matthew 14:4). Herod imprisoned him and, at a drunken party, to keep a foolish promise, had him beheaded. Truth spoken with love can be costly.

In Acts, James is killed and Peter imprisoned. But the church prayed fervently to God for him (Acts 12:5), and an angel set him free. Some witnesses are freed, some die, but all are held by God. Let us pray for courage to speak the truth with love, and let us pray fervently for the persecuted Church. Amen.

For children

Children, John the Baptist was very brave. He told the king the truth even when it was dangerous. Let us always tell the truth, kindly and bravely.

Prayer

O God, you sent John the Baptist to prepare the way of your Son and to bear witness to the truth even to death. Give us courage like Nathan, Jeremiah and John, to speak the truth with love, and keep your persecuted Church in your hand. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
