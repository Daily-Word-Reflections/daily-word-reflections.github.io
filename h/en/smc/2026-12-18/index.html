Reflection

Today the Syro-Malabar calendar commemorates the Miraculous Cross at Mylapore. The ancient stone cross with a Pahlavi inscription, found on St Thomas Mount near Chennai in the sixteenth century and venerated in connection with the apostle Thomas, is one of the oldest Christian monuments in India. Tradition records that it showed miraculous signs during the Holy Qurbana. Whatever one makes of the reports, the cross itself witnesses to the antiquity of the faith of the St Thomas Christians, who honoured the Cross without a corpus as the sign of victory and life.

The readings unfold the meaning of the Cross. Moses strikes the rock and water flows; the Fathers saw this fulfilled when the soldier's lance opened Christ's side. Peter proclaims: “God has made him both Lord and Messiah, this Jesus whom you crucified.” Paul says Christ reconciled Jews and Gentiles “to God in one body through the cross.” And Jesus, about to go to his Passion, tells Thomas: “I am the way.” The Way passes through the Cross.

Full homily

Dear friends, today our Church remembers the Miraculous Cross at Mylapore, the ancient stone cross connected with the memory of the apostle Thomas. It reminds us that our faith in India is very old, and that it is a faith in the Cross of Christ.

In the desert, when the people were dying of thirst, Moses struck the rock and water came out (Exodus 17:6). The Fathers saw in this rock the pierced side of Christ, from which flow the sacraments.

St Paul writes that Christ is our peace: through the Cross He broke down the dividing wall of hostility and made us one people (Ephesians 2:14–16). And in the Gospel Jesus tells Thomas: I am the way, and the truth, and the life (John 14:6).

Let us venerate the Cross with love today. Let us ask the Crucified One to break down walls of hostility in our families and our Church, and to keep us on the Way that Thomas preached. Amen.

For children

Children, the cross shows us how much Jesus loves us. Make the sign of the cross slowly and with love today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you made peace through your Cross and opened the way to the Father. As our fathers in faith honoured your Cross, let us glory in it and walk your way to life. Amen.
