Word for the day

“But blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear. Truly I tell you, many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see, but did not see it.” Matthew 13:16–17

Reflection

“If you have been raised with Christ, seek the things that are above… Set your minds on things that are above.” Paul asks the baptised to put to death what is earthly and to “clothe yourselves with the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its creator.”

Jesus tells his disciples that they are blessed: prophets and righteous people longed to see what they see. In the Annunciation season we stand where the prophets longed to stand, at the threshold of the Incarnation. On this First Friday we look at the Heart of the One the prophets longed to see.

Full homily

Dear friends, on this First Friday of December St Paul lifts our eyes: if you have been raised with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, seated at the right hand of God. Set your minds on things that are above, not on things that are on earth (Colossians 3:1–2).

This does not mean despising the world. It means ordering our lives. Our houses, jobs, savings and plans are good, but they are not our final home. A person who knows heaven is his home lives differently on earth: more generously, more peacefully, less anxiously.

Then Paul becomes very concrete. Put to death what is earthly in you: sexual immorality, impurity, evil desire and greed. Get rid of anger, wrath, malice, slander and abusive language. Do not lie to one another (Colossians 3:5–9). It is like taking off old, dirty clothes.

And put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge according to the image of its creator (Colossians 3:10). In the old Syriac liturgy, the newly baptised were clothed in white. The Christmas fast is a time to wash those white garments again in confession.

In the Gospel Jesus says to his disciples: blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear; many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see, but did not see it (Matthew 13:16–17). We have what the prophets only dreamed of: the Gospel, the sacraments, the Holy Qurbana, the Church.

Yet we can have eyes and not see. We can hear the Gospel every Sunday and not let it touch us. The First Friday devotion to the Sacred Heart is an invitation to let Christ's love open our eyes.

Today, if possible, make a good confession and receive Holy Communion in honour of the Sacred Heart. Take off one old garment of sin, and ask Jesus to give you eyes that truly see and ears that truly hear. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: happy are your eyes, because you can know me! Thank Jesus today that you know Him.

Prayer

Sacred Heart of Jesus, the prophets longed to see you; I am blessed to know you. Lift my mind to the things above and renew me in your image. Amen.
