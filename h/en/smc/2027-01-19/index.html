Word for the day

“The next day he saw Jesus coming toward him and declared, “Here is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” John 1:29

Reflection

On the second day of the fast the readings teach us to confess. Solomon, dedicating the Temple, prays that when the people sin and then turn, “acknowledge your name, pray and plead with you in this house”, God will “hear from heaven, and forgive.” Baruch puts words of confession on the lips of exiles: “The Lord our God is in the right, but there is open shame on us today.” True confession does not blame God or others; it says: we have sinned.

James gives the Church's practice: “Are any among you sick? They should call for the elders of the church… Therefore confess your sins to one another, and pray for one another, so that you may be healed.” Here the Church has always seen the sacraments of Anointing and Reconciliation. And the Baptist shows us where sin is taken away: “Here is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” Our fasting is not what removes sin; the Lamb does. The fast prepares us to bring our sins to him in confession.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the second day of our fast the readings teach us how to confess. Solomon prays at the dedication of the Temple: when your people sin and then turn, acknowledge your name and pray, then hear from heaven, and forgive (2 Chronicles 6:24–27).

Baruch gives words of confession to the exiles: the Lord our God is in the right, but there is open shame on us today (Baruch 1:15). True confession does not blame God or others. It simply says: I have sinned.

James gives the Church's practice: are any among you sick? They should call for the elders of the church. Confess your sins to one another, and pray for one another, so that you may be healed (James 5:14, 16). Here the Church has always seen the sacraments of Anointing and Reconciliation.

John the Baptist points to the One who takes sin away: here is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world! (John 1:29). Our fasting does not remove sin; the Lamb does. Let the fast lead us to the confessional and to the Lamb. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus is the Lamb of God who takes away our sins. When we say sorry in confession, he makes our hearts clean and new!

Prayer

Lamb of God, who take away the sin of the world, have mercy on us. On this second day of the fast, give us the humility to confess our sins and the joy of your forgiveness. Hear us from heaven and heal us. Amen.
