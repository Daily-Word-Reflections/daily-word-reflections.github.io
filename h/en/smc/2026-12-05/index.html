Word for the day

“Jesus said to him, “No one who puts a hand to the plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God.”” Luke 9:62

Reflection

Paul asks the Galatians: did you receive the Spirit by works of the law or by believing what you heard? “Those who believe are the descendants of Abraham.” Faith is the open hand that receives God's gift.

On the road to Jerusalem three would-be followers meet Jesus. To one he says, “The Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head”; to another, “Let the dead bury their own dead”; to the third, “No one who puts a hand to the plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God.” Elisha burned his plough to follow Elijah. Discipleship is wholehearted; the Lord who comes at Christmas asks for an undivided heart.

Homily outline

Theme: “Hand on the plough.”

Faith receives the Spirit; discipleship does not look back.

1. Children of Abraham by faith

  • Abraham believed and was reckoned righteous.
  • Believers share his blessing.
“And he believed the LORD; and the LORD reckoned it to him as righteousness.” Genesis 15:6
“Jesus said to him, “Foxes have holes, and birds of the air have nests; but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head.”” Luke 9:58

Apply: Make an act of faith today.

2. Elisha burned his plough

  • Elisha left everything to follow Elijah.
  • A real decision has no way back.
“He returned from following him, took the yoke of oxen, and slaughtered them; using the equipment from the oxen, he boiled their flesh, and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he set out and followed Elijah, and became his servant.” 1 Kings 19:21
“Jesus said to him, “No one who puts a hand to the plow and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God.”” Luke 9:62

Apply: Give up one thing that pulls you back.

3. Follow without delay

  • Excuses delay discipleship.
  • Mary said yes at once.
“Is anything too wonderful for the LORD?” Genesis 18:14
“Then he said to them all, “If any want to become my followers, let them deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me.”” Luke 9:23

Apply: Pray the Rosary with Mary today.

The Lord who comes asks for an undivided heart.

Let us follow without looking back.

Take-home resolution: Renew your commitment to Christ at the Qurbana tomorrow.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul asks the Galatians: did you receive the Spirit by doing the works of the law, or by believing what you heard? And he reminds them that those who believe are the children of Abraham (Galatians 3:2, 7). Faith is the open hand that receives God's gift.

In the Gospel three people want to follow Jesus. To one He says: foxes have holes, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay His head. To another who wants to bury his father first: let the dead bury their own dead. To a third who wants to say goodbye: no one who puts a hand to the plough and looks back is fit for the kingdom of God (Luke 9:57–62).

These are hard words. Jesus is not against family; He is against delay. When God calls, the answer must not be someday.

What is God asking you to do that you keep postponing? Reconciliation, confession, prayer, a change of habit? During this fast, put your hand to the plough and do not look back. Amen.

For children

Children, a farmer who looks back cannot plough a straight line. When Jesus asks you to do something good, do it right away!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you had nowhere to lay your head. Give me a faith that follows you without delay and without looking back, like Mary your mother. Amen.
