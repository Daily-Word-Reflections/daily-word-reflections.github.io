Word for the day

“And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.” Matthew 16:18

Reflection

On the Fridays of Denha the Church honours the great witnesses who made Christ known. Today we keep the memory of Peter and Paul. At Caesarea Philippi Jesus asks: “Who do people say that the Son of Man is?” The answers are generous but too small: John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, one of the prophets. Then comes the question no one else can answer for us: “But who do you say that I am?” Simon answers: “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.” This is a moment of Denha, for Jesus says: “flesh and blood has not revealed this to you, but my Father in heaven.” On this faith, and on the man who confessed it, Christ builds: “you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.” He gives him the keys of the kingdom and the power to bind and to loose. The ministry of Peter continues in the Pope, the Successor of Peter, who strengthens his brothers and sisters in this same confession.

The other readings show the apostles carrying the life of Christ into the world. In Joppa the disciple Tabitha, who “was devoted to good works and acts of charity”, falls ill and dies, and the widows show Peter the tunics she had made for them. Peter puts everyone outside, kneels and prays, and says: “Tabitha, get up.” She opens her eyes. Long before, Elisha had raised the Shunammite woman's son after praying behind a closed door; now Peter raises a daughter of the Church in the name of the risen Jesus, and “many believed in the Lord”. Paul, for his part, lists what his apostleship cost him: “Three times I was beaten with rods. Once I received a stoning. Three times I was shipwrecked”, and on top of all this, “my anxiety for all the churches”. He boasts only of his weakness. Peter the rock and Paul the weak one, both martyred in Rome: through them the same Lord gives life and builds his Church.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the Fridays of Denha the Church honours the great witnesses of Christ. Today: Peter and Paul, the pillars of the Church.

At Caesarea Philippi Jesus asks: but who do you say that I am? Simon Peter answers: you are the Messiah, the Son of the living God (Matthew 16:15–16). Jesus says that this did not come from flesh and blood but from his Father in heaven. And he adds: you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church (Matthew 16:18).

Peter carries that faith into the world. In Joppa he kneels beside the body of Tabitha, a woman full of good works, and says: Tabitha, get up. And she opens her eyes (Acts 9:40). Like Elisha long before him, but in the name of the risen Jesus, Peter brings life.

Paul tells us what it cost him: beatings, a stoning, shipwrecks, hunger, cold, and the daily anxiety for all the churches. And he says: if I must boast, I will boast of the things that show my weakness (2 Corinthians 11:30).

Both gave their lives in Rome. Let us pray today for the Pope, Peter's successor, and for our bishops. And let us answer Jesus' question from our hearts: you are the Christ, the Son of the living God. Amen.

For children

Prayer

Lord Jesus, Messiah and Son of the living God, you built your Church on the rock of Peter and sent Paul to the nations through many sufferings. Through their prayers keep me firm in confessing you, generous in good works like Tabitha, and faithful in my weakness. Bless our Holy Father the Pope and all our shepherds. Amen.
