Word for the day

“Then from the cloud came a voice that said, “This is my Son, my Chosen; listen to him!”” Luke 9:35

Reflection

Peter, near the end of his life, remembers the mountain: “we had been eyewitnesses of his majesty… We ourselves heard this voice come from heaven.” The faith of the Church rests on eyewitnesses and on the prophetic word, “a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns.”

On the mountain Jesus prays and is transfigured; Moses and Elijah speak with him of his departure in Jerusalem. The glory prepares the disciples for the Cross. The Church, in her last season, looks toward the same glory: the day when the Morning Star rises in our hearts.

Homily outline

Theme: “Listen to him.”

Peter's eyewitness memory of the Transfiguration.

  • Moses' face shone after speaking with God.
  • Jesus' face changes as he prays.

  • The psalmist longs to see God's face.
  • In Jesus the face of God shines on us.
““Come,” my heart says, “seek his face!” Your face, LORD, do I seek.” Psalm 27:8
“Then from the cloud came a voice that said, “This is my Son, my Chosen; listen to him!”” Luke 9:35

Apply: Read the Gospel slowly and keep one phrase.

3. Light for the dark place

  • The prophetic word is a lamp until the day dawns.
  • The Church waits for the Morning Star.
“The LORD make his face to shine upon you, and be gracious to you.” Numbers 6:25
“Jesus said to him, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”” John 14:6

Apply: Bless your family with Numbers 6:24–26 tonight.

The glory of the mountain is the promise of our future.

Take-home resolution: Read one Gospel passage daily this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, near the end of his life Peter remembers the mountain: we had been eyewitnesses of his majesty; we ourselves heard this voice come from heaven while we were with him on the holy mountain (2 Peter 1:16–18). The faith of the Church rests on eyewitnesses and on the prophetic word.

That word is like a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts (2 Peter 1:19). On the mountain of the Transfiguration Jesus' face shone, and a voice came from the cloud: this is my Son, my Chosen; listen to him (Luke 9:35).

In the dark places of life we have God's word as our lamp. The Father's command, listen to him, is for us too. This week let us read the Gospel for ten minutes each day and quietly contemplate the face of Jesus. Amen.

For children

Children, on the mountain Jesus' face shone like the sun. God said: listen to him. Read one word of Jesus in the Bible today!

Prayer
