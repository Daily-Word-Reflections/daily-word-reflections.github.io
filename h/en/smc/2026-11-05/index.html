First Thursday of the Dedication of the Church · Sts Zechariah and Elizabeth

5 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Ephesians 3:1–6 · The Gentiles are fellow heirs of the promise
  • Gospel Matthew 8:5–13 · The healing of the centurion's servant

Word for the day

“Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof; but only speak the word, and my servant will be healed.” Matthew 8:8

Reflection

A Roman centurion, a Gentile and an officer of the occupying army, comes to Jesus for his servant. His humility and faith astonish Jesus: “In no one in Israel have I found such faith.” Then Jesus opens the horizon: “Many will come from east and west and will eat with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.”

Paul calls this the mystery hidden for ages and now revealed: the Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the same body. We Indians are among those who came “from the east”. The centurion's words have become the prayer of the Church before Communion: Lord, I am not worthy… but only say the word.

Homily outline

Theme: “Only say the word.”

A Gentile centurion's faith opens the vision of all nations at the banquet of the Kingdom.

1. Faith that astonishes Jesus

  • The centurion believes that Jesus' word is enough.
  • Humility is the doorway of faith.
“For I desire steadfast love and not sacrifice, the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.” Hosea 6:6
“The centurion answered, “Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof; but only speak the word, and my servant will be healed.”” Matthew 8:8

Apply: Say the centurion's words slowly before Communion.

2. From east and west

  • The prophets foresaw all nations streaming to God's house.
  • We Indians are among those who came from the east.
“In days to come the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established as the highest of the mountains, and shall be raised above the hills; all the nations shall stream to it.” Isaiah 2:2
“I tell you, many will come from east and west and will eat with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.” Matthew 8:11

Apply: Pray for missionaries from Kerala serving abroad.

3. A house of prayer for all

  • God's house is open to all peoples.
  • Zechariah and Elizabeth kept praying through long waiting.
“For my house shall be called a house of prayer for all peoples.” Isaiah 56:7
“When Jesus heard him, he was amazed and said to those who followed him, “Truly I tell you, in no one in Israel have I found such faith.”” Matthew 8:10

Apply: Pray for one sick person you know.

One word of Jesus is enough.

Let us come to him with humble faith.

Take-home resolution: Pray for the sick of your parish this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, a Roman centurion, a Gentile and an officer of the occupying army, comes to Jesus for his servant. His humility and faith, Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof; only speak the word, astonish Jesus: in no one in Israel have I found such faith (Matthew 8:8–10). Many will come from east and west and eat with Abraham in the kingdom of heaven.

Paul says the mystery revealed to him is that the Gentiles have become fellow heirs, members of the same body and sharers in the promise in Christ Jesus (Ephesians 3:6). The door of God's house is open to every nation.

Today we remember Sts Zechariah and Elizabeth. They were righteous before God; they waited many years and kept praying, and God gave them John the Baptist. Let us pray with the humility of the centurion and the perseverance of this holy couple. Before every Holy Qurbana, let us repeat the centurion's words in our hearts. Amen.

For children

Children, Zechariah and Elizabeth prayed for many years, and God gave them John. Don't stop praying even when the answer is slow!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof; but only say the word, and my soul shall be healed. Gather all nations into your Kingdom. Amen.
