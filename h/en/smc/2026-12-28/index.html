Word for the day

“A voice was heard in Ramah, wailing and loud lamentation, Rachel weeping for her children; she refused to be consoled, because they are no more.” Matthew 2:18

Reflection

The joy of Christmas is followed by the tears of Bethlehem. Herod, furious at being tricked by the Magi, kills all the boys of Bethlehem two years old and under. Matthew hears in the mothers' cry the voice of Rachel weeping in Ramah. From Abel onward, innocent blood has cried out to God; the Holy Innocents are the first to die in place of Christ.

The Church honours them as martyrs who witnessed to Christ not by speaking but by dying. On this day Kerala's Church celebrates Holy Childhood Day: children praying and sharing for other children. We also remember all innocent children who suffer today: the unborn, abused, trafficked, hungry and war-torn. Jeremiah's promise stands: “there is hope for your future.”

Theme: “Rachel weeping for her children.”

The Holy Innocents and Holy Childhood Day.

1. Innocent blood cries out

  • Abel's blood cries from the ground.
  • God is never deaf to the innocent.
“And the LORD said, “What have you done? Listen; your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground!”” Genesis 4:10
“When Herod saw that he had been tricked by the wise men, he was infuriated, and he sent and killed all the children in and around Bethlehem who were two years old or under.” Matthew 2:16

Apply: Pray for children who suffer violence.

2. Rachel weeping

  • Jeremiah's lament becomes the cry of Bethlehem.
  • God promises a future of hope.
“Thus says the LORD: Keep your voice from weeping, and your eyes from tears; for there is a reward for your work, says the LORD.” Jeremiah 31:16
“A voice was heard in Ramah, wailing and loud lamentation, Rachel weeping for her children; she refused to be consoled, because they are no more.” Matthew 2:18

Apply: Console a mother who has lost a child.

3. Treasure in heaven

  • The Innocents' treasure is with God.
  • Children who share store up treasure in heaven.
“But the righteous live forever, and their reward is with the Lord; the Most High takes care of them.” Wisdom 5:15
“But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust consumes and where thieves do not break in and steal. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.” Matthew 6:20–21

Apply: Help children share a gift with poor children.

Full homily

Dear friends, today our Church keeps Holy Childhood Day, remembering the Holy Innocents. The joy of Christmas is followed by tears in Bethlehem: Herod kills the little boys, and Rachel weeps for her children (Matthew 2:16–18).

From Abel onward, innocent blood has cried out to God (Genesis 4:10). The Letter to the Hebrews remembers those who suffered for their faith, of whom the world was not worthy (Hebrews 11:38). God hears every innocent cry.

Today let us think of children who suffer: the unborn, the abused, the hungry, child workers, refugee children. Jesus Himself became a refugee child, fleeing to Egypt.

On Holy Childhood Day, children help children: through prayer and small offerings, they support missions for poor children around the world. Parents, encourage your children in this. And let us all protect the little ones God entrusts to us. Amen.

For children

Children, today is your special day! Pray for children who are sad, hungry or in danger, and share something to help them.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, the Holy Innocents died in your place. Protect all children today, the unborn and the suffering, and console every weeping mother. Make our children generous for other children. Amen.
