Word for the day

“Therefore, I tell you, her sins, which were many, have been forgiven; hence she has shown great love. But the one to whom little is forgiven, loves little.”” Luke 7:47

Reflection

On the last day of the fast, the readings speak of love. Jacob serves seven years for Rachel, “and they seemed to him but a few days because of the love he had for her.” Hosea, after many warnings, ends with God's tender promise: “Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God… I will heal their disloyalty; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned from them.” The goal of all fasting is not hunger but love restored.

In the Gospel a woman known as a sinner enters Simon the Pharisee's house, weeps at Jesus' feet, dries them with her hair and anoints them. Simon judges her; Jesus defends her: “her sins, which were many, have been forgiven; hence she has shown great love.” Peter's letter says: “Above all, maintain constant love for one another, for love covers a multitude of sins.” Today we also honour St Sebastian, much loved in Kerala, and pray for our bishops who bear his name. He loved Christ more than life; the forgiven woman loved Christ more than her reputation. Let our fast end in that kind of love.

Full homily

Dear friends, on the last day of our fast the readings are full of love. Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed to him but a few days because of the love he had for her (Genesis 29:20). Love makes sacrifice light.

Hosea ends his book of warnings with tenderness: return, O Israel, to the LORD your God. I will heal their disloyalty; I will love them freely (Hosea 14:1, 4). The goal of the fast is not an empty stomach but a heart filled with God's love.

In the Gospel a woman known as a sinner weeps at Jesus' feet in Simon's house. Simon judges her. Jesus says: her sins, which were many, have been forgiven; hence she has shown great love (Luke 7:47). Her love is the sign of her forgiveness.

Peter says: above all, maintain constant love for one another, for love covers a multitude of sins (1 Peter 4:8). Today we honour St Sebastian and pray for our bishops who bear his name. Let us end this fast as the woman did: at Jesus' feet, forgiven, and loving much. Amen.

For children

Children, a woman was very sorry for her sins and cried at Jesus' feet. Jesus forgave her, and she loved him so much! Let us love Jesus and our families today.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you forgave the woman who loved much and defended her before her accusers. As our fast ends, heal our disloyalty, fill us with love that covers many sins, and through the prayers of St Sebastian make us brave in faith. Amen.
