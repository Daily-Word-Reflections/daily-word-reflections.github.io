Word for the day

“But you, O Bethlehem of Ephrathah, who are one of the little clans of Judah, from you shall come forth for me one who is to rule in Israel, whose origin is from of old, from ancient days.” Micah 5:2

Reflection

On Christmas Eve the Church looks to Bethlehem, “one of the little clans of Judah”. God chooses the small: a little town, a poor couple, a manger. Bethlehem means “house of bread”; there the Bread of Life is born.

Tonight families gather, lamps and stars are lit, and the parish celebrates the night Qurbana. Let the noise of preparations give way, at least for a few moments, to silent adoration. The One whose origin is from ancient days comes as a baby who can be held.

Homily outline

Theme: “O little Bethlehem.”

Christmas Eve: God chooses the small. (The day's official readings are pending.)

1. Little among the clans

  • Micah foretold the ruler from Bethlehem.
  • God chooses what is small.
“But you, O Bethlehem of Ephrathah, who are one of the little clans of Judah, from you shall come forth for me one who is to rule in Israel, whose origin is from of old, from ancient days.” Micah 5:2
“And she gave birth to her firstborn son and wrapped him in bands of cloth, and laid him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the inn.” Luke 2:7

Apply: Spend five minutes in silence before the crib.

2. He shall be the one of peace

  • The ruler from Bethlehem is a shepherd.
  • He is our peace.
“And he shall stand and feed his flock in the strength of the LORD… and he shall be the one of peace.” Micah 5:4–5
“I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.” John 10:11

Apply: Call someone who will be alone this Christmas.

3. House of bread

  • Bethlehem means house of bread.
  • The Bread of Life is born for us.
“O taste and see that the LORD is good; happy are those who take refuge in him.” Psalm 34:8
“Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will never be hungry.”” John 6:35

Apply: Receive Communion at the Christmas Qurbana with deep faith.

God comes in the small and the poor.

Let us adore him in silence.

Take-home resolution: Make a good Christmas Communion.

Full homily

Dear friends, on Christmas Eve the Church looks to Bethlehem. Micah says: you, Bethlehem, one of the little clans of Judah, from you shall come forth one who is to rule in Israel (Micah 5:2). God chooses the small.

A little town, a poor couple, a manger, shepherds in the fields: God's greatest work is done in the smallest places. No one is too small or too poor for Christ to come to them.

Bethlehem means house of bread. There the Bread of Life is born. Tonight, in the Holy Qurbana of the Nativity, He comes again under the appearance of bread.

Tonight families gather, stars are lit, carols are sung. Let us also keep a moment of silence before the crib, to adore the Child. Let us forgive one another, and welcome Him with simple hearts. A blessed Christmas to you all. Amen.

For children

Children, tonight baby Jesus is born! Kneel at the crib and tell Him: Jesus, I love you.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, born in Bethlehem, the house of bread, come into our homes tonight. Let us adore you in silence and share your love with the lonely. Amen.
