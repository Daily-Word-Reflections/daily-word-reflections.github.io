First Wednesday of the Dedication of the Church

4 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle 1 Peter 5:1–4 · The chief Shepherd will give the crown of glory
  • Gospel John 21:15–19 · Feed my sheep

Word for the day

“Simon son of John, do you love me more than these?… Feed my lambs.” John 21:15

Reflection

The Church is a flock with a Shepherd. Peter, who once denied the Lord, is asked three times, “Do you love me?” and three times receives the charge: “Feed my lambs… tend my sheep.” Authority in the Church is born of love and exists for service.

Years later Peter writes to the elders: “Tend the flock of God that is in your charge… not for sordid gain but eagerly… be examples to the flock.” He had learned shepherding from the Shepherd. Today pray for your bishop and parish priest, and ask how you too shepherd those entrusted to you.

Homily outline

Theme: “Love first, then feed.”

Peter's threefold confession of love becomes a threefold mission.

1. The Lord seeks his sheep

  • God himself promised to search for his flock.
  • Jesus fulfils it as the Good Shepherd.
“For thus says the Lord GOD: I myself will search for my sheep, and will seek them out.” Ezekiel 34:11
“When they had finished breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “Simon son of John, do you love me more than these?” He said to him, “Yes, Lord; you know that I love you.” Jesus said to him, “Feed my lambs.”” John 21:15

Apply: Pray for someone who has drifted from the Church.

2. Shepherds after God's heart

  • God promised shepherds who would feed with knowledge.
  • Pastors serve, not dominate.
“I will give you shepherds after my own heart, who will feed you with knowledge and understanding.” Jeremiah 3:15
“Keep watch over yourselves and over all the flock, of which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to shepherd the church of God that he obtained with the blood of his own Son.” Acts 20:28

Apply: Pray for your bishop and parish priest by name.

3. Examples to the flock

  • Every Christian shepherds someone.
  • We lead best by example.
“He has told you, O mortal, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?” Micah 6:8
“Jesus said to them again, “Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, so I send you.”” John 20:21

Apply: Speak to a younger person about your faith this week.

Love for Christ is the root of all service.

Let us love him, and feed his sheep.

Take-home resolution: Offer a decade of the Rosary for priests each day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, the Church is a flock with a Shepherd. Peter, who once denied the Lord, is asked three times: do you love me? And three times he receives the charge: feed my lambs, tend my sheep (John 21:15–17). Authority in the Church is born of love and exists for service.

Years later Peter himself tells the elders of the Church: tend the flock of God, not under compulsion but willingly, not lording it over those in your charge but being examples to the flock (1 Peter 5:2–3). And when the chief Shepherd appears, they will receive the crown of glory that never fades.

Let us pray today especially for our priests and bishops. And each of us is a shepherd in some way: parents, teachers, catechists. Jesus asks us too: do you love me? Let us answer with our service. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus asked Peter: do you love me? Today you too can say: Jesus, I love you!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, Good Shepherd, give your Church shepherds after your own heart. Teach me to love you first and to serve those you entrust to me. Amen.
