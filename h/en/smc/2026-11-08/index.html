Word for the day

“And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or fields, for my name's sake, will receive a hundredfold, and will inherit eternal life.” Matthew 19:29

Reflection

Today's readings speak of inheritance. In Egypt, crushed by slavery, Israel hears God's promise: “I will take you as my people, and I will be your God… I will bring you into the land… I will give it to you for a possession.” The promised land was never only a territory; it was life with God.

The Servant in Isaiah feels he has laboured in vain, “yet surely my cause is with the LORD, and my reward with my God.” Peter writes to scattered Christians that they have been born anew “into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you.” The Church is the people already on the way to that inheritance.

In the Gospel the disciples are shocked: if the rich can hardly enter the Kingdom, who can be saved? Jesus answers, “For mortals it is impossible, but for God all things are possible.” Then Peter asks what they will receive for leaving everything. Jesus promises thrones, a hundredfold, and eternal life. The apostles, on whom the Church is founded, will share in Christ's judgement and glory.

For Kerala Christians, whose families have often been measured by land and property, this Gospel is searching. Our deepest inheritance is not the family property but the faith received from the apostle Thomas and handed down for centuries. What we leave for Christ is never lost; it returns a hundredfold.

Homily outline

Theme: “The inheritance that does not fade.”

From the promised land to the heavenly inheritance: God gives himself to his people, and what we leave for Christ returns a hundredfold.

1. God promises an inheritance

  • In Egypt God promises freedom, a people and a land.
  • The greatest gift is God himself: “I will be your God.”
  • The Church is the people on the way to the inheritance.
“I will take you as my people, and I will be your God. You shall know that I am the LORD your God, who has freed you from the burdens of the Egyptians.” Exodus 6:7
“And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or fields, for my name's sake, will receive a hundredfold, and will inherit eternal life.” Matthew 19:29

Apply: Thank God for the freedom he has given you in Christ.

2. The Lord is my portion

  • The Servant's reward is with God, even when his labour seems vain.
  • Peter: an inheritance imperishable, undefiled, unfading.
  • Faith, not property, is the family's true wealth.
“The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup; you hold my lot. The boundary lines have fallen for me in pleasant places; I have a goodly heritage.” Psalm 16:5–6
“By his great mercy he has given us a new birth into a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, and into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you.” 1 Peter 1:3–4

Apply: Tell your children how faith came to your family.

3. Impossible for us, possible for God

  • Riches cannot open the door of the Kingdom.
  • Salvation is God's work; we receive it with open hands.
  • The apostles who left everything will share Christ's glory.
“But I said, “I have labored in vain, I have spent my strength for nothing and vanity; yet surely my cause is with the LORD, and my reward with my God.”” Isaiah 49:4
“But Jesus looked at them and said, “For mortals it is impossible, but for God all things are possible.”” Matthew 19:26

Apply: Give something valuable to someone in need this week.

What we keep we lose; what we give to Christ we keep forever.

Let us leave our children the inheritance of faith.

Take-home resolution: Read 1 Peter 1:3–9 as a family this week.

Full homily

Dear brothers and sisters, in Kerala we know a lot about inheritance: land, houses, gold, and sometimes long family disputes over them. Today God speaks to us about a different inheritance.

In Egypt the Israelites were slaves. God said to Moses: “I will take you as my people, and I will be your God. I will bring you into the land I promised.” The land was a gift, but the greater gift was God himself: “I will be your God.”

St Peter writes to Christians who had lost homes and property: God has given you new birth “into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you.” No one can steal it, divide it, or take it to court.

In the Gospel Jesus says it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the Kingdom. The disciples are shocked. Jesus says, “For mortals it is impossible, but for God all things are possible.” And to those who leave everything for him he promises a hundredfold and eternal life.

So let us ask ourselves today: what am I leaving to my children? If we leave them land without faith, we leave them poor. If we leave them faith, we leave them an inheritance that will never fade. Amen.

For children

Children, do you know what an inheritance is? It's something precious your parents and grandparents give you.

Jesus said the most precious gift is not money or land, but God himself and heaven, which no one can take away.

Let's thank God for our faith, the best gift from our families. Say together: “Jesus, you are my treasure!”

Prayer

Father of mercy, you have given us new birth into a living hope and an inheritance kept in heaven. Free us from all that holds us back from following your Son, and make us faithful to the faith we have received, for all things are possible with you. Amen.
