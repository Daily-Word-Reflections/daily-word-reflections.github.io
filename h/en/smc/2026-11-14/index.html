Second Saturday of the Dedication of the Church

14 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Revelation 22:1–5 · They will see his face
  • Gospel Matthew 19:13–14 · Let the little children come to me

Word for the day

“Let the little children come to me, and do not stop them; for it is to such as these that the kingdom of heaven belongs.” Matthew 19:14

Reflection

The Bible ends where it began, in a garden: the river of the water of life flows from the throne of God and of the Lamb, and the tree of life bears fruit every month, its leaves for the healing of the nations. “They will see his face… there will be no more night.” This is the goal of the Church's journey.

And who enters? Those like children. The disciples try to keep the children away; Jesus says, “Let them come.” The Kingdom belongs to those who receive it as a gift, with open hands and trusting hearts. On this Saturday, often kept in honour of Mary, we remember her as the humble handmaid who received everything as gift.

Homily outline

Theme: “Like children, toward the river of life.”

The vision of the heavenly city and Jesus' welcome to the little ones.

1. The river from the sanctuary

  • Ezekiel saw a river from the Temple bringing life and healing.
  • Revelation shows it flowing from the throne of God and the Lamb.
“Their leaves will not wither nor their fruit fail, but they will bear fresh fruit every month, because the water for them flows from the sanctuary. Their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for healing.” Ezekiel 47:12
“Then the angel showed me the river of the water of life, bright as crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb.” Revelation 22:1

Apply: Thank God for the grace that flows from the sacraments.

2. They will see his face

  • The longing of every psalm is fulfilled.
  • There will be no more night.
““Come,” my heart says, “seek his face!” Your face, LORD, do I seek.” Psalm 27:8
“They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads. And there will be no more night.” Revelation 22:4–5

Apply: End the day with a prayer of longing for heaven.

3. The Kingdom belongs to the little ones

  • God's praise comes from the mouths of infants.
  • Jesus welcomes children; so must we.
“Out of the mouths of babes and infants you have founded a bulwark because of your foes.” Psalm 8:2
“But Jesus said, “Let the little children come to me, and do not stop them; for it is to such as these that the kingdom of heaven belongs.”” Matthew 19:14

Apply: Pray with the children in your family tonight.

The journey of the Church ends at the river of life.

Let us go there as children.

Take-home resolution: Teach a child one short prayer this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, the Bible ends where it began, in a garden: the river of the water of life flows from the throne of God and of the Lamb; the tree of life bears fruit every month, and its leaves are for the healing of the nations. They will see his face, and there will be no more night (Revelation 22:1–5).

Jesus shows the way to that glory in the Gospel. When people brought little children to him, the disciples spoke sternly to them. But Jesus said: let the little children come to me, and do not stop them; for it is to such as these that the kingdom of heaven belongs (Matthew 19:14).

Heaven belongs to the humble and the trusting. Let nothing stop us from bringing our children to Jesus: Holy Qurbana, family prayer, catechism. And let us too take the Father's hand with the simple faith of a child. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said: let the little children come to me. Jesus loves you very much! Talk to him today like a friend.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you welcome the little ones. Make me childlike in trust, and lead me to the river of life, where I shall see your face and there will be no more night. Amen.
