Readings

  • Epistle 1 Corinthians 12:12–18 · One body with many members
  • Gospel John 15:1–10 · Abide in me; abide in my love

Word for the day

“I am the vine, you are the branches. Those who abide in me and I in them bear much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing.” John 15:5

Reflection

Yesterday Jesus prayed that we may be one; today the readings show what that unity looks like from the inside. Paul uses the image of the body: “For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ.” Notice that Paul does not end with the Church but with Christ himself: the Church is Christ's own Body, and we became its members in Baptism, for “in the one Spirit we were all baptized into one body”. No member may say, “Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body.” The grandmother who prays her rosary quietly at the back, the catechism teacher, the young man who carries the processional cross: “God arranged the members in the body, each one of them, as he chose.” Envy and self-contempt both forget this.

Jesus teaches the same truth with the image of the vine: “I am the vine, you are the branches.” Life flows from the vine into every branch; cut off, a branch withers. The Father is the vinegrower, and he prunes the fruitful branch “to make it bear more fruit”: our trials are often his pruning, not his rejection. Again and again in these verses Jesus says abide. It is the language of a home, of staying. “As the Father has loved me, so I have loved you; abide in my love.” We abide in him by keeping his word, by prayer, and above all in the Holy Qurbana, where he says to us once more: abide in me as I abide in you.

Homily outline

Theme: “Apart from me you can do nothing.”

One body and one vine: our life is in Christ and in one another.

1. One body, many members

  • Baptized into one body.
  • No member is useless.
  • God arranged each one.
“I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Wonderful are your works; that I know very well.” Psalm 139:14
“For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ.” 1 Corinthians 12:12

Apply: Thank one person whose service in the parish goes unnoticed.

2. I am the vine

  • Israel, the vine God planted.
  • Jesus, the true vine.
  • Apart from him, nothing.
“You brought a vine out of Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it.” Psalm 80:8
“I am the vine, you are the branches. Those who abide in me and I in them bear much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing.” John 15:5

Apply: Begin each task this week with: Lord, apart from you I can do nothing.

3. Abide in my love

  • An everlasting love.
  • Pruning for more fruit.
  • Keeping his commandments.
“the LORD appeared to him from far away. I have loved you with an everlasting love; therefore I have continued my faithfulness to you.” Jeremiah 31:3
“As the Father has loved me, so I have loved you; abide in my love.” John 15:9

Apply: Visit Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament once this week.

Each of us is a needed member of Christ's Body.

Joined to the vine, we bear much fruit.

Take-home resolution: Stay with Jesus for five minutes each day this week, and thank one fellow member of the parish.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul says that the Church is like a body: one body, many members. In the one Spirit we were all baptized into one body (1 Corinthians 12:13). The foot cannot say: I do not belong, because I am not a hand.

Some of us think we are too important; others think we are not important at all. Both forget that God arranged the members in the body, each one of them, as he chose (1 Corinthians 12:18). You are needed in the Body of Christ.

Jesus gives the same lesson with a vine: I am the vine, you are the branches. Those who abide in me and I in them bear much fruit, because apart from me you can do nothing (John 15:5). The secret of a fruitful life is not effort alone but staying joined to him.

Sometimes the Father prunes us. It hurts, but he does it so that we may bear more fruit. Let us abide in his love through his word, through our prayer and through the Holy Qurbana. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says he is a vine and you are a little branch. Stay close to Jesus every day with a prayer, and you will grow lovely fruit: kindness, joy and peace!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you are the vine and we are the branches. Keep me joined to you and to all the members of your Body. Prune in me whatever bears no fruit, and let me abide in your love, so that the Father may be glorified by the fruit of my life. Amen.
