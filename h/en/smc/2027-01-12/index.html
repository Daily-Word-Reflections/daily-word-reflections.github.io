Second Tuesday of Denha

12 January 2027 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle 1 John 3:1–10 · The Son of God was revealed to destroy the works of the devil
  • Gospel Mark 1:21–28 · The unclean spirit confesses the Holy One of God

Word for the day

“See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God; and that is what we are. The reason the world does not know us is that it did not know him.” 1 John 3:1

Reflection

St John begins with wonder: “See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God; and that is what we are.” This is not a title only; it is a new reality given in Baptism. And it has a purpose: “The Son of God was revealed for this purpose, to destroy the works of the devil.” Denha, the revelation of the Son, is God's campaign against evil. Those born of God do not make sin their way of life.

Mark shows the campaign beginning. In the synagogue of Capernaum a man with an unclean spirit cries out: “What have you to do with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God.” The demons recognise him before the people do. Jesus rebukes the spirit: “Be silent, and come out of him!” and the man is freed. Christ has come to set us free; our part is to live as the children we are.

Full homily

Dear friends, St John cannot contain his wonder: see what love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God; and that is what we are (1 John 3:1). Not just a name, but a reality given in Baptism.

John tells us why the Son came: the Son of God was revealed for this purpose, to destroy the works of the devil (1 John 3:8). Denha is God's revelation for our freedom.

In the synagogue a man with an unclean spirit shouts: I know who you are, the Holy One of God. Jesus says: be silent, and come out of him! And the man is freed (Mark 1:24–25). Evil recognises its Master.

We are children of God. When temptation comes, let us remember who we are. When evil oppresses, let us call on the Holy One of God. Amen.

For children

Children, God loves you so much that he calls you his child. You really are God's child! Say it today: I am God's child!

Prayer

Father, see what love you have given us: we are your children. Your Son was revealed to destroy the works of the devil. Free us from every evil, and let us live as your children in holiness and love. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
