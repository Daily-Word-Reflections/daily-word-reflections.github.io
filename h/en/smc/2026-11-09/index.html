Second Monday of the Dedication of the Church

9 November 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Colossians 1:12–20 · Christ is the head of the Church
  • Gospel Matthew 16:13–20 · Peter's confession of faith

Word for the day

“And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.” Matthew 16:18

Reflection

Paul's great hymn proclaims Christ as the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation, “the head of the body, the church”. Everything was created through him and for him, and in him all things hold together.

At Caesarea Philippi Peter confesses, “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God,” and Jesus builds his Church on that rock. The Church has Christ as head and Peter as the visible foundation of unity. Our Syro-Malabar Church lives this: an ancient apostolic tradition in full communion with the successor of Peter.

Homily outline

Theme: “On this rock.”

Christ the head of the Church; Peter the rock of unity.

1. A tested cornerstone

  • God promised a precious cornerstone in Zion.
  • Christ is that stone, and the head of the body.
“See, I am laying in Zion a foundation stone, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, a sure foundation: “One who trusts will not panic.”” Isaiah 28:16
“Simon Peter answered, “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.”” Matthew 16:16

Apply: Make Christ the first thought of your day.

2. Revealed by the Father

  • Faith is a gift, not a human achievement.
  • We ask the Father to deepen our faith.
“The stone that the builders rejected has become the chief cornerstone.” Psalm 118:22
“And Jesus answered him, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah! For flesh and blood has not revealed this to you, but my Father in heaven.”” Matthew 16:17

Apply: Pray for the gift of deeper faith.

3. The Church on the rock

  • Christ builds; the gates of Hades will not prevail.
  • Communion with Peter's successor keeps the Church one.
“Therefore thus says the Lord GOD, See, I am laying in Zion a foundation stone.” Isaiah 28:16
“And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it.” Matthew 16:18

Apply: Pray for the Pope and our Major Archbishop.

Christ is the head; Peter's faith is the rock.

Let us stand firm in that faith.

Take-home resolution: Pray the Creed slowly each day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul's great hymn proclaims Christ: the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation, the head of the body, the Church. All things were created through him and for him, and in him all things hold together (Colossians 1:15–18).

At Caesarea Philippi Peter confesses: you are the Messiah, the Son of the living God. Jesus answers that flesh and blood did not reveal this, but his Father in heaven; on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not prevail against it (Matthew 16:16–18).

The Church is not only a human organisation; it is a body with Christ as its head, built on the rock of faith. This week let us make Peter's confession our own: pray the Creed slowly and from the heart, and pray for our Holy Father and our Major Archbishop. Amen.

For children

Children, when Jesus asked who he was, Peter said: you are the Son of the living God. Tell Jesus the same thing today!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, head of the Church, you are the Messiah, the Son of the living God. Build us on the rock of Peter's faith and keep your Church one. Amen.
