Third Saturday of Annunciation

19 December 2026 · Syro-Malabar Rite

Open in the Daily Word app

Readings

  • Epistle Galatians 1:11–17 · Chosen by the grace of God
  • Gospel John 3:31–36 · The one who comes from above is above all

Word for the day

“He whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for he gives the Spirit without measure.” John 3:34

Reflection

Paul tells how God “set me apart before I was born and called me through his grace” and “was pleased to reveal his Son to me.” His Gospel is not human invention but revelation. Like Jeremiah and John the Baptist, Paul was called from the womb.

John the Baptist testifies: “The one who comes from above is above all… He whom God has sent speaks the words of God.” As Christmas approaches, the Church contemplates the One who comes from above: not a teacher among teachers, but the Son into whose hands the Father has given all things.

Homily outline

Theme: “The One who comes from above.”

Paul called from the womb; John's witness to the One from heaven.

1. Called before birth

  • Jeremiah, John and Paul were called from the womb.
  • Each of us has a vocation from God.
“Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you were born I consecrated you.” Jeremiah 1:5
“The one who comes from above is above all; the one who is of the earth belongs to the earth and speaks about earthly things. The one who comes from heaven is above all.” John 3:31

Apply: Thank God for your vocation today.

2. He speaks the words of God

  • Jesus reveals the Father.
  • The Spirit is given without measure.
“Then afterward I will pour out my spirit on all flesh; your sons and your daughters shall prophesy.” Joel 2:28
“He whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for he gives the Spirit without measure.” John 3:34

Apply: Pray to the Holy Spirit before reading the Gospel.

3. All things in his hands

  • The Father has given all to the Son.
  • Believing in him is eternal life.
“For a child has been born for us, a son given to us; authority rests upon his shoulders.” Isaiah 9:6
“The Father loves the Son and has placed all things in his hands.” John 3:35

Apply: Set up the crib at home this weekend.

The One from above is coming.

Let him be above all in our lives.

Take-home resolution: Prepare the crib and pray before it each evening until Christmas.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul tells how God set him apart before he was born and called him through grace, and was pleased to reveal His Son to him (Galatians 1:15–16). Like Jeremiah and John the Baptist, Paul was called from the womb.

Each of us too was known and called by God before birth. Our life is not an accident. Christmas, the birth of the Son, reminds us of the dignity of every birth.

In the Gospel John the Baptist says of Jesus: the one who comes from above is above all; He whom God has sent speaks the words of God, for He gives the Spirit without measure (John 3:31–34).

Let us thank God today for our own life and vocation, and pray for unborn children and for mothers awaiting birth this Christmas. And let us listen to the words of Christ, who gives the Spirit without measure. Amen.

For children

Children, God knew you even before you were born, and He loves you very much. Thank Him for your life today!

Prayer

Father, you set us apart and called us by grace. Prepare our hearts to welcome your Son who comes from above and gives the Spirit without measure. Amen.
