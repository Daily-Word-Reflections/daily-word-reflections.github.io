Readings

Word for the day

“Do not let your hearts be troubled. Believe in God, believe also in me.” John 14:1

Reflection

In the last week of the year the Church hears again the promise of the Father's house: “In my Father's house there are many dwelling places… I go to prepare a place for you.” The Church on earth is the antechamber of that house.

Paul says that in Christ the dividing wall is broken down, and “through him both of us have access in one Spirit to the Father.” We are “being built together spiritually into a dwelling place for God.” Jesus is not only the destination but the Way: “Whoever has seen me has seen the Father.”

Homily outline

Theme: “The Way to the Father's house.”

The promise of many dwelling places and access to the Father in one Spirit.

1. Do not be troubled

  • Jesus speaks peace before his Passion.
  • Faith in God and in Christ calms the heart.
“As a mother comforts her child, so I will comfort you.” Isaiah 66:13
“Do not let your hearts be troubled. Believe in God, believe also in me.” John 14:1

Apply: Hand over one anxiety to Jesus each time it returns.

2. The Way

  • Thomas asks; Jesus answers.
  • No one comes to the Father except through him.
“The LORD make his face to shine upon you, and be gracious to you.” Numbers 6:25
“Jesus said to him, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”” John 14:6

Apply: Pray: “Jesus, show me the way today.”

3. Whoever has seen me

  • Jesus reveals the Father's face.
  • In Christ, God dwells among us.
“Sing and rejoice, O daughter Zion! For lo, I will come and dwell in your midst, says the LORD.” Zechariah 2:10
“Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you all this time, Philip, and you still do not know me? Whoever has seen me has seen the Father.”” John 14:9

Apply: Spend five minutes looking at a crucifix and seeing the Father's love.

The Father's house has a room for each of us.

Jesus is the Way there.

Take-home resolution: Pray John 14:1–6 each morning this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, in the last week of the year the Church hears again the promise of the Father's house: do not let your hearts be troubled. In my Father's house there are many dwelling places; I go to prepare a place for you (John 14:1–2). Thomas the Apostle asks: how can we know the way? Jesus answers: I am the way, and the truth, and the life (John 14:5–6).

Paul says that in Christ the dividing wall has been broken down: you are no longer strangers and aliens, but citizens with the saints and members of the household of God, built on Christ the cornerstone, growing into a dwelling place for God in the Spirit (Ephesians 2:19–22).

The Church on earth is the porch of that house. As the season of the Dedication of the Church ends, let us ask: am I a visitor in my parish, or a member of God's household? This week let us decide to take an active part in one ministry of the parish. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus said: I am the way. If we follow Jesus, we will reach the Father's house in heaven!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you are the Way to the Father. Calm my troubled heart, build me into your dwelling, and bring me home to the place you have prepared. Amen.
