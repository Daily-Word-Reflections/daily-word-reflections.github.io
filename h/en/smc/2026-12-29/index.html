Word for the day

“He said to them, “Why were you searching for me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?”” Luke 2:49

Reflection

At twelve Jesus stays behind in the Temple; Mary and Joseph search for him for three days in anguish. When they find him, he answers: “Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?” Even as a boy he knows who his Father is. Then he goes down with them to Nazareth and is obedient to them, growing in wisdom and favour.

Paul gives the rule of Christian family and community life: “Bear one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfil the law of Christ… let us not grow weary in doing what is right… let us work for the good of all.” The home at Nazareth was such a place: love that carried, patience that waited, and obedience that grew.

Full homily

Dear friends, St Paul tells the Galatians: bear one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfil the law of Christ (Galatians 6:2). Christian life is not lived alone. Each of us carries something heavy, and each of us is called to help carry another's load.

Paul is practical: if anyone is detected in a transgression, you who have received the Spirit should restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness. Take care that you yourselves are not tempted (Galatians 6:1). Correction must be gentle and humble.

He adds: let us not grow weary in doing what is right, for we will reap at harvest time, if we do not give up. So then, whenever we have an opportunity, let us work for the good of all, and especially for those of the family of faith (Galatians 6:9–10).

In the Gospel the twelve-year-old Jesus stays behind in the Temple. Mary and Joseph search for three days, anxious. When they find him, Mary says: your father and I have been searching for you in great anxiety. Jesus answers: did you not know that I must be in my Father's house? (Luke 2:48–49).

Even the Holy Family knew anxiety and misunderstanding. Mary did not fully understand, but she treasured all these things in her heart. Then Jesus went down with them to Nazareth and was obedient to them, and increased in wisdom and in years (Luke 2:51–52).

Many parents today search anxiously for children who are lost: in faith, in addiction, in wrong company, far away abroad. Let us bear one another's burdens: pray for these families, support them, and never grow weary. And let us ask Mary to teach us to treasure and ponder, even when we do not understand. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus obeyed Mary and Joseph at home. Help your parents today without complaining!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you were found in your Father's house and lived obediently at Nazareth. Help us bear one another's burdens and grow in wisdom and grace. Amen.
