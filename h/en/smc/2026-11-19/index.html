Word for the day

“But as for what was sown on good soil, this is the one who hears the word and understands it, who indeed bears fruit.” Matthew 13:23

Reflection

Paul thanks God that the Gospel “is bearing fruit and growing in the whole world,” and prays that the Colossians may “lead lives worthy of the Lord… bearing fruit in every good work.” The Church is God's field.

Jesus explains the sower: the same seed falls on the path, rocky ground, thorns and good soil. The difference lies in the hearing. “Blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear.” Good soil is a heart that hears, understands and keeps the word, and so bears fruit thirty, sixty and a hundredfold.

Homily outline

Theme: “Good soil.”

The Gospel grows in the world; the sower's seed bears fruit in hearts that hear.

1. The word does not return empty

  • God's word always accomplishes its purpose.
  • The Gospel is bearing fruit worldwide.
“So shall my word be that goes out from my mouth; it shall not return to me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I purpose.” Isaiah 55:11
“But blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear.” Matthew 13:16

Apply: Thank God for those who sowed the word in you.

2. Hearts that do not hear

  • Isaiah warned of ears that hear but do not understand.
  • Hardness and worry choke the seed.
“Keep listening, but do not comprehend; keep looking, but do not understand.” Isaiah 6:9
“As for what was sown among thorns, this is the one who hears the word, but the cares of the world and the lure of wealth choke the word, and it yields nothing.” Matthew 13:22

Apply: Name one worry and give it to God.

3. Fruit thirty, sixty, a hundredfold

  • Good soil hears, understands and keeps.
  • Fruit is seen in good works.
“They are like trees planted by streams of water, which yield their fruit in its season.” Psalm 1:3
“But as for what was sown on good soil, this is the one who hears the word and understands it, who indeed bears fruit and yields, in one case a hundredfold, in another sixty, and in another thirty.” Matthew 13:23

Apply: Read tomorrow's Gospel tonight.

The seed is good; let the soil be good.

Blessed are the ears that hear.

Take-home resolution: Give ten minutes to the word of God every day this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, Paul thanks God that the Gospel is bearing fruit and growing in the whole world, and prays that the Colossians may lead lives worthy of the Lord, bearing fruit in every good work (Colossians 1:6, 10). The Church is God's field.

Jesus explains the parable of the sower: the same seed falls on the path, on rocky ground, among thorns and on good soil. The one who hears the word and understands it bears fruit, a hundredfold, sixtyfold, thirtyfold (Matthew 13:18–23). The difference is not in the seed but in the soil.

What kind of soil is my heart today? A path trodden hard by hurry, shallow rock, or thorns of worry? Let us pull out one thorn this week: one hour less on the phone, one hour more with the Word. Amen.

For children

Children, God's word is like a seed. Listen carefully so you can be good soil! Tell your family one word you heard in church today.

Prayer

Lord, sow your word in my heart. Break up its hardness, deepen its soil, pull out the thorns, and make it bear fruit in every good work. Amen.
