Third Thursday of Denha · Thursday after the Three Days' Fast

  • Second Reading Micah 7:8–9, 18–20 · Who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity?

  • Gospel Luke 15:1–10 · Joy in heaven over one sinner who repents

Word for the day

“Just so, I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous persons who need no repentance.” Luke 15:7

Reflection

The fast is over, and the Church celebrates the joy of God who forgives. Ezekiel hears God swear: “As I live, says the Lord GOD, I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from their ways and live.” Micah sings: “Who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity…? He does not retain his anger forever, because he delights in showing clemency… You will cast all our sins into the depths of the sea.” After three days of turning, we hear God's answer: mercy.

In the Gospel tax collectors and sinners come near to Jesus, and the Pharisees grumble. Jesus tells of a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine sheep to find one lost sheep, and of a woman who sweeps her house to find one lost coin. When they find them, they call their neighbours: “Rejoice with me.” “Just so… there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents.” John the Elder writes: “I have no greater joy than this, to hear that my children are walking in the truth.” The fruit of the fast is joy: God's joy over us, and our joy in walking in his truth.

Homily outline

Theme: “Joy in heaven over one sinner who repents.”

After the fast: the God who delights in mercy, and the joy of the found.

1. No pleasure in death

  • God swears by his life.
  • Turn and live.
  • He wants all to repent.
“Say to them, As I live, says the Lord GOD, I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from their ways and live; turn back, turn back from your evil ways; for why will you die, O house of Israel?” Ezekiel 33:11
“The Lord is not slow about his promise, as some think of slowness, but is patient with you, not wanting any to perish, but all to come to repentance.” 2 Peter 3:9

Apply: Thank God for his patience with you.

2. Sins into the sea

  • A God like no other.
  • He delights in clemency.
  • Sins cast away.
“He will again have compassion upon us; he will tread our iniquities under foot. You will cast all our sins into the depths of the sea.” Micah 7:19
“When he has found it, he lays it on his shoulders and rejoices. And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost.’” Luke 15:5–6

Apply: Do not dig up sins God has forgiven.

3. Heaven's joy

  • The lost sheep and coin.
  • Rejoice with me.
  • Joy over one sinner.
“The LORD, your God, is in your midst, a warrior who gives victory; he will rejoice over you with gladness, he will renew you in his love; he will exult over you with loud singing” Zephaniah 3:17
“Just so, I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous persons who need no repentance.” Luke 15:7

Apply: Reach out to one person who has drifted away.

The fast ends in God's joy.

Let us rejoice, and seek the lost.

Take-home resolution: Pray this week for one person far from God, by name.

Full homily

Dear friends, the Three Days' Fast is over, and today the Church celebrates God's joy. Through Ezekiel God swears: as I live, I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from their ways and live (Ezekiel 33:11).

Micah sings: who is a God like you, pardoning iniquity? He delights in showing clemency. You will cast all our sins into the depths of the sea (Micah 7:18–19). What we brought to God in these three days, he has thrown into the sea.

In the Gospel Jesus tells of a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine sheep to find one, and of a woman who searches her whole house for one coin. When they find them they say: rejoice with me. Just so, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents (Luke 15:6–7).

John the Elder writes: I have no greater joy than this, to hear that my children are walking in the truth (3 John 4). Let the fruit of our fast be joy, and let us become seekers of the lost. Amen.

For children

Children, a shepherd found his lost sheep and was so happy! God is that happy when we come back to him. Thank God for these three days of fasting.

Prayer

God of mercy, you take no pleasure in the death of sinners and you cast our sins into the depths of the sea. Thank you for the grace of these days of fasting. Let us share heaven's joy over every sinner who returns, and walk always in your truth. Amen.
