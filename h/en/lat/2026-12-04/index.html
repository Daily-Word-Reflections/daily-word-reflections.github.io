Word for the day

“Then he touched their eyes and said, “According to your faith let it be done to you.”” Matthew 9:29

Reflection

Isaiah describes the transformation God will bring: Lebanon becomes a fruitful field, “the deaf shall hear the words of a scroll, and out of their gloom and darkness the eyes of the blind shall see”, the meek find joy, the tyrant disappears. Advent is the season of this hope.

Two blind men follow Jesus crying, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!” They call him by his messianic title, though they cannot see him. In the house Jesus asks them one question: “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” “Yes, Lord.” He touches their eyes: “According to your faith let it be done to you.” Their eyes are opened. Faith sees before the eyes do. St John Damascene, remembered today, defended the veneration of icons: in Christ the invisible God has become visible, and we may look on his face.

Full homily

Dear friends, Isaiah describes the great change God will bring: the deaf will hear, the eyes of the blind will see out of their darkness, the humble will rejoice, and the proud and cruel will disappear (Isaiah 29:18–20). Advent is the season of this hope.

In the Gospel two blind men follow Jesus crying: have mercy on us, Son of David. Jesus asks them: do you believe that I am able to do this? They answer: yes, Lord. He touches their eyes and says: according to your faith let it be done to you (Matthew 9:27–29).

Jesus asks the same question to us: do you believe that I can do this? Can He heal my anger, my addiction, my fear, my broken family? Faith does not mean we know how He will act; it means we trust that He can.

The psalm sings: the Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? (Psalm 27:1). Today, name one darkness in your life and say to Jesus: yes, Lord, I believe you can. Then wait for Him with hope. Amen.

For children

Children, two blind men asked Jesus for help and He opened their eyes. When you pray, believe that Jesus can help you!

Prayer

Son of David, have mercy on me. Touch the eyes of my heart, increase my faith, and be my light and my salvation this Advent. Amen.
