  • First Reading Hebrews 10:32–39 · Do not abandon that confidence of yours

Word for the day

“He also said, “The kingdom of God is as if someone would scatter seed on the ground, and would sleep and rise night and day, and the seed would sprout and grow, he does not know how.” Mark 4:26–27

Reflection

Hebrews reminds a tired community of its early courage: “Recall those earlier days when, after you had been enlightened, you endured a hard struggle with sufferings… you cheerfully accepted the plundering of your possessions.” Now they are tempted to give up. “Do not, therefore, abandon that confidence of yours; it brings a great reward. For you need endurance.” Faith is not only a beginning; it is a long faithfulness. “We are not among those who shrink back and so are lost, but among those who have faith and so are saved.”

Jesus gives two small parables that are a medicine for discouragement. A farmer scatters seed, sleeps and rises, “and the seed would sprout and grow, he does not know how.” The earth produces “first the stalk, then the head, then the full grain in the head.” And the mustard seed, “the smallest of all the seeds on earth”, becomes a shrub where the birds nest. God's Kingdom grows quietly, slowly and surely, often while we sleep. Our part is to sow faithfully and to trust; the growth belongs to God.

Homily outline

Theme: “The seed would sprout and grow, he does not know how.”

Endurance, patience and the quiet growth of God's Kingdom.

1. Do not abandon your confidence

  • Remember early courage.
  • Endurance needed.
  • Not those who shrink back.
“Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; wait for the LORD!” Psalm 27:14
“Do not, therefore, abandon that confidence of yours; it brings a great reward.” Hebrews 10:35

Apply: Persevere in one good habit this week.

2. The seed grows by itself

  • Scatter and sleep.
  • Stalk, head, full grain.
  • God gives the growth.
“Commit your way to the LORD; trust in him, and he will act.” Psalm 37:5
“So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God who gives the growth.” 1 Corinthians 3:7

Apply: Leave one worry about results with God.

3. The mustard seed

  • The smallest seed.
  • The greatest shrub.
  • Birds in its shade.
“On the mountain height of Israel I will plant it, in order that it may produce boughs and bear fruit, and become a noble cedar. Under it every kind of bird will live; in the shade of its branches will nest winged creatures of every kind.” Ezekiel 17:23
“It is like a mustard seed, which, when sown upon the ground, is the smallest of all the seeds on earth;” Mark 4:31

Apply: Do one small act of faith and trust God with it.

God's Kingdom grows quietly and surely.

Sow faithfully, endure patiently.

Take-home resolution: Do not give up a good work you have begun.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews speaks to a tired community: recall those earlier days when, after you had been enlightened, you endured a hard struggle with sufferings (Hebrews 10:32). Now they were tempted to give up.

Do not, therefore, abandon that confidence of yours; it brings a great reward. For you need endurance (Hebrews 10:35–36). Faith is a long faithfulness.

Jesus gives two small parables for the discouraged. The seed grows, he does not know how (Mark 4:27). The mustard seed, the smallest of all seeds, becomes a great shrub (Mark 4:31–32). God's Kingdom grows quietly and surely.

Our part is to sow faithfully and to trust. The growth belongs to God. Do not shrink back. Amen.

For children

Children, a tiny mustard seed grows into a big plant! Small kind things we do for Jesus can grow into something big.

Prayer

Lord God, your Kingdom grows like seed in the night and like the smallest of seeds. Give us endurance not to shrink back, patience to sow and wait, and trust that you give the growth. Amen.
