Saints Paul Miki and Companions, Martyrs

Word for the day

“As he went ashore, he saw a great crowd; and he had compassion for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd; and he began to teach them many things.” Mark 6:34

Reflection

Hebrews closes with an invitation and a blessing. The invitation: “Through him, then, let us continually offer a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips that confess his name. Do not neglect to do good and to share what you have, for such sacrifices are pleasing to God.” The old temple sacrifices are over; now our praise and our sharing become the offering. The blessing is one of the most beautiful in the New Testament: “Now may the God of peace, who brought back from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, by the blood of the eternal covenant, make you complete in everything good so that you may do his will.”

In the Gospel the apostles return from their mission and tell Jesus everything. He says: “Come away to a deserted place all by yourselves and rest a while.” Jesus cares for tired workers. But the crowd runs ahead of them, and when Jesus sees them, “he had compassion for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd; and he began to teach them many things.” Rest gives way to compassion. Today we honour St Paul Miki, a Japanese Jesuit, and his twenty-five companions, priests, brothers, catechists and lay people, among them young boys, who were crucified on a hill at Nagasaki on 5 February 1597. From his cross Paul Miki preached to the crowd and forgave his executioners. For more than two centuries Japanese Christians kept the faith in secret without priests, until missionaries returned in the nineteenth century and found them still praying. The Good Shepherd had not left his flock.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews says: through him, then, let us continually offer a sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of lips that confess his name (Hebrews 13:15). And do not neglect to do good and to share what you have.

It blesses us: may the God of peace, who brought back from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep… make you complete in everything good so that you may do his will (Hebrews 13:20–21).

In the Gospel Jesus tells his tired apostles: come away to a deserted place all by yourselves and rest a while (Mark 6:31). But seeing the crowd, he had compassion for them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd (Mark 6:34).

Today we honour St Paul Miki and his companions, crucified at Nagasaki in 1597. From the cross Paul Miki preached and forgave. Let us rest in the Good Shepherd and, like the martyrs, give our lives with compassion. Amen.

For children

Children, St Paul Miki and his friends in Japan, even young boys, loved Jesus so much that they died on crosses, singing and forgiving. Let us pray for children everywhere who suffer for Jesus.

Prayer

O God, strength of all the Saints, who through the Cross were pleased to call the Martyrs Saint Paul Miki and companions to life, grant, we pray, that by their intercession we may hold with courage even until death to the faith that we profess. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.
