Word for the day

“At that moment she came, and began to praise God and to speak about the child to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.” Luke 2:38

Reflection

After Simeon comes Anna, a prophet, the daughter of Phanuel of the tribe of Asher, a widow of great age. She “never left the temple but worshiped there with fasting and prayer night and day.” A whole life of faithful prayer prepares her to recognise the child at once. She becomes one of the first evangelists: she praises God and speaks about the child to everyone waiting for redemption.

Luke places a man and a woman, Simeon and Anna, side by side as witnesses; both elderly, both full of the Spirit. The elderly have a mission in the Church: to pray, to remember and to pass on the faith (Pope Francis, catecheses on old age, 2022). The first reading warns that “the world and its desire are passing away, but those who do the will of God live forever.” Then the Holy Family returns to Nazareth, and “the child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom”.

Full homily

Dear friends, St John writes to Christians of every age: I am writing to you, little children, because your sins are forgiven; to you, fathers, because you know him; to you, young people, because you have conquered the evil one (1 John 2:12–14). Every generation has its place in God's family.

Then he warns: do not love the world or the things in the world. The desire of the flesh, the desire of the eyes, the pride in riches, comes not from the Father. The world and its desire are passing away, but those who do the will of God live for ever (1 John 2:15–17).

In the Gospel we meet Anna, a prophet, eighty-four years old, a widow who never left the Temple but worshipped there with fasting and prayer night and day. At that moment she came, began to praise God and to speak about the child to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem (Luke 2:36–38).

Anna was a widow for most of her life. She could have become bitter. Instead, she made her loneliness into prayer. And because she was praying, she was there at the right moment to meet the Messiah.

Then the Gospel says: the child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom; and the favour of God was upon him (Luke 2:40). The Son of God grew up slowly, in an ordinary home in Nazareth.

At the end of this year, let us examine what we love: the passing things of the world, or the will of God that lasts for ever? Let us honour the widows and elderly in our parish, and learn from Anna to turn loneliness into prayer and praise. Amen.

For children

Children, Anna was very old and prayed every day. Thank God for your grandparents who pray for you!

Prayer

Lord, give me Anna's faithfulness in prayer and her joy in speaking of your Son. Keep me from loving what is passing away, and let me do your will, that I may live forever. Amen.
