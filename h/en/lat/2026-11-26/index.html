Word for the day

“Now when these things begin to take place, stand up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near.” Luke 21:28

Reflection

Jesus speaks of Jerusalem surrounded by armies (fulfilled in AD 70) and then of cosmic signs: sun, moon and stars shaken, nations in distress, people fainting with fear. In the middle of it he gives an unexpected command: “stand up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near.” What makes others faint makes disciples look up. The coming of the Son of Man “with power and great glory” is not a threat to those who love him; it is their liberation.

Revelation tells the same story in two images. Babylon, the proud city of luxury, idolatry and oppression, falls: “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great!” And a wedding is announced: “Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” Every empire built on pride ends in ruins; the Bride of the Lamb is invited to a feast. At every Mass the priest echoes this verse before Communion: “Blessed are those called to the supper of the Lamb.”

Theme: “Raise your heads.”

When the world shakes, disciples look up: Babylon falls, and the Lamb's wedding feast is near.

1. Raise your heads

  • Signs in the heavens, distress on earth.
  • Others faint with fear.
  • Disciples look up to their Redeemer.
“It will be said on that day, Lo, this is our God; we have waited for him, so that he might save us. This is the LORD for whom we have waited; let us be glad and rejoice in his salvation.” Isaiah 25:9
“Then they will see ‘the Son of Man coming in a cloud’ with power and great glory. Now when these things begin to take place, stand up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near.” Luke 21:27–28

Apply: Pray a prayer of trust when you hear bad news.

2. Babylon falls

  • Babylon is the city of pride and luxury.
  • Empires built on pride end in ruins.
  • Do not build your life on it.
“Then he responded, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon; and all the images of her gods lie shattered on the ground.”” Isaiah 21:9
“He called out with a mighty voice, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great!”” Revelation 18:2

Apply: Renounce one luxury this Advent and give its cost to the poor.

“On this mountain the LORD of hosts will make for all peoples a feast of rich food, a feast of well-aged wines, of rich food filled with marrow, of well-aged wines strained clear.” Isaiah 25:6
“And the angel said to me, “Write this: Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb.” And he said to me, “These are true words of God.”” Revelation 19:9

Apply: Prepare carefully for Communion this Sunday.

Full homily

Dear friends, Jesus speaks of Jerusalem surrounded by armies, fulfilled in the year 70, and then of signs in the sun, the moon and the stars, nations in distress, people fainting from fear. And in the middle of it all he gives an unexpected command: stand up and raise your heads, because your redemption is drawing near (Luke 21:28).

What makes others faint makes disciples look up. The coming of the Son of Man in power and great glory is not a threat to those who love him; it is their liberation. We are not people of fear but of hope.

Revelation tells the same story in two pictures. Babylon, the proud city of luxury, idolatry and oppression, falls: fallen, fallen is Babylon the great (Revelation 18:2). And a wedding is announced: blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb (Revelation 19:9). Every empire built on pride ends in ruins; the Bride of the Lamb is invited to a feast.

At every Mass, before Communion, the priest echoes this invitation. Let us prepare for that supper with care this Sunday: a moment of silence before Mass, and, if we need it, a good confession before Advent begins. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: when you hear bad news, look up, because I am near! Before Communion on Sunday, close your eyes and tell Jesus you are happy he is coming.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, when the world shakes, let me stand up and raise my head, for my redemption is near. Free me from the pride of Babylon, and make me ready for the marriage supper of the Lamb. Amen.
