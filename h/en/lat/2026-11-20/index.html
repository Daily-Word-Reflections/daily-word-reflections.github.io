Word for the day

“It is written, ‘My house shall be a house of prayer’; but you have made it a den of robbers.” Luke 19:46

Reflection

John is told to take the little scroll and eat it: “it was sweet as honey in my mouth, but when I had eaten it, my stomach was made bitter.” Like Ezekiel before him, the prophet must first digest God's word before he can speak it. The word is sweet because it is God's; it is bitter because it carries judgement and suffering. Whoever proclaims it must first let it enter his own life.

Jesus enters the Temple and drives out the sellers, quoting Isaiah and Jeremiah: “My house shall be a house of prayer; but you have made it a den of robbers.” Then he teaches there daily, and the leaders cannot act against him, “for all the people were spellbound by what they heard”. The cleansed Temple becomes a school of the Word. Our churches and our hearts are meant to be the same: houses of prayer where God's word is heard and loved.

Full homily

Dear friends, John is told to take the little scroll from the angel's hand and eat it. It was sweet as honey in his mouth, but when he had eaten it his stomach was made bitter (Revelation 10:9–10). Like Ezekiel before him, the prophet must first digest God's word before he can speak it.

The word is sweet because it is God's: it consoles, it heals, it gives hope. It is also bitter, because it calls us to conversion and carries the cost of the Cross. Whoever proclaims it, parent, catechist or priest, must first let it enter his own life.

In the Gospel Jesus enters the Temple and drives out those who were selling, saying: my house shall be a house of prayer, but you have made it a den of robbers. And every day he was teaching in the Temple, and all the people were spellbound by what they heard (Luke 19:46–48).

Our churches, our homes and our hearts are meant to be houses of prayer. Let us clear out what crowds God out, and let us eat his word: read the Sunday Gospel at home every Saturday evening, slowly, so that on Sunday it is already in us. Amen.

For children

Children, God's word is sweeter than honey! Ask your parents to read the Sunday Gospel with you on Saturday night.

Prayer

Lord, your words are sweeter than honey. Let me eat your word and let it change me, even when it is bitter. Cleanse my heart and make it a house of prayer. Amen.
