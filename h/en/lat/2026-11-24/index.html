Word for the day

“Beware that you are not led astray; for many will come in my name and say, ‘I am he!’ and, ‘The time is near!’ Do not go after them.” Luke 21:8

Reflection

People admire the Temple's beautiful stones and gifts. Jesus says: “the days will come when not one stone will be left upon another”. Even the holiest building on earth is not permanent. Then he warns against false prophets who announce dates and claim his name, and against panic at wars and earthquakes: “these things must take place first, but the end will not follow immediately.” Christian hope is calm, not alarmist.

Revelation pictures the end as a harvest. One like a Son of Man on a cloud swings his sickle, for “the harvest of the earth is fully ripe”. Judgement belongs to God and comes in his time. Today's martyrs, 117 Vietnamese Christians (priests, bishops, catechists, fathers and mothers) killed between 1745 and 1862 and canonised in 1988, lived this calm hope under persecution. Their lives were the harvest; they were gathered into God's barn.

Homily outline

Theme: “Do not be terrified.”

The Temple's stones fall, false prophets come, but the harvest belongs to God.

1. Not one stone

  • The Temple was beautiful.
  • It would be destroyed in AD 70.
  • Only God's work lasts.
“Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain. Unless the LORD guards the city, the guard keeps watch in vain.” Psalm 127:1
“As for these things that you see, the days will come when not one stone will be left upon another; all will be thrown down.” Luke 21:6

Apply: Build your life on prayer and the sacraments.

2. Do not be led astray

  • Many will claim his name.
  • Date-setters are false prophets.
  • Wars do not mean the end is here.
“Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you, says the LORD.” Jeremiah 1:8
“And he said, “Beware that you are not led astray; for many will come in my name and say, ‘I am he!’ and, ‘The time is near!’ Do not go after them. When you hear of wars and insurrections, do not be terrified; for these things must take place first, but the end will not follow immediately.”” Luke 21:8–9

Apply: Do not forward fearful end-times messages.

3. The harvest

  • The sickle belongs to God.
  • Wheat and weeds grow until harvest.
  • The martyrs were ripe wheat.
“Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Go in, tread, for the wine press is full. The vats overflow, for their wickedness is great.” Joel 3:13
“Another angel came out of the temple, calling with a loud voice to the one who sat on the cloud, “Use your sickle and reap, for the hour to reap has come, because the harvest of the earth is fully ripe.”” Revelation 14:15

Apply: Pray for persecuted Christians.

Our hope is calm because God holds history.

Let us ripen in faith like the martyrs.

Take-home resolution: Learn the life of one Asian martyr this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, people were admiring the Temple with its beautiful stones and gifts. Jesus said: the days will come when not one stone will be left upon another (Luke 21:6). Even the holiest building on earth does not last for ever; only God's word remains.

Then he warns: beware that you are not led astray, for many will come in my name and say, the time is near. Do not go after them. When you hear of wars and insurrections, do not be terrified (Luke 21:8–9). Christian hope is calm, not alarmist. We do not need to chase predictions; we need to stay faithful.

Revelation pictures the end as a harvest: one like a Son of Man on a cloud swings his sickle, for the harvest of the earth is fully ripe (Revelation 14:15). The judgement belongs to God, and it comes in his time, not ours.

Today we honour St Andrew Dũng-Lạc and the martyrs of Vietnam: priests, catechists, fathers and mothers, who lived this calm hope under persecution. They were ripe wheat gathered into God's barn. Let us pray today for Christians who suffer for their faith, and let us share hope, not fear. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: do not be afraid. The martyrs of Vietnam loved Jesus even when it was dangerous. Pray today for children who cannot go to church freely.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, keep me from false alarms and from false prophets. Root me in your word, which does not pass away, and give me the courage of the Vietnamese martyrs, so that I may be ripe for your harvest. Amen.
