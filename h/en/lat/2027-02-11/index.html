Word for the day

“Then he said to them all, “If any want to become my followers, let them deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me.” Luke 9:23

Reflection

On the edge of the promised land Moses sets a choice before Israel: “See, I have set before you today life and prosperity, death and adversity.” To love the Lord, walk in his ways and keep his commandments is life; to turn away and bow to other gods is death. And Moses pleads: “Choose life so that you and your descendants may live, loving the LORD your God, obeying him, and holding fast to him; for that means life to you.” The Psalm paints the same two roads: the one who delights in the law of the Lord is “like trees planted by streams of water”, while the wicked are “like chaff that the wind drives away.” On the second day of Lent the Church places this choice before us too.

But Jesus reveals that the road to life passes through the cross. “The Son of Man must undergo great suffering, and be rejected… and be killed, and on the third day be raised.” Then he says to all: “If any want to become my followers, let them deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me.” Daily: not one heroic act, but the small faithful choices of each day. “For those who want to save their life will lose it, and those who lose their life for my sake will save it. What does it profit them if they gain the whole world, but lose or forfeit themselves?” Today, 11 February, we also remember Mary's appearing to St Bernadette at Lourdes in 1858. There Our Lady called for prayer and penance, and countless sick people have found healing or the grace to carry their cross. Mary, who stood by the cross of her Son, teaches us that the cross carried with Jesus leads to life.

Homily outline

Theme: “Choose life.”

Two ways, the daily cross, and Mary of Lourdes beside the sick.

1. Choose life

  • Life and death set before us.
  • Love, obey, hold fast.
  • The tree by the streams.

“I came that they may have life, and have it abundantly.” John 10:10

Apply: Each morning of Lent, say: today I choose life; today I choose you, Lord.

2. Take up your cross daily

  • The Son of Man must suffer.
  • Deny yourself.
  • Daily, not once.
“They are like trees planted by streams of water, which yield their fruit in its season, and their leaves do not wither. In all that they do, they prosper.” Psalm 1:3

Apply: Name your daily cross and carry it with love this week.

God sets before us life; Jesus shows the way through the cross.

Full homily

Dear friends, Moses sets a choice before Israel: I have set before you life and death, blessings and curses. Choose life so that you and your descendants may live (Deuteronomy 30:19). Lent begins with this same choice.

Jesus shows us the road to life. The Son of Man must suffer, be rejected, be killed and on the third day be raised. Then he says to all: if any want to become my followers, let them deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me (Luke 9:23).

Daily. Not one great sacrifice, but small faithful choices: patience at home, honesty at work, kindness to the difficult. And Jesus asks: what does it profit them if they gain the whole world, but lose or forfeit themselves? (Luke 9:25).

Today we remember Our Lady of Lourdes and pray for the sick on the World Day of the Sick. At Lourdes Mary asked for prayer and penance. Many have been healed; many more have received strength to carry their cross. With Mary, let us choose life by following Jesus on the way of the cross. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus says: carry your cross every day. That means doing hard good things with love. Mary helps us, like she helped St Bernadette at Lourdes. Let us pray for sick people today!

Prayer

Lord God, you set before us life and death and call us to choose life. Help us to deny ourselves, take up our cross daily and follow your Son. Through the prayers of Our Lady of Lourdes, comfort the sick and give them strength and healing. Amen.
