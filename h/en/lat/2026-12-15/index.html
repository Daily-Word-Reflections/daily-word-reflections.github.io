Word for the day

“For John came to you in the way of righteousness and you did not believe him, but the tax collectors and the prostitutes believed him; and even after you saw it, you did not change your minds and believe him.” Matthew 21:32

Reflection

Zephaniah condemns a proud city that “has listened to no voice” and promises a different future: God will purify the lips of the peoples, and “I will leave in the midst of you a people humble and lowly. They shall seek refuge in the name of the LORD.” This humble remnant is the true Israel of Advent: Mary, Joseph, Zechariah, Elizabeth, the shepherds.

Jesus tells of two sons. One refuses to work in the vineyard but later changes his mind and goes; the other says yes but does not go. The religious leaders gave God many words; tax collectors and prostitutes gave him their repentance. “Even after you saw it, you did not change your minds.” Advent asks not for fine words but for a changed heart. It is never too late to turn and go into the vineyard.

Full homily

Dear friends, Zephaniah condemns a proud city that listened to no voice, yet promises: I will leave in the midst of you a people humble and lowly; they shall seek refuge in the name of the Lord (Zephaniah 3:12). God's future belongs to the humble.

In the Gospel Jesus tells of two sons. The father asks both to work in the vineyard. The first says no, but later changes his mind and goes. The second says yes, sir, but does not go (Matthew 21:28–30). Which one did the father's will?

Jesus then says something shocking: the tax collectors and the prostitutes are going into the kingdom of God ahead of you, because they believed John and changed their lives (Matthew 21:31–32). Religious words are not enough; conversion is shown in action.

Are we the son who says yes but does not go? Advent is the time to turn our words into deeds. Make a good confession, repair one relationship, keep one promise you have made to God. Amen.

For children

Children, one son said no but then obeyed; the other said yes but did not. Jesus wants us to say yes and then really do it!

Prayer

Lord, the poor cry out and you hear them. Make me humble and lowly, change my heart where I have said no to you, and let my yes be more than words. Amen.
