Word for the day

“Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for the child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. She will bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.” Matthew 1:20–21

Reflection

Today's O Antiphon: “O Adonai, and leader of the house of Israel, who appeared to Moses in the fire of the burning bush and gave him the law on Sinai: come and redeem us with outstretched arm.” Jeremiah promises: “I will raise up for David a righteous Branch… And this is the name by which he will be called: ‘The LORD is our righteousness.’”

Matthew tells the story from Joseph's side. Mary is found with child; Joseph, “a righteous man and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace”, plans to dismiss her quietly. In a dream the angel tells him the child is from the Holy Spirit and gives him two tasks: take Mary as your wife, and name the child Jesus, “for he will save his people from their sins”. By naming him, Joseph makes Jesus legally a son of David. All this fulfils Isaiah: “the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall name him Emmanuel, God is with us.” Joseph awakes and obeys, without a word recorded.

Homily outline

Theme: “God with us.”

The righteous Branch, Joseph's obedient faith, and the name of Jesus.

1. The righteous Branch

  • Jeremiah's promise to David's house.
  • A King who deals wisely and justly.
  • His name: The Lord is our righteousness.
“The days are surely coming, says the LORD, when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch, and he shall reign as king and deal wisely, and shall execute justice and righteousness in the land. In his days Judah will be saved and Israel will live in safety. And this is the name by which he will be called: “The LORD is our righteousness.”” Jeremiah 23:5–6
“Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for the child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. She will bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins.” Matthew 1:20–21

Apply: Pray for just leaders in our country.

2. Emmanuel

  • The virgin conceives.
  • God is with us.
  • Christmas is God's nearness.
“Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign. Look, the young woman is with child and shall bear a son, and shall name him Immanuel.” Isaiah 7:14
““Look, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall name him Emmanuel,” which means, “God is with us.”” Matthew 1:23

Apply: Pray “Emmanuel, be with us” at each meal today.

3. Joseph obeyed

  • He was just and merciful.
  • He changed his plans.
  • He acted without a word.
“Noah did this; he did all that God commanded him.” Genesis 6:22
“When Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him; he took her as his wife.” Matthew 1:24

Apply: Do at once one thing God asks of you.

God is with us in Jesus.

Like Joseph, let us listen and obey.

Take-home resolution: Pray to St Joseph daily until Christmas.

Full homily

Dear friends, today's O Antiphon prays: O Adonai, ruler of the house of Israel, who appeared to Moses in the flame of the burning bush and gave him the law on Sinai: come and redeem us with outstretched arm.

Jeremiah announces: the days are surely coming, says the Lord, when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch, and he shall reign as king and deal wisely, and shall execute justice and righteousness in the land. And this is the name by which he will be called: The Lord is our righteousness (Jeremiah 23:5–6).

In the Gospel we meet St Joseph. Mary is found to be with child from the Holy Spirit. Joseph, being a righteous man and unwilling to expose her to public disgrace, planned to dismiss her quietly. But an angel says to him in a dream: Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife (Matthew 1:18–20).

Joseph's righteousness is not cold legalism. Even before the angel speaks, his justice is merciful: he will not shame Mary. Then, when God speaks, he obeys immediately: when Joseph awoke from sleep, he did as the angel commanded him (Matthew 1:24).

The angel tells him to name the child Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins, and calls him Emmanuel, God is with us (Matthew 1:21–23). Joseph gives Jesus his name and his place in the house of David. Without words, he serves God's plan.

Joseph is a model for fathers and for all of us: just and merciful, attentive to God's voice, quick to obey, faithful in silence. This Christmas, let us ask St Joseph to help us make room for Jesus in our homes, and not to be afraid. Amen.

For children

Children, Joseph listened to the angel and took care of Mary and baby Jesus. Ask St Joseph to help you obey God!

Prayer

O Adonai, Lord who spoke to Moses in the burning bush, come and redeem us with outstretched arm. Give me the faith of St Joseph to listen, to trust and to obey, and let Jesus be born in my home. Amen.
