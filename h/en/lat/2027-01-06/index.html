Word for the day

“There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear; for fear has to do with punishment, and whoever fears has not reached perfection in love.” 1 John 4:18

Reflection

After feeding the five thousand, Jesus sends the disciples ahead by boat and goes up the mountain to pray. In the night the wind is against them and they strain at the oars. Early in the morning he comes to them walking on the sea. They think he is a ghost and cry out in terror. Then comes one of the most beautiful sentences in the Gospels: “Take heart, it is I; do not be afraid.” In Greek, “it is I” is egō eimi, the name God revealed to Moses. He climbs into the boat, and the wind ceases.

Mark adds an honest note: “they did not understand about the loaves, but their hearts were hardened.” They had just seen a miracle and still did not see who Jesus was. St John gives the remedy for fear: “There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear.” God's love living in us, and our love for one another, is the only lasting answer to the storms of life. “No one has ever seen God; if we love one another, God lives in us.”

Full homily

Dear friends, after feeding the five thousand, Jesus sends his disciples ahead in the boat and goes up the mountain to pray (Mark 6:45–46). Even after a great success, he goes to the Father in prayer.

In the night the disciples are straining at the oars, for the wind is against them. Mark tells us that Jesus saw them (Mark 6:48). From the mountain, in the dark, he sees them. He sees you too, when life is hard work against the wind.

Early in the morning he comes walking on the sea. They think it is a ghost and cry out. Then he says: take heart, it is I; do not be afraid (Mark 6:50). The words it is I are the name of God revealed to Moses. The Lord of the sea climbs into their little boat, and the wind ceases.

Yet Mark says their hearts were hardened; they did not understand about the loaves. We too can see many signs of God's love and still be afraid. St John gives the remedy: there is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear (1 John 4:18).

Today, in many places, the Church celebrates the Epiphany, and our Eastern brothers and sisters celebrate the Baptism of the Lord. Christ is revealed as the one who comes to us in every storm. Let us invite him into our boat, and let his love drive out our fear. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus walked on the water to help his friends in the storm. When you are scared, say: Jesus, I trust in you! He is always near.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you saw your disciples straining at the oars and came to them across the sea. When the wind is against me, let me hear your voice: Take heart, it is I. Fill me with your perfect love that casts out fear. Amen.
