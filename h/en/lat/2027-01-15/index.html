Word for the day

“When Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”” Mark 2:5

Reflection

The house in Capernaum is so full that four men cannot bring their paralysed friend to Jesus. So they climb onto the roof, dig through it and lower him down on his mat. Mark says: “When Jesus saw their faith”, the faith of the four friends, he spoke to the paralytic. And his first words surprise everyone: “Son, your sins are forgiven.” Jesus goes first to the deepest paralysis, sin. The scribes object: who can forgive sins but God alone? They are right about God, and wrong about Jesus.

To show that “the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”, Jesus tells the man to take up his mat and walk, and he does. Everyone is amazed: “We have never seen anything like this!” Hebrews speaks of the “rest” God promised, which the wilderness generation failed to enter through unbelief, and urges: “Let us therefore make every effort to enter that rest.” Forgiveness is the beginning of that rest. In the sacrament of Reconciliation Christ still says: your sins are forgiven; rise and walk.

Full homily

Dear friends, the house in Capernaum is packed. Four men arrive carrying a paralysed friend on a mat. They cannot get near Jesus. So they climb the roof, dig through it and lower their friend right in front of Jesus.

Mark says: when Jesus saw their faith (Mark 2:5). The faith of the friends matters. We are saved as a people, carried by others' prayers and love.

Then Jesus says something unexpected: son, your sins are forgiven. He sees the deepest paralysis. The scribes think: who can forgive sins but God alone? They are right about God. They do not see that God is standing in front of them.

To show his authority, Jesus says: stand up, take your mat and go. And the man walks out before them all. Everyone says: we have never seen anything like this! (Mark 2:12).

Hebrews invites us: let us make every effort to enter God's rest (Hebrews 4:11). Forgiveness is the door. Let us go to confession, and let us be the four friends for someone who cannot reach Jesus alone. Amen.

For children

Children, four friends carried a sick man to Jesus, even through a roof! Jesus forgave him and made him walk. Can you bring a friend to Jesus by praying for them?

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you saw the faith of four friends and forgave the paralysed man. Thank you for those who have carried me to you. Forgive my sins, raise me up, and let me carry others to you. Amen.
