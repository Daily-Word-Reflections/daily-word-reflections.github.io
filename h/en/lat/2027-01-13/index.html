Word for the day

“In the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up and went out to a deserted place, and there he prayed.” Mark 1:35

Reflection

Mark shows us a full day in the life of Jesus. After the synagogue he enters Simon's house, where Simon's mother-in-law lies sick with fever. “He came and took her by the hand and lifted her up. Then the fever left her, and she began to serve them.” Healing becomes service. At sunset the whole town crowds at the door, and Jesus cures many. Then, “In the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up and went out to a deserted place, and there he prayed.” The secret of his day is his night prayer.

Hebrews explains why the Son came so close: he shared our flesh and blood “so that through death he might destroy the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil, and free those who all their lives were held in slavery by the fear of death.” He became like us in every respect to be a merciful high priest. “Because he himself was tested by what he suffered, he is able to help those who are being tested.” St Hilary of Poitiers, a married convert who became bishop, defended the divinity of Christ in exile; he knew both trials and the Lord who helps in them.

Homily outline

Theme: “In the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up… and there he prayed.”

Healing that leads to service, prayer that sustains, a priest who understands.

1. Lifted up to serve

  • He takes her by the hand.
  • The fever leaves.
  • She begins to serve.
“O LORD my God, I cried to you for help, and you have healed me.” Psalm 30:2
“He came and took her by the hand and lifted her up. Then the fever left her, and she began to serve them.” Mark 1:31

Apply: Serve someone today in thanks for a blessing.

2. Prayer before dawn

  • The town at the door.
  • Still very dark.
  • A deserted place.
“O LORD, in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I plead my case to you, and watch.” Psalm 5:3
“In the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up and went out to a deserted place, and there he prayed.” Mark 1:35

Apply: Pray before you touch your phone each morning.

3. Tested like us

  • He shared our flesh and blood.
  • He frees us from the fear of death.
  • He helps those who are tested.
“He was despised and rejected by others; a man of suffering and acquainted with infirmity; and as one from whom others hide their faces he was despised, and we held him of no account.” Isaiah 53:3
“Because he himself was tested by what he suffered, he is able to help those who are being tested.” Hebrews 2:18

Apply: Bring your present trial to Jesus by name.

Jesus' power flows from his prayer.

He who was tested lifts us up to serve.

Take-home resolution: Keep a morning prayer time all this week.

Full homily

Dear friends, today's Gospel shows us a full day in the life of Jesus. After teaching in the synagogue he goes to Simon's house, where Simon's mother-in-law is in bed with a fever.

He came and took her by the hand and lifted her up. Then the fever left her, and she began to serve them (Mark 1:31). The first thing she does with her new strength is serve. Healing leads to service.

At sunset the whole town gathers at the door, and Jesus cures many. Then: in the morning, while it was still very dark, he got up and went out to a deserted place, and there he prayed (Mark 1:35). Jesus' busy day rests on his night prayer.

Hebrews tells us why he came so close: he became like us in every respect, so that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest. Because he himself was tested by what he suffered, he is able to help those who are being tested (Hebrews 2:18).

Today we remember St Hilary, who defended faith in Christ even in exile. Let us ask for Jesus' habit of early prayer, and let him lift us up to serve. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus prayed very early in the morning, while it was still dark. Tomorrow, say a little prayer to Jesus as soon as you wake up!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you took Simon's mother-in-law by the hand and lifted her up, and before dawn you prayed to your Father. Lift me up from what weighs me down, teach me to pray, and help me in every test, for you were tested as I am. Amen.
