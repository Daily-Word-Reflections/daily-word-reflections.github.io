Word for the day

“Consequently he is able for all time to save those who approach God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them.” Hebrews 7:25

Reflection

Hebrews offers one of the most comforting truths in the New Testament: Christ “is able for all time to save those who approach God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them.” Right now, at the Father's right hand, the risen Jesus is praying for you. Unlike earthly priests who offered sacrifices daily, he offered himself “once for all”, and is “the mediator of a better covenant”.

Mark describes crowds pressing on Jesus from every region, so many that he asks for a boat to be ready. “All who had diseases pressed upon him to touch him.” The unclean spirits fall before him and shout, “You are the Son of God!”, but he silences them; he will be revealed in his own way, on the Cross. Today we honour St Agnes, a Roman girl of about twelve or thirteen who refused to give up her consecration to Christ and was martyred around 304. The weakest in the world's eyes, she was strong because her Lord was interceding for her.

Homily outline

Theme: “He always lives to make intercession for them.”

Christ the interceding High Priest; crowds who touch him; Agnes strong in weakness.

1. He always intercedes

  • Once for all.
  • Mediator of a better covenant.
  • Praying for us now.
“Therefore I will allot him a portion with the great, and he shall divide the spoil with the strong; because he poured out himself to death, and was numbered with the transgressors; yet he bore the sin of many, and made intercession for the transgressors.” Isaiah 53:12
“Consequently he is able for all time to save those who approach God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them.” Hebrews 7:25

Apply: Pray today: Jesus, I join my prayer to yours.

2. They pressed to touch him

  • Crowds from every region.
  • The sick reach out.
  • He heals by his word and touch.
“he sent out his word and healed them, and delivered them from destruction.” Psalm 107:20
“for he had cured many, so that all who had diseases pressed upon him to touch him.” Mark 3:10

Apply: Bring one sick person to Jesus in prayer daily.

3. Strong in weakness

  • Agnes, a young girl.
  • Consecrated to Christ.
  • Martyr for love.
“My beloved is mine and I am his; he pastures his flock among the lilies.” Song of Songs 2:16
“But God chose what is foolish in the world to shame the wise; God chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong;” 1 Corinthians 1:27

Apply: Pray for the protection of girls and children.

We never pray alone: Christ intercedes for us.

Like Agnes, the weak become strong in him.

Take-home resolution: Remember daily this week that Jesus is praying for you.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews tells us something deeply comforting: Christ is able for all time to save those who approach God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them (Hebrews 7:25). At this very moment the risen Jesus is praying for you.

He offered himself once for all, and now he is the mediator of a better covenant. We never pray alone; we pray inside his prayer.

In the Gospel crowds come from everywhere. All who had diseases pressed upon him to touch him (Mark 3:10). Even the unclean spirits cry: you are the Son of God! But Jesus silences them.

Today we honour St Agnes, a girl of twelve or thirteen who refused to deny her consecration to Christ and was martyred in Rome. She was weak in the world's eyes, but strong because Christ was praying for her. Let us trust his intercession and ask for her courage. Amen.

For children

Children, Jesus is praying for you right now in heaven! St Agnes was a young girl who loved Jesus very much and was very brave.

Prayer

Lord Jesus, you live for ever to intercede for us. Draw us to touch you in faith, and through the prayers of St Agnes give us courage and purity to belong wholly to you. Amen.
