Word for the day

“We have this hope, a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul, a hope that enters the inner shrine behind the curtain,” Hebrews 6:19

Reflection

Hebrews encourages a tired community: “God is not unjust; he will not overlook your work and the love that you showed for his sake in serving the saints.” Do not become sluggish, but imitate those who “through faith and patience inherit the promises”, like Abraham. God bound himself by an oath, so that “we… might be strongly encouraged to seize the hope set before us. We have this hope, a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul.” Our anchor is not thrown into the shifting sand of this world; it is fixed in heaven, where Jesus has gone ahead of us.

In the Gospel the Pharisees object when the disciples pluck grain on the sabbath. Jesus recalls David eating the holy bread when he was hungry, and declares: “The sabbath was made for humankind, and not humankind for the sabbath; so the Son of Man is lord even of the sabbath.” God's laws are gifts for our life, not burdens to crush it. The Lord's Day is his gift of rest and worship, and Christ himself is its Lord.

Homily outline

Theme: “A sure and steadfast anchor of the soul.”

Persevering love, hope anchored in heaven, the Lord of the sabbath.

1. An anchor of hope

  • God's promise and oath.
  • Hope set before us.
  • Fixed behind the curtain.
“God is not a human being, that he should lie, or a mortal, that he should change his mind. Has he promised, and will he not do it? Has he spoken, and will he not fulfill it?” Numbers 23:19
“We have this hope, a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul, a hope that enters the inner shrine behind the curtain,” Hebrews 6:19

Apply: Hold on to one promise of God in a present trouble.

2. God remembers your love

  • Serving the saints.
  • No good work forgotten.
  • Do not become sluggish.
“He has gained renown by his wonderful deeds; the LORD is gracious and merciful.” Psalm 111:4
“For God is not unjust; he will not overlook your work and the love that you showed for his sake in serving the saints, as you still do.” Hebrews 6:10

Apply: Return to one good work you abandoned.

3. The sabbath for humankind

  • David and the holy bread.
  • God's law serves life.
  • Christ, Lord of the sabbath.
“Remember the sabbath day, and keep it holy.” Exodus 20:8
“Then he said to them, “The sabbath was made for humankind, and not humankind for the sabbath; so the Son of Man is lord even of the sabbath.”” Mark 2:27–28

Apply: Make Sunday a day of worship, family and rest.

God remembers our love and anchors our hope.

Christ is Lord of our time and our rest.

Take-home resolution: Keep next Sunday holy as a family.

Full homily

Dear friends, Hebrews speaks to tired Christians: God is not unjust; he will not overlook your work and the love that you showed for his sake in serving the saints (Hebrews 6:10). No act of love is forgotten.

Do not become sluggish, but imitate those who through faith and patience inherit the promises, like Abraham. God bound himself by an oath so that we might seize the hope set before us.

We have this hope, a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul, a hope that enters the inner shrine behind the curtain (Hebrews 6:19). Our anchor is fixed in heaven, where Jesus has gone ahead of us.

In the Gospel Jesus says: the sabbath was made for humankind, and not humankind for the sabbath; so the Son of Man is lord even of the sabbath (Mark 2:27–28). God's law serves our life. Let us keep the Lord's Day as a gift, and hold fast to our anchor of hope. Amen.

For children

Children, an anchor keeps a boat from floating away. Hope in Jesus keeps our hearts safe. Hold on to Jesus!

Prayer

Faithful God, you never forget our love and you have given us hope as an anchor for the soul. Keep us patient in faith, and teach us to keep your day as a gift of life, with Christ the Lord of the sabbath. Amen.
