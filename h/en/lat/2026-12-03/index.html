Word for the day

“Everyone then who hears these words of mine and acts on them will be like a wise man who built his house on rock.” Matthew 7:24

Reflection

Jesus ends the Sermon on the Mount with a warning and a picture. Not everyone who says “Lord, Lord” will enter the Kingdom, but the one who does the Father's will. Two houses face the same rain, floods and wind; one stands because it is founded on rock. Isaiah gives the foundation's name: “Trust in the LORD forever, for in the LORD GOD you have an everlasting rock.” Advent is a time to check the foundations.

St Francis Xavier (1506–1552), Jesuit, one of the first companions of St Ignatius, arrived in Goa in 1542 and preached along the coasts of South India, among the Paravas of the Fishery Coast, in Travancore, then in Malacca, the Moluccas and Japan. He died on the island of Shangchuan, within sight of China. His body rests in the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Goa. He built on rock: “Lord, I am here; what do you want me to do?”

Full homily

Dear friends, Jesus ends the Sermon on the Mount with a warning: not everyone who says to me, Lord, Lord, will enter the kingdom, but the one who does the will of my Father (Matthew 7:21). Faith is not only words; it is a life built on God's will.

He tells of two houses. The rain falls, the floods rise, the winds blow; one house stands, the other falls with a great crash (Matthew 7:24–27). Both builders heard the same words. The difference was that one put them into practice. Isaiah sings of the same truth: trust in the Lord for ever, for the Lord is an everlasting rock (Isaiah 26:4).

Today we remember St Francis Xavier, who built his life on that rock. He heard the words of Jesus, what does it profit to gain the whole world, and acted on them. He left everything, came to our shores, and gave his life for the Gospel.

Storms come to every life: illness, failure, temptation, grief. What we build now decides whether we stand then. Build today: one word of the Gospel put into practice, one prayer, one act of love. Amen.

For children

Children, a house on rock does not fall in a storm. When you obey Jesus, you are building on rock. St Francis Xavier did that!

Prayer

Lord Jesus, everlasting Rock, let me not only call you “Lord” but do your will. Build my life on your word, and through the prayers of St Francis Xavier, make me a missionary wherever I am. Amen.
